# Poster and marketing copy (spec)

The strings agents may, must and may not use on posters and other marketing (social posts, promo images). Picked by the owner, one mod at a time. READMEs, player docs and Modrinth pages are out of scope: agents write those from the docs as usual.

How to use it:
- **Allowed** strings are used as written. Agents may shorten or combine them for space, but don't add new claims or a new tone.
- **Not allowed** strings, and anything close to them, are never used.
- Anything not in either list needs the owner's OK before it goes on a poster.

## All projects

- **No splash text** on posters (the yellow tilted Minecraft-title text). Not allowed anywhere.

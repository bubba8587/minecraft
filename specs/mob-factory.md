# Reworked Mobs: mob factories (spec, design: not built yet)

Owner's idea (2026-11-16), to make mob farming possible again after the spawn cut, creeper watchers and cairn endermen: an **underground factory**. A drippy, spiky growth on the ceiling, mixing **nether, warped and bone** elements (like dripstone made of bone blocks, warped wart, basalt), **spits out mobs** onto a platform below built in an **ancient city + redstone** style; mobs that aren't dealt with are **teleported away after some time**. Earlier the owner mentioned doing "something really awesome with huge underground areas with our double paired beacons": the ceiling growth and the platform are natural halves of a paired beacon.

## Independence

Lives in Reworked Mobs, built from vanilla blocks (B15). With Bubba's Reworked Beacons installed its two halves could also be a real paired beacon (to be designed); without it, the factory still works.

## Open questions (owner)

Builder's suggestions in brackets.

- **F1 Found or built?** [Found: a rare worldgen structure deep underground (deep dark depth, maybe near ancient cities); players repair and restart broken ones, can't build one from scratch.]
- **F2 On/off.** [Dormant until powered: a lever or redstone signal on the platform starts it, off stops it: a machine you claim.]
- **F3 What comes out.** [One mob type per factory, fixed at worldgen (zombie, skeleton, creeper: gunpowder's new home...). Vanilla-behaved, like spawner mobs.]
- **F4 The teleport away.** [A mob not killed within ~30 s of landing vanishes in portal particles, visibly (B19: shown, not hidden). No AFK pile-ups; rewards fighting.]
- **F5 Rate.** [About one mob per 5-10 s while running, ignoring the spawn cut.]
- **F6 Breaking it.** [The ceiling growth breaks like normal blocks: that shuts a factory down for good.]

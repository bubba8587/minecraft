# Camps 3: docs report (2026-11-23)

For merging into `specs/monster-camps.md` and the Reworked Mobs player page.

## Skull rock rework (spec)
- The noise ellipsoid is gone. The skull is now drawn from a hand-made 13x13 front grid (`SKULL_FACE` in CampPiece: # bone, E eye, N nose, M mouth), pushed 4 deep (5 at the brow), with an ellipsoid back half (radii 6.6 across, 6.2 deep, 7.0 high, centred 6.5 up). Hollow = every shape cell with shape all round it (a 1-block shell).
- Following the owner's "detail-lite" note: no teeth, fangs, cheekbones or bone trim. Bold features only: a big round cranium over a narrower jaw, a brow ledge one block out over the eyes, two 3x3 eye sockets 3 deep with a coal-block back (reads black at a distance), a nose hole the same way, and the mouth (5 wide, 2 high) as the doorway. 90% calcite, 10% bone block for texture.
- Floor inside: a mob skull.

## New forms (spec + player page)
- **Half-buried skull:** half of all skull rocks (picked from the skull's own seed bit, so how often skulls appear doesn't change). Sunk 4: only the cranium, the brow and the eyes show. The left eye (seen from the front) is the way in, with a calcite step up; the other eye stays dark.
- **Ribcage arch:** large camps only, 1 in 3. A bone spine 5 up, ribs on both sides every other block (4 ribs), curling in at the ground; a 3-wide path runs under it, along the edge of the clearing (7 from the centre). Footprint 9 x 7, in the plan like every other piece.
- Bridges to rock outcrops or skull tops: **not built** this round (time).

## Fences (owner request)
- `ClippedBuild.tidyFences(pieceBox)` runs after a camp is built in each chunk: removes lone fences (no side joined, nothing on top, no fence below), sets every fence's sides from its real neighbours, and also fixes fences just over the chunk edge that an earlier chunk built (vanilla lets worldgen write one chunk out).
- New test `campFencesEndCleanly` (all 6 kinds, large camps) and every walkability test now also checks fences: every side matches its neighbour and no lone rails.

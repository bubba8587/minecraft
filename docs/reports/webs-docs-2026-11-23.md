# Spun web hitboxes (BACKLOG 21): docs report, 2026-11-23

## Player-facing
- You can now hit, mine and use blocks and mobs through the gaps between a spun web's strands, whether you stand inside the web or outside it.
- Swings, shears, arrows and other projectiles only hit a web when they pass within about a third of a block of a strand. Shears cut, and fire arrows burn, the web you actually aim at.

## Spec facts (specs/spiders.md)
- The `SpunWeb` entity box still spans all its strands, but only for culling and entity searches (the slow, `getEntities` lookups). Hits never use the box.
- Hit test: `SpunWeb.strandClip(from, to)` finds the closest points between the ray segment and each strand segment. It counts as a hit within `SpiderTuning.WEB_PICK_RADIUS` (0.3), and the hit point is where the ray enters that radius.
- `mixin/ProjectileUtilWebMixin` hooks it into vanilla's three entity picks in `ProjectileUtil`:
  - the view pick (`getEntityHitResult(Entity, ...)`), which the client crosshair uses in `LocalPlayer.pick` (melee, shears, use);
  - the move pick (`getEntityHitResult(Level, ...)`), used by arrows, throwables, fireballs and rockets;
  - `getManyEntityHitResult`, used by attack-range weapons.
- For a web, the box clip is replaced by the strand clip, and both the "pick from inside the box" check and the margin hit are turned off.
- Why not multipart: 26.3's parts are specific to the ender dragon (`EnderDragonPart`, the level's dragon-part list), not a general API, and up to 16 strand parts per web would add entities.
- Known cosmetic issue: F3+B debug hitboxes still draw the whole span box.

## Milestone test objectives
- `GiantSpiderGameTest.webHitsOnlyTheStrands` (also in core as `spunWebHitsOnlyTheStrands`):
  - a crosshair pick through a gap, from inside or outside the span, hits nothing;
  - aiming at a strand hits the web at the strand;
  - a pig behind a gap gets hit;
  - an arrow through a gap misses the web, and one along a strand's line hits it.

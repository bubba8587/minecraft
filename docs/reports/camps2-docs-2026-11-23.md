# Camps 2: layout variety (skull rocks, winding towers, ramps, bridges), 2026-11-23

For the docs merge into `specs/monster-camps.md` ("Layout variety") and the player vault's camps page. Governs: C1.

## Research: what we take from other games' camps

- **Zelda: Breath of the Wild / Tears of the Kingdom (bokoblin camps):** monster strongholds are wooden platforms built round trees and **big skull-shaped rocks** (the skull's mouth is the doorway, the eyes are look-outs), plus stilted wooden lookouts with a ladder, and the occasional rickety bridge. Taken: the skull rock as a rare centrepiece you walk into by the mouth, with eye sockets; stilt towers; plank bridges between platforms.
- **Valheim (fuling villages):** a large loose village on open plains: wooden stake walls, several watchtowers, the camp spread out rather than packed. Taken: large camps get more and taller structures, linked rather than a single cluster.
- **Skyrim (bandit camps, giant camps):** bandit camps use tents, lookouts on high ground and ramps of rough planks up rock faces; giant camps are a fire ring with huge bones and mammoth skulls. Taken: plank ramps up to platforms (mobs can walk them, unlike ladders); bones and skulls as set dressing round the skull rock.
- **Elden Ring (camps and forts):** fortified camps on several levels: watchtowers with stairs winding round the outside, archers up high, connected platforms. Taken: the rare multi-story tower with an external winding stair and an archer spot at the top.
- **Far Cry (outposts):** an outpost reads as a puzzle from a distance: towers with sight lines, an alarm, several ways in. Taken: height variety (towers 5 to 14 high) and multiple routes up (ladders, ramps, bridges) so a camp offers a plan from afar.

## What was built (all through the footprint plan)

- **Skull rock** (large camps only, 1 in 3): a hollow dome about 11 across and 8 high, at 10 blocks from the fire, facing it. Round at the back, flatter face at the front. Mostly calcite with bone-block and diorite patches, the kind's rock along the base, a bone brow, bone teeth either side of the mouth, pointed-dripstone fangs. Eye sockets (2 x 2 each) and a nose hole; the 1-wide, 3-tall mouth is the way in. Inside: trodden floor (full blocks), dark, bones and a skull. Its inside floor cells are shade spots. Footprint 13 x 13 (`skull`).
- **Winding tower** (large camps only, 1 in 4): a 5 x 5 log-and-plank core, 12 to 14 high, a 1-wide wooden stair winding clockwise round the outside (corner landings on log posts, some upside-down stairs under the treads), log bands at each floor (every 5 blocks), fence window slits, doorways off the stair onto any floor it passes at the right height, a railed top (open where the stair arrives) with a slab roof on fence posts. Archer spot at the top; the top's 3 x 3 are shade spots. Footprint 7 x 7 (`winding tower`). Built as a vanilla-blocks structure in code (B15: no new blocks).
- **Ramps** (any tower, 35%): a 2-wide wooden-stair ramp from the ground to the platform on a side whose foot stays in the clearing, log stilts under every other step, the platform railing open where it arrives. Footprint `ramp` (may touch its own tower).
- **Bridges** (medium and large camps, 40% per tower): a second tower the same height placed 4 to 7 blocks off one side of the first, a 2-wide plank bridge between the platforms, its middle half sagging to slabs, iron-chain "rope" railings between fence end posts, railings open at both ends. Both towers and the bridge claim their cells; if the bridge's cells don't fit, the twin tower is dropped too, so bridges only ever join towers that exist. Ladders never come down the bridge side.
- Kinds keep their themes: ramps, stairs and bridges use the kind's wood (`CampKind.woodStairs()` added: spruce, dark oak, acacia, mangrove), skull rocks use the kind's rock along the base and kind's skull inside.
- Bounding box: out to radius + 5 (was + 3) and 22 up (was 18) to hold the skull rock and the tall tower.
- Plan order: fire, stairs tunnel, skull rock, winding tower, lean-tos, towers (+ ramp, + twin and bridge), outcrops, pikes, banners. Existing seeds lay out differently from before.

# Camps 4: bridge rails and skull rock materials (docs report, 2026-11-24)

For `specs/` (camps spec) and the player page on monster camps.

## Player-facing changes
- Tower bridges: the chain rope railing no longer hangs in the air. Each side of a bridge has a log beam along it,
  log posts at both ends (and every 4 blocks on long bridges), and horizontal chains strung between the posts.
- Skull rocks (full and half-buried): built mostly of bone block, some calcite, a few smooth quartz / quartz blocks,
  and a quartz-pillar brow ridge. The eye sockets and nose are plain holes into the hollow inside (the coal-block
  backing is gone).

## Spec facts
- Bridge railing (CampPiece.bridge): cells w=-1 and w=2 beside the 2-wide deck; log (axis along the bridge) at deck
  height, at deck+1 a log post at k=1, k=n and every k%4==1 with >=3 cells left, else iron chain with axis along the bridge.
  Railing no longer uses fences, so it can't leave lone fence posts.
- Skull materials (CampPiece.skullBone): brow (dy 9, w 5) quartz pillar, axis across the face; otherwise 70% bone block,
  15% calcite, 10% smooth quartz, 5% quartz block. Base ring and the buried skull's step are bone block. skullCell has no
  "dark socket" value any more.
- ClippedBuild.tidyFences: the neighbour-chunk ring pass now also removes a ring fence left joining nothing (air above,
  no fence below) and unjoins the in-chunk fence beside it. This fixed a position-dependent flake where
  bridge/ramp tests occasionally reported two lone fences at a chunk edge.
- Test: campFencesEndCleanly (fenceFaults) now also checks chains: each end along a chain's axis (top only for vertical)
  must be a chain on the same axis, a fence, a wall, or a sturdy face.

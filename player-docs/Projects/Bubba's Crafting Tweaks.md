# Bubba's Crafting Tweaks

A handful of extra recipes that make awkward or grindy items reachable: renewable sand, quartz and glowstone, a way to make tridents, uses for rotten flesh and spare charcoal.

## Recipes

**Crafting table**
- **Bamboo Paper:** 3 bamboo in a row → 2 Bamboo Paper. Works anywhere paper does; it's a light green with the optional *Bubba's Crafting Tweaks* resource pack, plain paper-colored without it.
- **Black dye:** 1 coal or charcoal → 1 black dye.
- **Charcoal Block:** 9 charcoal → 1 block of coal.
- **Trident:** 3 prismarine shards on top; prismarine crystals down both sides; sticks down the middle.

**Blast furnace**
- Gravel or glass → sand.
- Cobblestone → gravel.
- Andesite, diorite or granite → quartz.
- Stone → iron nugget.
- Block of coal → **Synthetic Diamond Ore** (deepslate diamond ore; very slow to cook). With the datapack alone it mines like normal diamond ore; with the companion mod it's much stingier (see below).

**Smoker**
- Glow berries, glow lichen or a glow ink sac → **Organic Glowstone Dust**.
- Rotten flesh → leather ("Cured zombie flesh. Gross...").

## How to use

Add it to a world like any datapack (see [[Installing Datapacks]]). New recipes show up in the recipe book once you've picked up an ingredient. Works on servers too: only the server needs it.

## With the companion mod

*Bubba's Crafting Tweaks* also comes as a Fabric mod. The mod already contains the recipes and the resource pack, so you install just the one file (and Fabric API). It must be installed on **both the server and every player's game**: a player without it is told to install it when they join.

It adds:

**The Glider.** A craftable, weaker cousin of the elytra.
- Recipe (crafting table): diamond, bamboo, diamond on top; phantom membrane on both sides of the middle row; feather on both sides of the bottom row. Makes one Glider.
- Wear it in the chest slot and jump while falling to glide, just like an elytra.
- It never breaks or wears out: no durability, no repairs.
- It turns about half as fast toward where you look, and firework rockets give it a weaker boost (it tops out at a little over half an elytra's rocket speed). Gliding down and lift work the same as an elytra.
- Its look (item and wings) is a placeholder for now.

**Synthetic Diamond Ore that feels synthetic.** Ore placed from the blast-furnace Synthetic Diamond Ore:
- Has about a 1 in 4 chance of dropping a diamond when mined, and often drops nothing. Fortune doesn't help, and it gives no experience.
- Silk Touch gives you the Synthetic Diamond Ore back, so you can move it.
- Ore you find in the world is unchanged: same diamonds, Fortune and experience as always.
- If the mod is ever removed from a world, placed Synthetic Diamond Ore turns back into ordinary diamond ore (and drops real diamonds).

## Works with

Plain vanilla, and any other recipe mods or datapacks. It doesn't replace any vanilla recipe.

# Crafting Tweaks Recipes

Part of [[Bubba's Crafting Tweaks]].

## Crafting table

- **Bamboo Paper:** 3 bamboo in a row → 2 Bamboo Paper. Works anywhere paper does; it's a light green with the optional *Bubba's Crafting Tweaks* resource pack, plain paper-colored without it.
- **Black dye:** 1 coal or charcoal → 1 black dye.
- **Charcoal Block:** 9 charcoal → 1 block of coal.
- **Trident:** 3 prismarine shards on top; prismarine crystals down both sides; sticks down the middle.

## Blast furnace

- Gravel or glass → sand.
- Cobblestone → gravel.
- Andesite, diorite or granite → quartz.
- Stone → iron nugget.
- Block of coal → **Synthetic Diamond Ore** (deepslate diamond ore; very slow to cook). With the datapack alone it mines like normal diamond ore; with the companion mod it's much stingier: see [[Synthetic Diamond Ore]].

## Smoker

- Glow berries, glow lichen or a glow ink sac → **Organic Glowstone Dust**.
- Rotten flesh → leather ("Cured zombie flesh. Gross...").

# Synthetic Diamond Ore

Part of [[Bubba's Crafting Tweaks]].

Made in a blast furnace from a block of coal (see [[Crafting Tweaks Recipes]]).

**With the datapack alone,** it places and mines like normal diamond ore.

**With the [[Crafting Tweaks Companion Mod]],** ore placed from it feels synthetic:
- About a 1 in 4 chance of dropping a diamond when mined, and often nothing. Fortune doesn't help, and it gives no experience.
- Silk Touch gives you the Synthetic Diamond Ore back, so you can move it.
- Ore you find in the world is unchanged: same diamonds, Fortune and experience as always.
- If the mod is ever removed from a world, placed Synthetic Diamond Ore turns back into ordinary diamond ore (and drops real diamonds).

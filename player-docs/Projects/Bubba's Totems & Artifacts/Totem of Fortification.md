The **Totem of Fortification** is an artifact that stops explosions from breaking blocks.

## Obtaining

The Totem of Fortification cannot be crafted. It is found in desert pyramid, bastion and stronghold crossing chests, and in [[Relic Vaults|relic vault]] chests. It is also found in trial chamber vaults: a 2 % chance each time a player opens a normal vault, and 4 % for an ominous vault.

A [[Relic Vaults|relic vault's]] pedestal may also hold a Totem of Fortification [[Totem Statue|statue]]; breaking the statue gives the totem.

## Usage

| Where | Protected blocks |
|---|---|
| Held in either hand | Within 8 blocks of the holder |
| In a [[Bubba's Reworked Beacons]] beacon | The beacon's whole area |
| As a statue | Within 5 blocks of the statue |

Explosions break no protected blocks and start no fires on them. Everything else about an explosion stays the same:

- It still hurts and knocks back players and mobs.
- Blocks outside the protected area still break.
- Item frames, paintings and armor stands can still be destroyed.

When the totem saves blocks, a dull clang sounds and a few glints show at the blast. The `mobGriefing` game rule is never changed, so villagers still farm. The beacon screen reads "Explosions break no blocks".

## Statue

Sneaking and using the totem on a block sets it down as a [[Totem Statue]] instead of using it. Explosions break no blocks within 5 blocks of the statue. Mining the statue gives the totem back.

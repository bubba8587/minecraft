# Totem of Fortification

The **Totem of Fortification** is an artifact that stops explosions from breaking blocks.

## Obtaining

Found in chests in desert pyramids, bastions and stronghold crossings.

## Usage

Held, explosions within 8 blocks of its holder break no blocks, though they still hurt. In a reworked beacon, no explosion breaks blocks anywhere in the beacon's range. Item frames and paintings can still be destroyed.

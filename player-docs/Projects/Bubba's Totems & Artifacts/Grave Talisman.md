The **Grave Talisman** is a small stone talisman whose gem glows when undead are near, brighter the closer they are. It only glows; it has no other effect. This item is an early test, and its look and name may change.

## Obtaining

The Grave Talisman is found, not crafted:

- With [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] installed, the chest in a [[Crypt Yards|crypt yard's]] family vault holds one about 15 % of the time, and a noble crypt's chest about 25 % of the time.
- [[Relic Vaults|Relic vault]] chests sometimes hold one.

It is also in the creative inventory, under the **Bubba's Totems & Artifacts** tab.

## Usage

The talisman glows while it is in either hand, in the hotbar or anywhere in the player's inventory. It senses every undead mob, such as zombies, skeletons, husks and drowned. With [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] installed it also counts that mod's [[Sleeping Undead|sleeping undead]].

| Nearest undead | Glow |
|---|---|
| Within 12 blocks | Brightest |
| Within 24 blocks | Bright |
| Within 40 blocks | Faint |
| None within 40 blocks | Off |

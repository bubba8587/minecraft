The **Grave Talisman** is a small stone talisman whose gem glows when undead are near, brighter the closer they are.

- It only glows; it has no other effect.
- This item is an early test, and its look and name may change.

## Obtaining

- Found, not crafted.
- With [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] installed: in the chest of a [[Crypt Yards|crypt yard's]] family vault about 15 % of the time, and in a noble crypt's chest about 25 % of the time.
- Sometimes in [[Relic Vaults|relic vault]] chests.
- In the creative inventory, under the **Bubba's Totems & Artifacts** tab.

## Usage

- Glows while in either hand, in the hotbar or anywhere in the player's inventory.
- Senses every undead mob, such as zombies, skeletons, husks and drowned.
- With [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] installed, also counts that mod's [[Sleeping Undead|sleeping undead]].

| Nearest undead | Glow |
|---|---|
| Within 12 blocks | Brightest |
| Within 24 blocks | Bright |
| Within 40 blocks | Faint |
| None within 40 blocks | Off |

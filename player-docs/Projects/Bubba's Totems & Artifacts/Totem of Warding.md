The **Totem of Warding** is a found item that keeps monsters away. Held, it pulses and makes nearby monsters run from the holder; placed in a beacon from [[Bubba's Reworked Beacons]], it wards the beacon's whole area against monsters. It is not a gem and cannot be set into armor or weapons. Its texture is a placeholder in this early test version.

## Obtaining

The Totem of Warding cannot be crafted, and evokers do not drop it.

With [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] installed, most totems of warding come from [[Crypt Yards|crypts]], which often have a Totem of Warding [[Totem Statue|statue]] standing guard. Breaking the statue gives the totem; left standing, it keeps the sleeping undead near it from waking.

| Crypt | Warding statues |
|---|---|
| Noble crypt | Up to 3: one each in the chamber and the two side halls, 85% each |
| Family vault | 65% |
| Pauper's ossuary | 35% |

Chests and vaults add a totem on top of their normal loot:

| Source | Chance |
|---|---|
| Woodland mansion chest | 20% per chest |
| Ancient city chest | 12% per chest |
| Trial chambers vault | 3% per opening |
| Ominous trial chambers vault | 6% per opening |
| Noble crypt chest (with [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]]) | 12% per chest |

[[Relic Vaults|Relic vault]] chests sometimes hold one too, and a relic vault's pedestal may hold a Totem of Warding statue.

## Usage

### Held

In either hand, the totem pulses every 10 seconds. The pulse shows as the normal item cooldown sweep over its hotbar slot (as after throwing an ender pearl), a ring of soul fire spreading out at shin height, rising souls and a low chime. The first pulse comes as soon as the totem is held, unless it is still on cooldown.

At each pulse:

- Every monster within 8 blocks runs straight away from the holder for about 5 seconds, the way creepers run from cats, then goes back to what it was doing. Running monsters do not attack. Piglins and hoglins flee in their own way.
- With [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] installed, [[Sleeping Undead|sleeping undead]] within 8 blocks settle a little. *See also: [[Noise and Waking]]*

Slimes, ghasts, phantoms, vexes, breezes and the warden are not scared off. A held totem does not stop monsters spawning. Mobs that pick up a Totem of Warding and hold it pulse in the same way.

### In a beacon

*See also: [[Beacon]]*

With [[Bubba's Reworked Beacons]] installed, a Totem of Warding placed in a beacon wards the beacon's area (a sky beacon's column, or the box of a pair of beacons underground). The beacon screen lists what the ward does.

| Pyramid | Effect in the warded area |
|---|---|
| Any (1 to 4 layers) | No monsters spawn naturally, phantoms included. No monster gets in: a monster chasing a player stops at the edge, and monsters already inside leave. |
| 4 layers | Also, with [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] installed: sleeping undead inside never wake, whatever the noise. The beacon screen shows this line only when that mod is installed. |

The edge of a ward works like an invisible wall for every monster, including those that do not walk in on foot: phantoms, ghasts, vexes, slimes, magma cubes, blazes, endermen teleporting and monsters knocked toward it all stop at the edge and can slide along it. The wall does no damage. Any monster already inside, such as one there when the ward went up or one from a spawner, walks out through the nearest side. One shut in with no way out, such as in a walled room under a beacon, gives up trying to leave and fights as normal, checking for a way out every 10 seconds.

Happy ghasts are kept out too, whether wild or ridden. A happy ghast that a player steers into the ward is pushed back outside the edge; the rider can get off and walk in. On a slow connection the rider may see the ghast jump back slightly at the edge.

Spawners, trial spawners, raids, bosses and commands still spawn monsters as normal, but those monsters do not stay in the ward either. Monsters outside can still shoot in. The ward ends about 5 seconds after the beacon stops working.

With [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] installed, a ward over an Ancient Fabricator stops it dropping new monsters, but leaves the limp monsters it already made alone, and its teleporter still sends them up.

### As a statue

*Main article: [[Totem Statue]]*

Sneaking and using the totem on a block sets it down as a statue, even while its pulse cooldown is running. Within 5 blocks of it, monsters keep out and sleeping undead stay asleep. With [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]], crypts often have one standing among their sleepers. Mining the statue gives the totem back.

## Compatibility

Without [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]], the totem works the same except that there are no sleeping undead for it to calm or keep asleep.

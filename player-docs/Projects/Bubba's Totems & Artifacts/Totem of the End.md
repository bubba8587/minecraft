The **Totem of the End** is a reusable artifact that makes endermen treat its holder as one of their own and teleports its user to the block they aim at.

## Obtaining

The Totem of the End cannot be crafted. It is found:

- In End city treasure chests (5 % per chest).
- Rarely, in the chests of any [[Relic Vaults|relic vault]].
- As a statue on the pedestal of some grand relic vaults; breaking the statue gives the totem.

## Usage

### Held

While a player holds the totem in either hand, endermen treat them as one of their own: looking an enderman in the eye does not anger it. This applies to every enderman, in every dimension. With [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]], breaking a cairn or fighting near one also does not turn the cairn's endermen against the holder. An enderman that is hit still fights back.

### Teleporting

Using the totem teleports the player to the block they are aiming at, up to 32 blocks away. The totem is not used up; a 4-second cooldown follows each teleport. Aiming at nothing, or at a place with nowhere safe to land, does nothing and starts no cooldown.

| Aimed at | Landing |
|---|---|
| The top of a block | On that block. |
| The side of a block | On top of that block if there is room; otherwise straight down from in front of it, onto the nearest safe ground below. |
| The underside of a block | Straight down from under it, onto the nearest safe ground below. |

A landing spot is safe when the player fits there and it has no lava, fire, campfire, powder snow, magma block, cactus, sweet berry bush or wither rose or water in or under it. The void is never a landing spot. The aim passes through grass, flowers and water, but it never lands you in water.

The player is taken off any mount first, keeps facing the same way and takes no fall damage from the jump. The teleport doesn't hurt. An enderman's teleport sound and portal particles mark both ends.

## Statue

Sneaking and using the totem on a block sets it down as a [[Totem Statue]] instead of using it. Mining the statue gives the totem back.

## Textures

An end stone figure with a purpur crown, an enderman's dark face and magenta eyes, and an ender pearl on its chest. Placeholder art in this early version.

## Water

While you hold the totem, water hurts you like it hurts an enderman: about a heart a second while you're in it. Each time it hurts, the totem tries to teleport you to dry ground within about 32 blocks. Far out at sea there may be nowhere to go, so put it away before you swim. Rain doesn't count.

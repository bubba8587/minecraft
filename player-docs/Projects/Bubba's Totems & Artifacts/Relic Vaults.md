# Relic Vaults

*Part of [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]].*

> [!infobox] Relic vault
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Type** | Structure (underground) |
> | **Location** | Overworld, about 12 blocks under the ground, never under the sea; one per relic name and size |
> | **Found with** | A finished [[Relic Maps|relic map]] |
> | **Sizes** | Small (four-piece map), grand (nine-piece map); smaller unnamed vaults behind some noble crypts |

A **relic vault** is a sealed treasure room buried deep under the world, belonging to a named relic whose [[Relic Maps|relic map]] leads to it.

## Generation

- Every world has exactly one vault for each relic name and size: 64 small vaults for the four-piece maps and 64 grand vaults for the nine-piece maps.
- Their places are fixed from the world seed.
- Small vaults lie 400 to 2,400 blocks from the world's centre, grand vaults 1,600 to 4,800 blocks, and no two lie close together.
- A vault's floor is about 12 blocks under the ground, as deep as one dug off a crypt; vaults are never under the sea.
- There is no way in from the surface: an old stone stair climbs seven steps from the vault's door and stops in solid rock.
- The way in is to dig down, following the map's X, which marks the door.

### Crypt vaults

- With [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] installed, about a third of noble [[Crypt Yards|crypts]] hide a smaller vault.
- Its stair starts just behind the crypt's far wall, between the two family chests, so it is found only by breaking through the wall.
- Crypt vaults have no name and no map leads to them.

## Structure

- The room lies behind an iron door with a lever inside; nothing outside opens it.
- Deepslate tile walls with polished deepslate pillars, a checkered floor of smooth quartz and polished diorite, sea lanterns set in the ceiling and two pearlescent froglights beside a chiseled quartz pedestal.
- A red carpet runs from the door to the pedestal.
- Chests stand stacked two high, armour stands wear sets of armour, and tables have chairs with cushions.

| | Small | Grand | Crypt vault |
|---|---|---|---|
| Room | 9 x 9 | 13 x 11 | 7 x 7 |
| Chests | 3 | 6 | 2 |
| Armour stands | 2 (iron or gold) | 4 (iron, diamond or gold) | 1 (chainmail) |
| Tables | 1 | 2 | 1 |
| Guard (with Bubba's Reworked Mobs) | Skeleton in iron | Skeleton in diamond | None |

## The pedestal

- Each vault's pedestal holds its prize, about half the time each:
  - A **[[Totem Statue|totem statue]]** facing the door. Breaking it gives the totem; left standing, it works as a statue. Grand vaults can hold any totem's statue, including the [[Totem of the Beginning]] and the [[Totem of the End]]; small and crypt vaults any but those two.
  - A **fine weapon on display**: in a grand vault a heavily enchanted netherite sword or axe, otherwise an enchanted diamond sword or axe.
- With [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] the weapon is planted point-down in the pedestal; without it, it hangs in a glow item frame on the pedestal's front.
- With [[Bubba's Infused Gems]] the weapon carries gems: two on a grand vault's weapon, one otherwise, mostly ones that harm the target.
- With Bubba's Reworked Mobs, a skeleton in armour sits beside the pedestal of a named vault: a [[Sleeping Undead|sleeping undead]] that wakes like any other.
- With Bubba's Infused Gems its chestplate and sword carry gems.

## Loot

- Each chest holds treasure, more [[Relic Maps|relic map shreds]] of other relics, and often a totem.
- Grand vaults are much richer than small ones.

| | Grand | Small | Crypt vault |
|---|---|---|---|
| Treasure | Many rolls: diamonds (1-4), strongly enchanted books and gear | Fewer: diamonds (1-2), enchanted books and gear | A few rolls |
| Relic map shreds | 2-4 | 1-3 | 1-2 |
| Totem chance | 45 % | 30 % | 20 % |
| Rare find chance | 40 % | 15 % | None |

- Rare finds are an enchanted golden apple, netherite scrap, a Totem of Undying, the Pigstep disc or a heart of the sea.
- With Bubba's Infused Gems, chests also hold rare gems.
- Once a vault has been emptied, other maps of the same name and size lead to the same, now empty, room.

## Commands

- Operators can build a vault with `/bubbas_totems_artifacts relic_vault <small|grand|crypt>`: its door is at the player's feet and the room runs south.
- Vaults are not found by `/locate`.

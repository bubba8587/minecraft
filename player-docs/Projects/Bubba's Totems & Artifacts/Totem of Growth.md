The **Totem of Growth** is an artifact that makes plants grow faster and plants dropped seeds.

- Its name may change.

## Obtaining

- Cannot be crafted.
- Found in jungle temple, igloo, shipwreck supply and [[Relic Vaults|relic vault]] chests.
- Trial chamber vaults: 0.7 % chance each time a player opens a normal vault, 1.4 % for an ominous vault.
- A [[Relic Vaults|relic vault's]] pedestal may hold a Totem of Growth [[Totem Statue|statue]]; breaking the statue gives the totem.

## Usage

### Faster growth

- Crops, saplings, sugar cane, cactus, bamboo, cocoa, nether wart, kelp, vines, berry bushes and chorus flowers grow faster near the totem.
- They grow by the normal rules, only more often, so they still need light, farmland, room for a tree and so on.
- A plant that grows thanks to the totem gives off a small sparkle.

| Where | Range | Speed |
|---|---|---|
| Held in either hand | 8 blocks in every direction | About 3 times as fast |
| In a [[Bubba's Reworked Beacons]] beacon | The beacon's area | 2 times as fast with 1 pyramid layer, up to 5 times with 4 |
| As a statue | 5 blocks | 2 times as fast |

- The beacon screen shows how much faster plants grow.

### Planting

- Held, in a beacon or as a statue, the totem also plants things.
- Every couple of seconds it may pick one dropped seed, crop, sapling or similar item in its range and plant it close to where it lies, wherever that plant can grow, always inside the totem's range.
- It only plants where the plant has room to grow: not against walls, tree trunks or other plants. Crops can still go in rows.
- It never plants on top of another plant, so sugar cane, bamboo and cactus do not get stacked into towers.
- It never harvests anything.
- Items circling a [[Totem of Gathering]] ring can be planted too.

## Statue

- Sneaking and using the totem on a block sets it down as a [[Totem Statue]] instead of using it.
- Within 5 blocks of the statue, plants grow twice as fast and dropped seeds get planted.
- Mining the statue gives the totem back.

A **relic map shred** is a torn piece of an old map. Shreds of the same relic put together make a **relic map**, a real explorer map that leads to that relic's [[Relic Vaults|relic vault]].

## Obtaining

Relic map shreds turn up in many chests: dungeons, mineshafts, shipwrecks, buried treasure, desert and jungle temples, igloos, strongholds, woodland mansions, pillager outposts, ancient cities, ruined portals, ocean ruins, villages (cartographers most often), trial chamber corridors and bastions. With [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] installed they are also in [[Crypt Yards|crypt]] chests, [[Monster Camps|monster camp]] chests, [[Ancient Fabricators|Fabricator]] offices, and the caretaker endermen's [[Trading at Cairns|cairn trades]]. Relic vaults hold more shreds of other relics.

A found shred is usually a single piece, sometimes two or three, never a whole map. About a third of shreds belong to nine-piece maps.

## Names

Every shred names its relic, such as **"Wondrous Crab Relic Map (1/9)"** or **"Egregious Zealot Relic Map (2/4)"**. A name is one of eight words (Wondrous, Egregious, Forgotten, Gilded, Sunken, Whispering, Crooked, Radiant) and one of eight things (Crab, Zealot, Lantern, Serpent, Crown, Owl, Anvil, Bell), so there are 64 names. Each name comes as a four-piece map and a nine-piece map, which lead to different places: 128 maps in every world.

The tooltip reads like a found scrap: "A torn scrap of an old map, the ink faded but the crest still clear.", the pieces it holds ("2 of 4 pieces") and how many the whole map needs.

## Usage

Shreds of the **same name and the same size** placed together in any crafting grid (the 2x2 grid in the inventory, a crafting table or a crafter) combine into one shred whose pieces add up: 2/9 + 2/9 + 5/9 makes 9/9. Shreds of different names, or a four-piece and a nine-piece shred, do not combine, and the pieces cannot go over the whole.

A **whole** map turns into a real filled map the moment it is in the player's inventory (a whole map made in a crafter turns once it is picked up). The map is named after its relic, such as "Wondrous Crab Relic Map", and like an explorer map it shows the land around the vault with a red X on the vault's door.

Every world has exactly one vault per name and size, fixed from the world seed when the world is made. Once the Wondrous Crab vault has been found and emptied, other Wondrous Crab maps of the same size lead to an empty room. Four-piece maps lead 400 to 2,400 blocks from the world's centre; nine-piece maps 1,600 to 4,800 blocks.

Relic vaults are not found by `/locate` or the [[Totem of Seeking]]; the maps are the way to them.

## Commands

Operators can make shreds and maps:

- `/bubbas_totems_artifacts relic_map <name> <4|9>` gives the finished map for that relic.
- `/bubbas_totems_artifacts relic_shred <name> <4|9> <count>` gives a shred with that many pieces.

Names are written in lower case with an underscore, such as `wondrous_crab`.

## Textures

A four-piece shred is a big torn corner of parchment; a nine-piece shred is a small, yellower scrap torn all round; a whole map shows tear lines and a red X. Placeholder art in this early version.

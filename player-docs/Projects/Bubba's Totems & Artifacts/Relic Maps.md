*Part of [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]].*

A **relic map shred** is a torn piece of an old map; shreds of the same relic put together make a **relic map**, a real explorer map that leads to that relic's [[Relic Vaults|relic vault]].

## Obtaining

- Found in many chests: dungeons, mineshafts, shipwrecks, buried treasure, desert and jungle temples, igloos, strongholds, woodland mansions, pillager outposts, ancient cities, ruined portals, ocean ruins, villages (cartographers most often), trial chamber corridors and bastions.
- With [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] installed, also in [[Crypt Yards|crypt]] chests, [[Monster Camps|monster camp]] chests and [[Ancient Fabricators|Fabricator]] offices.
- With [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] installed, each answer the caretaker endermen give at a [[Trading at Cairns|cairn trade pad]] has a 10 % chance to come with a shred as well.
- Relic vaults hold more shreds of other relics.

| Found in | Chance per chest |
|---|---|
| Shipwreck map chest, buried treasure, stronghold library | 40 % |
| Dungeon, desert and jungle temple, igloo, woodland mansion, large ocean ruin, shipwreck treasure, village cartographer | 30 % |
| Pillager outpost, ancient city, stronghold corridor and crossing | 25 % |
| Mineshaft | 20 % |
| Ruined portal, small ocean ruin, shipwreck supplies, village temple | 15 % |
| Village weaponsmith and toolsmith, trial chamber corridor, bastion | 10 % |
| Village house | 8 % |
| With Bubba's Reworked Mobs: noble / family / pauper crypt | 50 % / 35 % / 15 % |
| With Bubba's Reworked Mobs: monster camp, Fabricator office | 30 %, 25 % |

- A found shred is usually a single piece, sometimes two or three, never a whole map.
- About a third of shreds belong to nine-piece maps.

### Cartographers

- Cartographer villagers sell relic maps among their usual trades:

| Level | Trade | Price | How often |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apprentice | One relic map shred (four- or nine-piece, sometimes two or three pieces) | 4-6 emeralds | About 1 apprentice cartographer in 5 |
| Master | A finished four-piece relic map | 20-24 emeralds and a compass | About 1 master cartographer in 8 |

- A shred bought from a cartographer shows no relic name in the trade window ("Relic Map Shred (1/4)").
- It picks its relic when it reaches the player's inventory, and every piece bought picks its own, so buying many shreds from one cartographer does not finish a map by itself.
- A finished map from a master cartographer leads to one relic's vault like any other map of that name.
- The same cartographer sells the same map again after restocking.

## Names

- Every shred names its relic, such as **"Wondrous Crab Relic Map (1/9)"** or **"Egregious Zealot Relic Map (2/4)"**.
- A name is one of eight words (Wondrous, Egregious, Forgotten, Gilded, Sunken, Whispering, Crooked, Radiant) and one of eight things (Crab, Zealot, Lantern, Serpent, Crown, Owl, Anvil, Bell): 64 names.
- Each name comes as a four-piece map and a nine-piece map, which lead to different places: 128 maps in every world.
- The tooltip shows only the pieces it holds ("2 of 4 pieces").

## Usage

- Shreds of the **same name and the same size** placed together in any crafting grid (the 2x2 grid in the inventory, a crafting table or a crafter) combine into one shred whose pieces add up: 2/9 + 2/9 + 5/9 makes 9/9.
- Shreds of different names, or a four-piece and a nine-piece shred, do not combine, and the pieces cannot go over the whole.
- A **whole** map turns into a real filled map the moment it is in the player's inventory (a whole map made in a crafter turns once it is picked up).
- The map is named after its relic, such as "Wondrous Crab Relic Map", and like an explorer map it shows the land around the vault with a red X on the vault's door.
- Every world has exactly one vault per name and size, fixed from the world seed when the world is made.
- Once the Wondrous Crab vault has been found and emptied, other Wondrous Crab maps of the same size lead to an empty room.
- Four-piece maps lead 400 to 2,400 blocks from the world's centre; nine-piece maps 1,600 to 4,800 blocks.
- Relic vaults are not found by `/locate` or the [[Totem of Seeking]]; the maps are the way to them.

## Commands

- Operators can make shreds and maps:
  - `/bubbas_totems_artifacts relic_map <name> <4|9>` gives the finished map for that relic.
  - `/bubbas_totems_artifacts relic_shred <name> <4|9> <count>` gives a shred with that many pieces.
- Names are written in lower case with an underscore, such as `wondrous_crab`.

## Textures

- A four-piece shred is a big torn corner of parchment.
- A nine-piece shred is a small, yellower scrap torn all round.
- A whole map shows tear lines and a red X.
- Placeholder art in this early version.

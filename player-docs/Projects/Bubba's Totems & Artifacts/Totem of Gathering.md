The **Totem of Gathering** is an artifact that pulls in dropped items.

## Obtaining

The Totem of Gathering cannot be crafted. It is found in mineshaft, dungeon and pillager outpost chests, and in [[Relic Vaults|relic vault]] chests. It is also found in trial chamber vaults: a 0.7 % chance each time a player opens a normal vault, and 1.4 % for an ominous vault.

A [[Relic Vaults|relic vault's]] pedestal may also hold a Totem of Gathering [[Totem Statue|statue]]; breaking the statue gives the totem.

## Usage

### Held

Held in either hand, the totem pulls dropped items within 8 blocks to its holder, who then picks them up as usual.

- Items the holder has just thrown are not pulled back, so dropping items still works.
- Items thrown by someone else are pulled.
- Experience orbs already fly to nearby players on their own, so the totem leaves them alone.

### In a beacon

In a [[Bubba's Reworked Beacons]] beacon, dropped items and experience anywhere in the beacon's area fly to the beacon and circle it in a slowly turning ring just above its top.

- A hopper placed against any side of the beacon, leading into a chest, catches the items as they come round.
- Items in the ring never despawn.
- Experience stays in the ring until a player comes close to the beacon, then flies to them.
- The ring holds up to 48 things. When the beacon stops working, the ring falls apart after a few seconds and everything drops.

The beacon screen reads "Dropped items and experience circle the beacon".

## Statue

*Main article: [[Totem Statue]]*

Sneaking and using the totem on a block sets it down as a [[Totem Statue]] instead of using it. Dropped items and experience within 5 blocks of the statue fly to it and circle it in a small ring, just above the blocks beside it. A hopper placed next to the statue, leading into a chest, collects the items as they come round. A chest placed against the statue gets nothing. Mining the statue gives the totem back.

# Totem of Gathering

The **Totem of Gathering** is an artifact that pulls in dropped items.

## Obtaining

Found in chests in mineshafts, dungeons and pillager outposts.

## Usage

Held, it pulls dropped items within 8 blocks to its holder. In a reworked beacon with a chest or barrel under the beacon (below its pyramid), it sends every dropped item in the beacon's range into that chest, and experience gathers on top of the beacon. Without a chest it does nothing.

## Statue

Sneaking and using the totem on a block sets it down as a [[Totem Statue]] instead of using it. Mining the statue gives the totem back.

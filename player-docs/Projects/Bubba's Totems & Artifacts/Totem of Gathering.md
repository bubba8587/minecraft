# Totem of Gathering

The **Totem of Gathering** is an artifact that pulls in dropped items.

## Obtaining

Found in chests in mineshafts, dungeons and pillager outposts.

## Usage

Held, it pulls dropped items within 8 blocks to its holder.

In a reworked beacon, dropped items and experience anywhere in the beacon's range fly to the beacon and circle it in a slowly turning ring just above its top. A hopper placed against any side of the beacon, leading into a chest, catches the items as they come round. Items in the ring never despawn. Experience stays in the ring until a player walks up to the beacon. The ring holds up to 48 things; when the beacon stops, it falls apart after a few seconds.

## Statue

*Main article: [[Totem Statue]]*

Sneaking and using the totem on a block sets it down as a [[Totem Statue]] instead of using it. Dropped items and experience within 5 blocks of the statue fly to it and circle it in a small ring, just above the blocks beside it. A hopper placed next to the statue, leading into a chest, collects the items as they come round. A chest placed against the statue gets nothing. Mining the statue gives the totem back.

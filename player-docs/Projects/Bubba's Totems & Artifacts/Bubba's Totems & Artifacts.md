**Bubba's Totems & Artifacts** is a Fabric mod that adds **artifacts**: found items that do things potions and enchantments do not. It holds the Grave Talisman and the Totem of Warding, which were part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] before this mod existed, and found totems can be set down as decorative statues.

Item icons and statues use placeholder art in this early test version.

## Grave Talisman

*Main article: [[Grave Talisman]]*

A talisman whose gem glows brighter the closer undead are.

## Totem of Warding

*Main article: [[Totem of Warding]]*

A found item that makes nearby monsters run away while held, and in a beacon from [[Bubba's Reworked Beacons]] keeps an area free of monsters: none spawn there and none can get in.

## More totems

- [[Totem of Keeping]]: saves its holder's belongings once.
- [[Totem of Gathering]]: pulls in dropped items.
- [[Totem of Fortification]]: stops explosions from breaking blocks.
- [[Totem of Growth]]: makes plants grow faster.
- [[Totem of Seeking]]: points toward a chosen kind of structure.

## Totem statues

*Main article: [[Totem Statue]]*

Sneaking and using a totem on a block sets it down as a decorative statue. Mining the statue gives the totem back.

## Installation

The mod installs like any Fabric mod (see [[Installing Fabric Mods]]) and is needed on both the server and every player's game.

The items are in the creative inventory under the **Bubba's Totems & Artifacts** tab.

### Upgrading from Bubba's Reworked Mobs

Earlier versions of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] contained the Grave Talisman and the Totem of Warding. Players updating that mod should install this one alongside it: talismans and totems already in a world (in chests, inventories and item frames) then carry over as this mod's items. Without this mod, Minecraft removes them when the world loads.

## Textures

The mod comes with vanilla-style 16x textures. A higher-resolution set, **Bubba's Totems & Artifacts 32x (Faithful style)**, can be turned on in Options, Resource Packs; it goes above Faithful 32x if that pack is also used.

## Compatibility

The mod requires Fabric API and works on its own. Two of Bubba's mods add to it when installed, and neither is required:

| Installed with | Adds |
|---|---|
| [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] | The Grave Talisman also senses [[Sleeping Undead|sleeping undead]]; the Totem of Warding calms sleeping undead, and a full ward keeps them asleep. |
| [[Bubba's Reworked Beacons]] | The Totem of Warding fits in a [[Beacon|beacon]] and wards its area. |

The **Totem of Clear Skies** is an artifact that keeps the weather off.

## Obtaining

- Cannot be crafted.
- Found in igloo chests, desert village houses, shipwreck supply chests and [[Relic Vaults|relic vault]] chests.
- Trial chamber vaults: 0.7 % chance each time a player opens a normal vault, 1.4 % for an ominous vault.
- A [[Relic Vaults|relic vault's]] pedestal may hold a Totem of Clear Skies [[Totem Statue|statue]]; breaking the statue gives the totem.

## Usage

- Used while it rains or storms, it ends the weather at once.
- Not used up and has no cooldown; it can be used again the next time it rains.
- In clear weather, using it does nothing.

### In a beacon

- In a [[Bubba's Reworked Beacons]] beacon, no rain, snow or lightning falls anywhere in the beacon's area, from the sky to the ground.
- Snow does not pile up and cauldrons do not fill.
- Ice still forms in cold places.
- The beacon screen reads "No rain, snow or lightning in the area".

## Statue

- Sneaking and using the totem on a block sets it down as a [[Totem Statue]].
- No rain, snow or lightning falls within 5 blocks of the statue.
- Mining the statue gives the totem back.

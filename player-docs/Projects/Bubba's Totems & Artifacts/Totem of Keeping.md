The **Totem of Keeping** is an artifact that saves its holder's belongings once, like a Totem of Undying for items.

## Obtaining

The Totem of Keeping cannot be crafted. It is found in ancient city, end city, stronghold library and woodland mansion chests, and in [[Relic Vaults|relic vault]] chests. It is also found in trial chamber vaults: a 2 % chance each time a player opens a normal vault, and 4 % for an ominous vault.

Evokers drop one about a quarter of the time, as well as their usual Totem of Undying, so like the Totem of Undying it is renewable through raids.

A [[Relic Vaults|relic vault's]] pedestal may also hold a Totem of Keeping [[Totem Statue|statue]]; breaking the statue gives the totem.

## Usage

The totem works from anywhere in the player's inventory, not only from a hand. When the player dies:

- One Totem of Keeping is used up.
- The player keeps every item, all armor and their experience levels, as with the `keep_inventory` game rule. Nothing drops.
- Where the player died, the Totem of Undying's burst of particles and its sound play. After respawning, the player sees the Totem of Keeping fill the screen and the message "Your Totem of Keeping held on to your things".

With the `keep_inventory` game rule already on, the totem is not used. It does nothing in a beacon.

With [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] installed, the totem does not work against a death from [[Soulfire]] (its burning, soul fire or a soul campfire): it is not used and the player's things drop as normal. A player with [[Soulguard]] is kept as usual. A Totem of Undying is skipped the same way.

## Statue

Sneaking and using the totem on a block sets it down as a [[Totem Statue]] instead of using it. The statue has no effect. Mining it gives the totem back.

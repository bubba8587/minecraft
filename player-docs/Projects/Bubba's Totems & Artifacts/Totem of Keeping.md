The **Totem of Keeping** is an artifact that saves its holder's belongings once, like a Totem of Undying for items.

## Obtaining

- Cannot be crafted.
- Found in ancient city, end city, stronghold library, woodland mansion and [[Relic Vaults|relic vault]] chests.
- Trial chamber vaults: 0.7 % chance each time a player opens a normal vault, 1.4 % for an ominous vault.
- Evokers drop one about a quarter of the time, as well as their usual Totem of Undying, so it is renewable through raids.
- A [[Relic Vaults|relic vault's]] pedestal may hold a Totem of Keeping [[Totem Statue|statue]]; breaking the statue gives the totem.

## Usage

- Works from anywhere in the player's inventory, not only from a hand.
- When the player dies:
  - One Totem of Keeping is used up.
  - The player keeps every item, all armor and their experience levels, as with the `keep_inventory` game rule. Nothing drops.
  - Where the player died, the Totem of Undying's burst of particles and its sound play.
  - After respawning, the player sees the Totem of Keeping fill the screen and the message "Your Totem of Keeping held on to your things".
- With the `keep_inventory` game rule already on, the totem is not used.
- Does nothing in a beacon.
- With [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] installed, it does not work against a death from [[Soulfire]] (its burning, soul fire or a soul campfire): it is not used and the player's things drop as normal.
  - A player with [[Soulguard]] is kept as usual.
  - A Totem of Undying is skipped the same way.

## Statue

- Sneaking and using the totem on a block sets it down as a [[Totem Statue]] instead of using it.
- The statue has no effect.
- Mining it gives the totem back.

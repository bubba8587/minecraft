# Totem Statue

*Part of [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]].*

A **totem statue** is a totem set down in the world as decoration: a small stone plinth and column with the totem carved on its front and a glowing rune line on its sides. A statue does nothing; it has no pulse, no ward and no beacon effect. Its look is a placeholder in this early test version.

## Obtaining

The player sneaks and uses a [[Totem of Warding]] on a block. The statue appears on that block, facing the player, and the totem is used up (in Creative mode it is kept). Using a totem without sneaking places nothing, so holding a totem for its pulse never sets it down by accident.

The [[Grave Talisman]] is not a totem and has no statue.

## Breaking

Mining a statue with any tool, or by hand, drops the totem it was made from. A piston pushing the statue also pops it off as its totem.

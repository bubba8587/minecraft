# Shipwreck Crew

*Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]].*

A **shipwreck crew** is the sleeping drowned that [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] places inside shipwrecks.

- The crew lie on their backs, lie face down or sit **between decks**, never outside on the sand. Where the hull is flooded, they lie in the water.
- A whole wreck usually has several; broken fragments mostly have none.
- Some carry a trident, a fishing rod, a nautilus shell or a leather cap.
- They wake like any [[Sleeping Undead]], from noise (see [[Noise and Waking]]), and get up as [[Drowned]] that lurk in the water.
- A sleeping drowned can be mined with Silk Touch and placed again like other sleepers (the item is called "Sleeping Drowned").

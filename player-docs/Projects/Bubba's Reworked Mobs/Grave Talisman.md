The **Grave Talisman** is a small stone talisman whose gem glows when undead are near, brighter the closer they are. It only glows; it has no other effect. This item is an early test, and its look and name may change.

## Obtaining

The Grave Talisman is planned as loot in graves and crypts, not as a crafted item. Until those exist, it is only available in the creative inventory.

## Usage

The talisman glows while it is in either hand, in the hotbar or anywhere in the player's inventory. It counts [[Sleeping Undead|sleeping undead]] as well as awake ones.

| Nearest undead | Glow |
|---|---|
| Within 12 blocks | Brightest |
| Within 24 blocks | Bright |
| Within 40 blocks | Faint |
| None within 40 blocks | Off |

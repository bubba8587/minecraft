# Grave Talisman

Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]]. Early test.

A small stone talisman whose gem glows when undead are near: faintly within 40 blocks, brighter within 24, brightest within 12. It counts [[Sleeping Undead|sleeping undead]] as well as awake ones, and works in either hand or just sitting in your hotbar or inventory. It only glows; it doesn't do anything else.

**Getting one:** it will be found as loot in graves and crypts, not crafted. Until those exist, it's only in the creative inventory. Its look and name may change.

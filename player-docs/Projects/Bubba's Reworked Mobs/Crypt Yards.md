A **crypt yard** is an old graveyard behind an iron-bar fence with stone brick posts and a gate. A single soul lantern hangs on one gate post, and a dirt path leads in from the gate. Rows of graves fill the yard, each a [[Gravestone]] over a plot of coarse or rooted dirt. Some plots have flowers or weeds on them. One or two old trees of the local wood grow where a grave would be; in deserts, dead bushes grow on the graves instead.

Yards are overgrown: some fence bars are missing, some stones are turned askew, and a few have fallen over.

## Graves

Most graves have a [[Sleeping Undead|sleeping]] skeleton or zombie lying under the plot. Now and then a weapon or tool is half-buried in a plot; dig it out with a shovel (see [[Loot on Display]]). The stones never carry names, only a few enchanting-table letters or nothing.

## The family vault

About three in five yards have stone brick stairs at the far end, leading down to a **family vault**: a stone brick room about 9 by 7, lit only by candles. Sleeping skeletons and zombies lie in niches along both walls, some wearing helmets. At the far end, a small altar holds candles and a chest of the family's things: bones, candles, gold nuggets, some iron, and sometimes an emerald, a name tag or an enchanted book. With [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]] installed, the chest sometimes holds a [[Grave Talisman]].

## Finding one

Crypt yards are about one per 36 by 36 chunks, never on water. More states of decay, sizes and kinds of crypt are planned.

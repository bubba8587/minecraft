A **crypt yard** is an old graveyard behind an iron-bar fence with stone brick posts and a gate. A single soul lantern hangs on one gate post, and a path leads in from the gate. Rows of graves fill the yard, each a [[Gravestone]] over a plot of coarse or rooted dirt, some with flowers or weeds. One or two old trees of the local wood grow where a grave would be; in deserts, dead bushes grow on the graves instead.

## Sizes

Yards range from a **lone family plot** (two or three graves, no path, never a crypt) through small and medium yards to **large** yards over 20 blocks across.

## States of decay

Each yard is in one state:
- **Kept:** the whole fence standing, a neat gravel path, stones upright, flowers on the graves.
- **Overgrown:** gaps in the fence, mossy posts, grass and moss through the path, a few stones askew or fallen.
- **Sunken:** the plots have settled into hollows, the path is muddy, half the stones lean, more of the fence is down.
- **Collapsed:** most of the fence down, posts broken off, many stones fallen, and open graves showing the dead below.
- **Nearly gone:** a few stones and scraps of fence in the grass, the plots grassed over, no path and no lantern. The dead are still there.

## The resting dead

Every grave in a yard holds a skeleton that **never wakes**, whatever the noise, a bell, or robbing it. Most are dressed in leather dyed in muted burial colours. Take their gear and dig them up freely: mined with a Silk Touch pickaxe, one stays asleep wherever it is placed, so it works as decoration. Now and then a weapon or tool is half-buried in a plot; dig it out with a shovel (see [[Loot on Display]]). The stones never carry names, only a few enchanting-table letters or nothing.

## Desert and snowy yards

- **Deserts and badlands:** the plots are sand, the crypts hold mostly husks buried in sand, and the graves hold resting husks.
- **Snowy places:** the plots are snow, the crypts hold mostly strays frozen in clear ice, and the graves hold resting strays.

See [[Sleeping Undead]].

## Crypts

Many yards have stairs at the far end leading down to a crypt. Bigger yards have richer crypts. Unlike the yard's dead, the [[Sleeping Undead|sleepers]] down here **do wake**.
- **Pauper's ossuary** (small and medium yards): a cramped tunnel of cobblestone, packed mud and dirt with bones in the walls, lined with niches, almost every one holding a sleeper with little or nothing on them, some stacked two high. A candle or two and a chest of poor things: bones, rotten flesh, string, coal, iron nuggets, rarely an emerald.
- **Family vault:** a candlelit stone brick room, skeletons and zombies in niches along both walls, and a small altar with a chest of the family's things: gold nuggets, some iron, sometimes an emerald, a name tag or an enchanted book. With [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]], sometimes a [[Grave Talisman]].
- **Noble crypt** (medium and large yards): polished andesite and chiseled stone, an entry hall, a pillared chamber with a sarcophagus holding an armoured sleeper under a stone lid, and two side halls lined with niches of sleepers in full iron, chainmail or gold armour, some armed. The chests hold gold, emeralds, iron, a golden apple, sometimes diamonds, enchanted books and enchanted gear. A second roll adds one or two burial goods: a high-level enchanted book, a worn but strongly enchanted diamond sword or chestplate, enchanted gold armour, rarely an enchanted golden apple, a Totem of Undying or music disc 13. With [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]], a [[Grave Talisman]] (one chest in four) and a [[Totem of Warding]] (about one in eight); with [[Bubba's Reworked Mining]], a True Unbreaking book (one chest in five).

A sleeper lying in a niche wakes standing in front of it.

With [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]] installed, crypts often have a [[Totem Statue|Totem of Warding statue]] standing among the sleepers, facing the way in. While it stands, the sleepers near it never wake. Noble crypts can have one in the chamber and one in each side hall (each 85 % likely), family vaults 65 %, pauper's ossuaries 35 %. Breaking the statue gives you the totem, and the sleepers can wake again.

## Finding one

Crypt yards are about one per 36 by 36 chunks, on fairly flat, dry ground, never on water. Faint [[Will-o'-Wisps|will-o'-wisps]] lead you to ones you haven't found, and cartographers sell [[Explorer Maps|Graveyard Explorer Maps]]. With Infused Gems, noble crypt dead wear gemmed [[Noble Finery|finery]]. Operators can build any kind with `/bubbas_reworked_mobs graveyard build <lone|small|medium|large> <kept|overgrown|sunken|collapsed|nearly_gone> <none|pauper|family|noble>` (see [[Reworked Mobs Commands]]).

# Crypt Yards

*Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]].*

> [!infobox] Crypt yard
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Type** | Structure |
> | **Location** | Fairly flat, dry ground in plains, meadows, forests, dark forests, taigas, snowy plains and taigas, savannas, deserts, swamps, windswept hills and the pale garden |
> | **Contains** | [[Gravestone\|Gravestones]], [[Sleeping Undead\|sleeping undead]], sometimes a crypt |

A **crypt yard** is an old graveyard behind an iron-bar fence with stone brick posts and a gate.

- A single soul lantern hangs on one gate post, and a path leads in from the gate.
- Rows of graves fill the yard, each a [[Gravestone]] over a plot of coarse or rooted dirt, some with flowers or weeds.
- One or two old trees of the local wood grow where a grave would be; in deserts, dead bushes grow on the graves instead.

## Generation

- Crypt yards generate about one per 36 by 36 chunks, on fairly flat, dry ground in the biomes listed in the infobox, never on water.
- Faint [[Will-o'-Wisps|will-o'-wisps]] lead the player to yards they haven't found yet, and cartographers sell [[Explorer Maps|Graveyard Explorer Maps]].

### Sizes

- Yards range from a **lone family plot** (two or three graves, no path, never a crypt) through small and medium yards to **large** yards over 20 blocks across.

### States of decay

Each yard is in one state:

- **Kept:** the whole fence standing, a neat gravel path, stones upright, flowers on the graves.
- **Overgrown:** gaps in the fence, mossy posts, grass and moss through the path, a few stones askew or fallen.
- **Sunken:** the plots have settled into hollows, the path is muddy, some stones lean, more of the fence is down.
- **Collapsed:** most of the fence down, posts broken off, many stones fallen, and open graves showing the dead below.
- **Nearly gone:** a few stones and scraps of fence in the grass, the plots grassed over, no path and no lantern. The dead are still there.

### Desert and snowy yards

*See also: [[Sleeping Undead#Husks and strays]]*

- **Deserts:** the crypts hold mostly husks buried in sand, and the graves hold resting husks.
- **Snowy places:** the crypts hold mostly strays frozen in clear ice, and the graves hold resting strays.

## The resting dead

- Every grave in a yard holds a skeleton (a husk or stray in deserts and snowy places) that **never wakes**, whatever the noise, a bell, or robbing it.
- Most are dressed in leather dyed in muted burial colours.
- Their gear can be taken and their bodies dug up freely. Mined with a Silk Touch pickaxe, one stays asleep wherever it is placed, so it works as decoration.
- Now and then a weapon or tool is half-buried in a plot; it has to be dug out with a shovel (see [[Loot on Display]]).
- The stones never carry names, only a few enchanting-table letters or nothing.

## Crypts

- Many yards have stairs at the far end leading down to a crypt.
- Bigger yards have richer crypts.
- Unlike the yard's dead, the [[Sleeping Undead|sleepers]] down here **do wake**. A sleeper lying in a niche wakes standing in front of it.
- **Pauper's ossuary** (small and medium yards):
  - a cramped tunnel of cobblestone, packed mud and dirt with bones in the walls, lined with niches;
  - almost every niche holds a sleeper with little or nothing on, some stacked two high;
  - a candle or two and a chest of poor things: bones, rotten flesh, string, coal, iron nuggets, rarely an emerald.
- **Family vault:**
  - a candlelit stone brick room, skeletons and zombies in niches along both walls;
  - a small altar with a chest of the family's things: gold nuggets, some iron, sometimes an emerald, a name tag or an enchanted book;
  - with [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]], sometimes a [[Grave Talisman]].
- **Noble crypt** (medium and large yards):
  - polished andesite and chiseled stone, an entry hall, and a pillared chamber with a sarcophagus holding an armoured sleeper under a stone lid;
  - two side halls lined with niches of sleepers, a little under half of them in full iron, chainmail or gold armour, some armed;
  - with [[Bubba's Infused Gems]], some of them wear gemmed gear ([[Noble Finery]]).

### Noble crypt loot

- **Chests:** gold, emeralds, iron, sometimes a golden apple or diamonds, enchanted books and enchanted gear.
- **Burial goods** (one per chest): a high-level enchanted book, a worn but strongly enchanted diamond sword or chestplate, or enchanted gold armour; rarely an enchanted golden apple, a Totem of Undying or music disc 13.
- **With [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]]:** a [[Grave Talisman]] (one chest in four) and a [[Totem of Warding]] (about one in eight).
- **With [[Bubba's Reworked Mining]]:** a True Unbreaking book (one chest in five).

### With Bubba's Totems & Artifacts

- **Warding statues:**
  - Crypts often have a [[Totem Statue|Totem of Warding statue]] standing among the sleepers, facing the way in.
  - While it stands, the sleepers near it never wake.
  - Breaking the statue gives the totem, and the sleepers can wake again.

  | Crypt | Statue chance |
  |---|---|
  | Noble crypt | 85 % in the chamber, and 85 % in each side hall |
  | Family vault | 65 % |
  | Pauper's ossuary | 35 % |

- **Relic vaults:**
  - About a third of noble crypts hide a small [[Relic Vaults|relic vault]] behind the far wall, between the two family chests: an old stair leads down from there to a sealed treasure room.
  - Nothing shows it from inside the crypt; it is found by breaking through the wall.
- **Relic maps:** crypt chests also hold [[Relic Maps|relic map shreds]].

## Commands

- Operators can build any kind with `/bubbas_reworked_mobs graveyard build <lone|small|medium|large> <kept|overgrown|sunken|collapsed|nearly_gone> <none|pauper|family|noble>` (see [[Reworked Mobs Commands]]).

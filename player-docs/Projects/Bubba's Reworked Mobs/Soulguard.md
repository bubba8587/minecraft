**Soulguard** is a status effect and an armor enchantment that give full protection from [[Soulfire]]. Its effect icon is a soul-blue flame with a small padlock.

## Effect

A player or mob with Soulguard:

- is not set alight by soul fire or lit soul campfires, and takes no damage from them; stepping into soul fire puts out their flames;
- is still hit by Soulfire charges, but not set burning by them;
- stops soul-burning at once when they gain Soulguard;
- is saved by a Totem of Undying, and kept by a [[Totem of Keeping]], from a Soulfire death as from any other.

Ordinary fire and lava still hurt as normal.

A player with Soulguard does not burn while Phasing from an [[Eye of the Soul]].

## Enchantment

The **Soulguard** enchantment has one level and fits any armor piece. One enchanted piece worn anywhere is enough.

It is a treasure enchantment: it never comes from the enchanting table, villager trades or randomly enchanted loot. Soulguard books are found in about 1 chest in 25 of these:

- bastion remnant chests (treasure room, bridge, hoglin stable and the others);
- Nether fortress chests;
- the room chest of a [[Monster Camps#Nether outposts|Nether outpost]].

Books can be combined onto armor in an anvil as usual.

The [[Wither Captain#Captain's Helm|Captain's Helm]] always comes with Soulguard, shown in its tooltip.

## Gem

With [[Bubba's Infused Gems]] installed, a Potion of Soulguard brews a **Soulguard gem** like any other potion gem (see [[Making Infused Gems]]).

- **In armor:** any Soulguard gem worn gives full Soulguard, however strong the gem is. Its tooltip just says *+ Soulguard*.
- **In a weapon:** a hit gives the target Soulguard for a few seconds.
- **In a beacon** (with [[Bubba's Reworked Beacons]]): it gives Soulguard to everyone in range.

Soulguard gems are never another gem's bonus effect or drawback. Without Bubba's Reworked Mobs there is no Soulguard gem.

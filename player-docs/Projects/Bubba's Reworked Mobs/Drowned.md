A **drowned** in [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] is a rare, tough lurker. Instead of hitting players, it waits in shallow water by the shore, grabs anyone who turns their back to the water, and drags them into deep water to drown.

## Spawning

Drowned spawn in water as before, at the reduced rate of all monsters (see [[Monster Spawning]]), and zombies that drown still turn into drowned. Drowned from spawners and trial spawners behave as normal drowned.

## Behavior

### Lurking

A drowned swims to a shallow spot by the shore, about two blocks deep, and waits there with only its eyes and the top of its head above the water, facing the land. A drowned that someone is looking toward sinks out of sight and rises again when they look away. While anyone watches it, it grabs no one. It is never quite still: it drifts a little, and now and then dips under for a moment. When nobody is looking its way it may move to another shallow spot, and it follows a player who walks along the shore, so a drowned that was there a moment ago may be somewhere else when you look back.

### The grab

A drowned lunges out at a player, animal or villager near the water's edge with its back turned, up to a few blocks onto the bank, and grabs it by the ankles. It then drags its victim backwards, feet first, into the deepest water nearby. A held player cannot swim up or get free by sneaking. Now and then the drowned hits its victim, and each hit knocks out some of its breath. On land a drowned crawls, low and sideways.

### Grip

A drowned's grip starts half-tight and **tightens the longer it holds on**: it strikes more often and harder, knocks out more breath, and more bubbles stream off its victim. Every hit on it loosens the grip, and when the grip is gone it lets go. Hits count more when they come quickly; axes and Impaling loosen it most, and Knockback helps. A Riptide trident pulls its user free at once. Water Breathing, Respiration and Conduit Power only buy time. An unarmored player who doesn't fight back drowns in under ten seconds.

### Others join in

Other drowned nearby swim over and grab the victim's other leg, up to three at once. Each one holding on makes the grip tighten faster and blows count for less, so getting free from several takes help.

## Loot

Drowned no longer throw tridents. A trident picked up is held but not thrown. Tridents can be found in buried treasure instead.

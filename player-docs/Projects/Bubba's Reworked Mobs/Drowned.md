A **drowned** in [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] is a tough lurker that waits in shallow water by the shore, grabs anyone who turns their back to the water and drags them into deep water to drown.

## Spawning

- Drowned spawn in water as in vanilla.
- The reduced monster rate (see [[Monster Spawning]]) holds them back less than other monsters: once the reduced limit is full, a drowned can still spawn naturally as long as fewer than five drowned are within about 48 blocks of the spot, so a player travelling along a coast or river can expect up to five nearby.
- Zombies that drown turn into drowned.
- Drowned from spawners and trial spawners behave as normal vanilla drowned.
- The crew sleeping inside shipwrecks wake up as reworked drowned (see [[Shipwreck Crew]]).

## Behavior

### Lurking

- Swims to a shallow spot by the shore, about two blocks deep, and waits facing the land with only its eyes and the top of its head above the water.
- Sinks out of sight when someone looks toward it and rises again when they look away. While anyone watches it, it grabs no one.
- Is never quite still: drifts a little and now and then dips under for a moment.
- When nobody is looking its way, it may move to another shallow spot.
- Follows a player who walks along the shore, so a drowned seen a moment ago may be somewhere else when the player looks back.

### The grab

- Lunges at a player, animal or villager near the water's edge that has its back turned; it can come a few blocks up onto the bank.
- Grabs its victim by the ankles and drags it backwards, feet first, into the deepest water nearby. On land it crawls, low and sideways.
- A held player cannot swim up, and sneaking does not get them free.
- Now and then hits its victim; each hit knocks out some of the victim's breath.

### Grip

- The grip starts half-tight and **tightens the longer the drowned holds on**.
- As it tightens, the drowned strikes more often and harder, knocks out more breath, and more bubbles stream off its victim.
- Every hit on the drowned loosens the grip, from the victim or anyone else; when the grip is gone, it lets go.
- Harder hits loosen it more, so quick, heavy blows work best. Axes and Impaling loosen it most, and Knockback helps.
- A Riptide trident pulls its user free at once.
- Water Breathing, Respiration and Conduit Power only buy time.
- An unarmored player who doesn't fight back drowns in under ten seconds.
- The game rule `bubbas_reworked_mobs:show_meters` shows the grip as a blue bar.

### Others join in

- Other drowned nearby swim over and grab the victim's other leg, up to three drowned at once.
- Each extra one makes the grip tighten faster and makes blows count for less, so getting free from several usually takes help from another player.

## Loot

- Drowned don't throw tridents. A drowned that picks one up holds it but doesn't throw it.
- Tridents can be found in buried treasure.

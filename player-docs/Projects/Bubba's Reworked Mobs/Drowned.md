> [!infobox] Drowned (reworked)

A **drowned** in [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] is a tough lurker. Instead of hitting players, it waits in shallow water by the shore, grabs anyone who turns their back to the water, and drags them into deep water to drown.

## Spawning

- Drowned spawn in water as in vanilla.
- The reduced monster rate (see [[Monster Spawning]]) holds them back less than other monsters. A player travelling along a coast or river can expect a handful nearby, up to about five in one stretch of water.
- Zombies that drown still turn into drowned.
- Drowned from spawners and trial spawners behave as normal vanilla drowned.
- The crew sleeping inside shipwrecks wake up as reworked drowned (see [[Shipwreck Crew]]).

## Behavior

### Lurking

- A drowned swims to a shallow spot by the shore, about two blocks deep, and waits there facing the land, with only its eyes and the top of its head above the water.
- When someone looks toward it, it sinks out of sight, and it rises again when they look away. While anyone watches it, it grabs no one.
- It is never quite still: it drifts a little, and now and then dips under for a moment.
- When nobody is looking its way, it may move to another shallow spot. It also follows a player who walks along the shore. A drowned that was there a moment ago may be somewhere else when the player looks back.

### The grab

- A drowned lunges at a player, animal or villager near the water's edge that has its back turned. It can come a few blocks up onto the bank.
- It grabs its victim by the ankles and drags it backwards, feet first, into the deepest water nearby. On land it crawls, low and sideways.
- A held player cannot swim up, and sneaking does not get them free.
- Now and then the drowned hits its victim, and each hit knocks out some of its breath.

### Grip

A drowned's grip starts half-tight and **tightens the longer it holds on**. As it tightens, the drowned strikes more often and harder, knocks out more breath, and more bubbles stream off its victim.

- Every hit on the drowned loosens the grip, from the victim or from anyone else. When the grip is gone, it lets go.
- Harder hits loosen it more, so quick, heavy blows work best. Axes and Impaling loosen it most, and Knockback helps.
- A Riptide trident pulls its user free at once.
- Water Breathing, Respiration and Conduit Power only buy time.
- An unarmored player who doesn't fight back drowns in under ten seconds.
- The game rule `bubbas_reworked_mobs:show_meters` shows the grip as a blue bar.

### Others join in

Other drowned nearby swim over and grab the victim's other leg, up to three drowned at once. Each extra one makes the grip tighten faster and makes blows count for less, so getting free from several usually takes help from another player.

## Loot

- Drowned no longer throw tridents. A drowned that picks one up holds it but doesn't throw it.
- Tridents can be found in buried treasure instead.

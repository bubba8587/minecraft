A **drowned** in [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] is a rare, tough lurker. Instead of hitting players, it waits in shallow water by the shore, grabs anyone who turns their back to the water, and drags them into deep water to drown.

## Spawning

Drowned spawn in water as before, at the reduced rate of all monsters (see [[Monster Spawning]]), and zombies that drown still turn into drowned. Drowned from spawners and trial spawners behave as normal drowned.

## Behavior

### Lurking

A drowned swims to a shallow spot by the shore, about two blocks deep, and waits there with only its eyes and the top of its head above the water, facing the land. A drowned that someone is looking at sinks out of sight and rises again when they look away. While anyone watches it, it grabs no one.

### The grab

A drowned lunges out at a player, animal or villager near the water's edge with its back turned, up to a few blocks onto the bank, and grabs it by the ankles. It then drags its victim backwards, feet first, into the deepest water nearby. A held player cannot swim up or get free by sneaking. Now and then the drowned hits its victim, and each hit knocks out some of its breath. On land a drowned crawls, low and sideways.

### Breaking free

Hitting the drowned loosens its grip, faster when the hits come quickly. Axes and Impaling loosen it most, and Knockback helps. Other players' hits count too. A Riptide trident pulls its user free at once. Water Breathing, Respiration and Conduit Power only buy time.

## Loot

Drowned no longer throw tridents. A trident picked up is held but not thrown. Tridents can be found in buried treasure instead.

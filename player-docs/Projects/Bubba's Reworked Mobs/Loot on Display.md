# Loot on Display

*Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]].*

> [!infobox] Loot on display
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Placements** | Ground, buried, wall, stuck |
> | **Holds** | One item |
> | **Collision** | None; players walk over and through it |
> | **Taken with** | Empty hand (buried: shovel) |
> | **Drops when broken** | The displayed item |

**Loot on display** is a weapon or tool shown in the world without an item frame: lying on the ground, hanging on a wall, stuck point-down in the ground, or buried with only the handle showing. It looks exactly like the real item, including items from resource packs and other mods.

## Placements

| Placement | Appearance |
|---|---|
| Ground | Lying flat on the floor |
| Wall | Hanging flat against the side of a block |
| Stuck | Standing upright, point-down in the ground |
| Buried | Mostly underground, with the handle showing |

A display falls and drops its item if the block holding it up is removed.

## Usage

- **Taking:** using a display with an empty hand takes the item. Taking it is quiet, and silent while sneaking.
- **Buried loot** cannot be taken by hand; it has to be dug up with a shovel, which costs the shovel some durability and is loud enough to wake [[Sleeping Undead|sleeping undead]] nearby.
- **Breaking** a display drops its item.
- **Undead:** [[Woken Undead|woken undead]] take whatever is on display near them, not only weapons.

## Obtaining

There is no survival way to place items on display yet. Operators can place them with the `display` command; see [[Reworked Mobs Commands]].

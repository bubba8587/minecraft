# Loot on Display

Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]].

Weapons and tools can lie on the ground, hang on a wall, stand stuck point-down in the ground, or be buried with only the handle showing, looking just like the real item (resource packs and modded items too).

- **Taking it:** use one with an empty hand. Picking it up is quiet, and silent if you sneak.
- **Buried loot** has to be dug up with a shovel, which is noisy enough to wake [[Sleeping Undead|sleeping undead]] nearby.
- **Breaking** the display drops the item.
- **Watch out:** [[Woken Undead|undead]] grab whatever is on display near them, not just weapons.

There's no survival way to put items on display yet; operators can use the `display` command ([[Reworked Mobs Commands]]).

# Loot on Display

*Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]].*

> [!infobox] Loot on display
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Placements** | Ground, buried, wall, stuck |
> | **Holds** | One item |
> | **Collision** | None; players walk over and through it |
> | **Taken with** | Empty hand (buried: shovel) |
> | **Drops when broken** | The displayed item |

**Loot on display** is an item shown in the world without an item frame: lying on the ground, hanging on a wall, stuck point-down in the ground, or buried with only the handle showing.

- It looks exactly like the real item, including items from resource packs and other mods.
- Weapons and tools look best.

## Obtaining

- **Generation:** lies near sleeping undead, half-buried in graves in [[Crypt Yards]], and hung on crypt walls.
- **Placing:** **sneak + middle click** with an item in hand lays one of it on the ground as a display; see [[Placing Items Anywhere]].
- **Commands:** operators can place any placement kind with the `display` command; see [[Reworked Mobs Commands]].

## Placements

| Placement | Appearance |
|---|---|
| Ground | Lying flat on the floor |
| Wall | Hanging flat against the side of a block |
| Stuck | Standing upright, point-down in the ground |
| Buried | Mostly underground, with the handle showing |

- A display breaks and drops its item if the block holding it up is removed, or if a piston pushes it.

## Usage

- **Taking:** using a display with an empty hand takes the item. Taking it is quiet, and silent while sneaking. Using it with something in hand does nothing.
- **Buried loot** cannot be taken by hand; it has to be dug up with a shovel. Digging costs the shovel 1 durability and is loud enough to wake [[Sleeping Undead|sleeping undead]] nearby.
- **Breaking** a display drops its item. A buried display takes a moment to mine, like packed dirt; the others break at once.
- **Undead:** [[Woken Undead|woken undead]] with empty hands take whatever is on display near them, not only weapons.

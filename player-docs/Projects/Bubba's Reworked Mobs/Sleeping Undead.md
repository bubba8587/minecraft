A **sleeping undead** is a dormant skeleton, zombie, husk or stray that lies motionless in the world until noise wakes it. All four sit slumped or lie on their backs. They often hold a weapon and wear armor, and they neither move nor attack while asleep.

## Husks and strays

In deserts and snowy places the sleepers are husks and strays.

- A **sleeping husk** lies buried in sand. When it wakes, the sand bursts in a cloud that **blinds** anyone within a few blocks for 2 seconds. Woken husks don't burn by day, and their hits make you hungry.
- A **sleeping stray** lies frozen in ice. When something wakes it, the ice **cracks first** with a glassy sound and shards. You have about a second and a half before it breaks free. Woken strays shoot Slowness arrows.

## Behavior

*Main article: [[Noise and Waking]]*

Noise near a sleeping undead disturbs it, and enough noise wakes it. Once awake it becomes a [[Woken Undead|woken undead]] with the same gear.

**Back to sleep at dawn:** undead that were awake during the night and have nothing to fight at sunrise walk to the nearest dark, covered spot within about 16 blocks (an open grave, a crypt niche, a dim roofed corner) and become sleepers again, keeping their gear and loot. A cleared crypt slowly fills up again if you leave survivors.

**Fire:** a lit campfire, soul campfire or fire keeps idle monsters that fear fire about 6 blocks away. A monster already chasing someone ignores it, and Nether mobs and fire-proof mobs don't care. An unlit campfire does nothing.

**Drowned:** shipwrecks hold sleeping drowned; see [[Shipwreck Crew]].

## Robbing

Using a sleeping undead with an empty hand takes one item from it: the weapon first, then the armor from the head down, one piece per use.

| Item taken | Chance of waking it |
|---|---|
| Weapon | 10 % |
| Armor piece | 30 % |

A sleeping undead woken by a theft attacks the thief.

## Mining

| Tool | Drops |
|---|---|
| Silk Touch pickaxe | The body, as a sleeping-undead item that can be placed again |
| Anything else | A bone (skeleton, stray) or rotten flesh (zombie, husk) |

In either case, the weapon and armor drop as separate items.

## Placing

A sleeping-undead item can be placed again; it turns to face the player who places it. Placed sleeping undead can still wake up, which makes them usable as traps, or as decoration if the player is careful.

## Obtaining

Sleeping undead lie in the crypts under [[Crypt Yards]]: husks in desert yards, strays in snowy ones. All four kinds, sitting and lying, are in the creative inventory under the **Bubba's Reworked Mobs** tab.

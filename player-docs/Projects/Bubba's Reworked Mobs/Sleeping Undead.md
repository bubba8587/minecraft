A **sleeping undead** is a dormant skeleton, zombie, husk, stray or drowned that lies motionless in the world until noise wakes it. Sleeping undead sit slumped, lie on their backs or lie face down. They often hold a weapon and wear armor, and they neither move nor attack while asleep.

## Obtaining

- **Crypts:** sleeping undead lie in the crypts under [[Crypt Yards]]: mostly husks in desert yards, mostly strays in snowy ones, skeletons and zombies elsewhere.
- **Shipwrecks:** sleeping drowned lie between the decks of shipwrecks; see [[Shipwreck Crew]].
- **Creative mode:** every kind, in every pose, sleeping or as a statue, is in the **Bubba's Reworked Mobs** tab.

## Behavior

*Main article: [[Noise and Waking]]*

Noise near a sleeping undead disturbs it, and enough noise wakes it. Once awake it becomes a [[Woken Undead|woken undead]] with the same gear.

- **Back to sleep at dawn:** undead that were awake during the night and have nothing to fight at sunrise walk to the nearest dark, covered spot within about 16 blocks (an open grave, a crypt niche, a dim roofed corner) and become sleepers again, keeping their gear and loot. A cleared crypt slowly fills up again if survivors are left in it.
- **Fire:** a lit campfire, soul campfire or fire keeps idle monsters that fear fire about 6 blocks away. A monster already chasing someone ignores it, and Nether mobs and fire-proof mobs don't care. An unlit campfire does nothing.
- **Face down:** about a third of the sleepers lying in crypt niches and noble halls lie face down, as do a few of the resting dead in yard graves and many of a shipwreck's lying crew. A face-down sleeper wakes like any other.

### Husks and strays

In deserts and snowy places the sleepers are husks and strays.

- A **sleeping husk** lies buried in sand. When it wakes, the sand bursts in a cloud that **blinds** any player within 4 blocks for 2 seconds. Woken husks don't burn by day, and their bare-handed hits cause Hunger.
- A **sleeping stray** lies frozen in ice. When something wakes it, the ice **cracks first** with a glassy sound and shards, and the stray breaks free about a second and a half later. Woken strays shoot Slowness arrows.

## Robbing

Using a sleeping undead with an empty hand takes one item from it: the weapon first, then the armor from the head down, one piece per use.

| Item taken | Chance of waking it |
|---|---|
| Weapon | 10 % |
| Armor piece | 30 % |

A sleeping undead woken by a theft attacks the thief.

## Mining

| Tool | Drops |
|---|---|
| Silk Touch pickaxe | The body, as a sleeping-undead item that can be placed again |
| Anything else | A bone (skeleton, stray) or rotten flesh (zombie, husk, drowned) |

In either case, the weapon and armor drop as separate items.

The item keeps the body's pose (sitting, lying or face down) and whether it is a statue, and so does picking the block with the middle mouse button in Creative mode. The item's name shows its form, such as "Sleeping Zombie (Lying)", "Skeleton Statue (Sitting)" or "Sleeping Skeleton (Face Down)".

## Placing

- A sleeping-undead item places the body back exactly as it was, turned to face the player who places it.
- A placed sleeping undead can still wake up, which makes it usable as a trap, or as decoration if the player is careful.
- A statue never wakes.

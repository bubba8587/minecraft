# Camp Patrols

*Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]].*
*See also: [[Monster Camps]]*

> [!infobox] Camp patrol

A **camp patrol** is a small band of a monster camp's monsters that leaves the camp to look for players in the land around it.

- Patrols carry a camp's danger out across its land, so clearing a camp makes the whole area round it safer.

## Spawning

- Every **armed** camp (one of its banners still standing, and no lit campfire in it) watches the land within about 320 blocks of it.
- At night, a player in that land may be found by a patrol of the camp's kind. A player out at night usually meets about one patrol a night.
- Patrols appear some distance from the player, in a dark spot, out of sight where they can.
- Skeleton, zombie, stray and bogged patrols come only at night, when the sun would not burn them. Husk patrols come by day too.
- A player standing in the land of more than one camp can meet a patrol from either.
- Nether outposts send no patrols, and vanilla pillager patrols are unchanged.

## Behavior

- A patrol has **2-3 members from a small camp, 3-4 from a medium one and 4-5 from a large one**, the leader included.
- The patrol first heads toward where the player stood when it appeared, then wanders the camp's land, keeping together.
- Its members attack any player they see.
- Patrol members are ordinary monsters of the camp's kind, with the usual chance of armor and weapons. They despawn like ordinary monsters.
- Patrols are not part of the camp's roster: killing a patrol does not clear the camp or count toward clearing it.

### The leader

- The **leader** carries the camp's banner on its head. It is otherwise an ordinary member of the patrol.
- When killed, it always drops a **Monster Camp Explorer Map** centred on its camp, with the camp marked by a white X.
- The banner itself does not drop.

### Riders

- Camps with a stable (every large camp and about half of medium ones; see [[Monster Camps#Stables]]) often send their patrols out riding.
- The leader always rides, and each other member half the time.

| Camp | Riders |
|---|---|
| Zombie | Zombies on zombie horses; sometimes with iron spears |
| Skeleton, stray | Skeletons or strays on skeleton horses, with bows |
| Husk | Husks on camel husks with a parched riding behind; sometimes with iron spears |
| Bogged | Always on foot |

## Making an area safe

- **Break the camp's banners** or **keep a campfire lit** in it, and the camp stops patrolling its land (see [[Monster Camps#Banners are the key]] and [[Monster Camps#Lighting a fire]]).
- A **[[Totem of Warding]]** area (in a beacon or a statue, from [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]]) keeps patrols from appearing for a player standing inside it, and no patrol member appears inside one.
- Patrols do not appear in Peaceful, or when the `spawn_patrols`, `spawn_monsters` or `spawn_mobs` game rules are off.

## Commands

- Operators can call up a patrol with `/bubbas_reworked_mobs camp patrol [<kind>] [mounted|foot]` (see [[Reworked Mobs Commands]]).

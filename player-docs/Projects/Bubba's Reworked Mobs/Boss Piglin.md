The **Boss Piglin** is a huge, fat piglin that sits on a throne in medium and large Nether outposts.

- He wears trimmed gold armor and a crimson cape.
- He watches while his piglins fight, and waddles off to escape once they are all dead.

## Spawning

- Medium and large [[Monster Camps#Nether outposts|Nether outposts]] have a throne near the fire, facing it, and the Boss Piglin sits on it. Small outposts have none.
- When the outpost refills (its banner still standing, after a day with no player nearby), he is back on his throne with the new piglins.
  - If he is still alive somewhere, he goes back and sits down.
  - A new one only appears if at least half of the throne still stands.
- The **Boss Piglin Spawn Egg** (with the vanilla piglin egg icon) is in the creative inventory under the Bubba's Reworked Mobs tab.
  - A Boss Piglin from an egg has no throne and just runs away.
  - Like any piglin, one from an egg turns into a zombified piglin outside the Nether; an outpost's boss does not.

## Behavior

### On the throne

- While any of the outpost's piglins are alive, the Boss Piglin stays seated.
- He doesn't move or fight, even when hit.
- He turns his head to watch the nearest player and shouts now and then in a deep piglin voice.

### Running away

- When the last of his piglins dies, he gets up, steps off the throne and runs, keeping about 10 blocks from players.
- He heads for the outpost's portal room. If the portal there is lit and he reaches it, he steps through and is gone, with his loot. He doesn't come out anywhere else.
- He is **too fat for the stair tunnel** down to the portal room. Mostly he gets stuck, gives up with an angry grunt and keeps running and hiding round the outpost, trying the portal again a while later.
- If the portal is unlit or broken, he just runs round the outpost.
- While running he doesn't watch his step and can waddle off a cliff.

### Cornered

- A player who stays right next to him (within about 2 blocks) for a second gets slapped: about as hard as a piglin's punch (5 damage), with strong knockback.
- He slaps at most every 2 seconds, then keeps running.

### Gold

- Hitting the Boss Piglin makes the outpost's piglins angry at the player, even one wearing gold.

## The throne

- Built from vanilla blocks: a seat of polished blackstone brick slabs with four purple cushions, a tall backrest of polished blackstone bricks, gilded blackstone, chiseled polished blackstone and gold blocks, framed by crimson posts, with crimson shelf armrests.
- A gold snack (a gold ingot, golden carrot, gold nugget or cooked porkchop) sits on each armrest.

## Appearance

- A piglin's head sunk into a huge round body, a big bare gut and a humped back, about half again as big as a piglin all round.
- His gold armor sits on him as plates over the chest, belly, hips, arms and legs.
- His crimson cape has a gold edge, a fringed hem and a gold crown over a piglin snout.
- He waddles with short, side-to-side steps, and flails his arms when he runs.

## Textures

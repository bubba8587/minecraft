# Soul Blaze Items

*Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]].*

**Soul blaze items** are the soul-blue versions of the blaze rod, blaze powder and fire charge: the **Soul Blaze Rod**, **Soul Blaze Powder** and **Soul Fire Charge**. They come from [[Soulfire Blaze|Soulfire Blazes]] and are stronger fuels than their vanilla twins.

## Soul Blaze Rod

> [!infobox] Soul Blaze Rod
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Type** | Material |
> | **Obtained** | [[Soulfire Blaze]] drop |
> | **Furnace fuel** | Smelts 24 items (a blaze rod: 12) |
> | **Renewable** | Yes |
> | **Stackable** | Yes (64) |

The **Soul Blaze Rod** is dropped by Soulfire Blazes instead of blaze rods, at the same odds as a blaze: 0 or 1 when killed by a player, with Looting adding up to 1 more. Blazes summoned by a [[Wither Captain]] that burn out when their captain dies drop nothing.

Uses:

- furnace fuel, smelting 24 items;
- crafts 2 Soul Blaze Powder;
- brews the [[Soulguard|Potion of Soulguard]] from an Awkward Potion.

## Soul Blaze Powder

> [!infobox] Soul Blaze Powder

> |---|---|
> | **Type** | Material |

> | **Brewing fuel** | 40 brews (blaze powder: 20) |

**Soul Blaze Powder** fuels a brewing stand for 40 brews, twice as many as blaze powder, and is used to craft Soul Fire Charges and the [[Eye of the Soul]].

| Name | Ingredients | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Soul Blaze Powder | 1 Soul Blaze Rod | 2 Soul Blaze Powder |

## Soul Fire Charge

> [!infobox] Soul Fire Charge
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Type** | Item, projectile |
> | **Obtained** | Crafted |
> | **Damage** | 7.5 on a hit (a blaze's fireball: 5) |
> | **Effect** | 5 seconds of [[Soulfire]] |
> | **Stackable** | Yes (64) |

The **Soul Fire Charge** is a fire charge that throws Soulfire.

- **Used on a block**, it lights a fire there (or lights a campfire or candle), like a fire charge.
- **Used in the air**, it is thrown where the player looks, with half a second between throws.
- **In a dispenser**, it is fired out like a blaze's fireball.

A thrown or fired charge hits harder than a fire charge (7.5 damage) and sets what it hits [[Soulfire|soul-burning]] for 5 seconds. Where it lands, it lights soul fire on soul sand and soul soil, and ordinary fire everywhere else. It is the same charge a [[Soulfire Blaze]] shoots.

| Name | Ingredients | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Soul Fire Charge | Gunpowder + Soul Blaze Powder + Coal or Charcoal (shapeless) | 3 Soul Fire Charges |

## Textures

All three have vanilla-style 16x textures, recoloured from the vanilla items to soul-fire blue, with 32x versions in **Bubba's Mods 32x**.

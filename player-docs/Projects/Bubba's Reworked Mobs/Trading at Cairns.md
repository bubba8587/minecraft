# Trading at Cairns

*Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]].*

> [!infobox] Trade pad
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Where** | Beside every enderman cairn |
> | **Accepts** | Any item |
> | **Answer** | Random, after 4 to 10 seconds |

**Trading at cairns** is a way for the player to swap any item for a random one from the endermen.

## Trade pad

- Every enderman cairn has a **trade pad**: a purpur plinth skirted by four end stone brick slabs, a few blocks outside the cairn.
- Each cairn keeps its pad in its own spot around it.
- The endermen build and repair the pad with the rest of the cairn; a cairn without its pad counts as unfinished until it has one.

## How to trade

1. Lay any item on the plinth with **sneak + middle click** (see [[Placing Items Anywhere]]).
2. Walk away. Once nobody is within 16 blocks, an enderman teleports in beside the pad, stares at the item for a few seconds, then takes it and vanishes with portal particles. If the player picks the item back up before then, the enderman leaves without it.
3. 4 to 10 seconds later an enderman teleports in beside the pad holding its answer and lays it on the plinth. The answer stays there until the player picks it up.

- No trades happen while the cairn is warded.

## Answers

- What the player gives doesn't matter: the answer is random.
- **Good (about 4 in 10):** ender pearls, chorus fruit, emeralds, bottles o' enchanting, end rods, popped chorus fruit, and now and then an enchanted book, a diamond, a golden apple, a name tag, a disc fragment, a nautilus shell, a shulker shell or an echo shard.
- **Rare (about 1 trade in 18):** a sniffer egg, or one of the rarest music discs: Pigstep, Otherside, Relic, Creator, Creator (Music Box) or Precipice.
- **Weird (the rest):** a poppy, dirt, end stone, a poisonous potato, a carved pumpkin, string, rotten flesh and other oddments, now and then TNT or a cake.
- It is a way to get rid of junk, and maybe get something better back.

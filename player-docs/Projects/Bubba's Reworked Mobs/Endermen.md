An **enderman** in [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] is a caretaker of the overworld. It slowly puts the land back the way it first generated, and it builds strange purple spires called **enderman cairns**.

## Spawning

Most overworld endermen come from cairn sites, at night. A few still appear anywhere, as rarely as other monsters; those are ordinary endermen that only pick up and drop blocks at random, as are endermen from spawners. A fresh cairn draws one only now and then and grows by a block or two a night; once its spire is clearly rising, more and more come, and the last part goes up quickly. A finished cairn draws the most, and the more endermen are already around a site, the faster more arrive. Killing them slows this down but never stops it. Tearing a cairn down drops it back to the lower rate until it is rebuilt. By day, cairn endermen out under the sky leave, vanishing in a puff of purple, along with any block they carry; a name-tagged one stays. Each cairn draws its own endermen, so cairns close together don't hold each other back. Now and then an idle enderman wanders off on an errand to fix something farther away, then comes back to its cairn. A builder waiting for the blocks below its own to go in stands in a ring around the cairn until it can place its block. Dig up the ground near a cairn and, at night, endermen arrive carrying exactly what you took (the right grass, dirt or stone) and put it back; the more holes, the more of them come.

Endermen in the Nether and the End are unchanged.

## Behavior

### Mending the land

An enderman takes blocks that don't belong in the natural landscape (a pile of dirt, a pillar, the wrong block on the ground's surface) and carries them to where they are missing. Holes dug near the surface slowly fill back in with the right kind of block, and farmland where dirt used to be gets jumped on until it turns back to dirt. Mines, caves and deep pits are left alone, and water and lava are never put back.

An enderman tends the land around wherever it happens to be: one that ends up on a farm or a path starts putting it back. It only notices what is close by, and not always.

Blocks that never generate naturally, such as planks, glass and bricks, are mostly left alone, but not always. Trees, flowers and other natural features are left where they are, unless a nearby cairn needs that exact block as an offering.

### Building cairns

An **enderman cairn** is a twisting spire of purpur on a footing of end stone bricks, with end rods sprouting from its branches like flowers and offerings at its foot that depend on the biome: flowers, pumpkins, mushrooms, cactus and, rarely, TNT. Every cairn is different. A new cairn site looks like nothing much: a few offerings, such as flowers on moss, mushrooms or a pumpkin, set out a little too neatly. Some endermen arrive carrying an End block it is missing, each bringing a different one; others arrive empty-handed and clear grass, dirt or trees out of its way, or bring a block it wants if they happen to spot one nearby. Together they grow it to its full shape over two to four weeks of nights, starting with an end stone footing sunk into the ground. An enderman whose block can't go in yet waits by the cairn until the blocks below it are in place. Killed, an enderman drops the block it carries.

The tallest end rod of every cairn lies on its side and points toward the nearest stronghold.

### Finished cairns

A finished cairn shimmers with purple portal particles, and sparkles drift up from its top, visible from far away at night. Endermen at a finished cairn with nothing to do stand in a ring around it, staring at it. The ring grows as more arrive.

With [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]] installed, the endermen of some finished cairns (about one in seven) leave a [[Totem of the Beginning]] lying at the cairn's foot as an offering, about a minute after it is finished while one of them is there. A player takes it with an empty hand. Each cairn does this only once, ever. Finishing a cairn yourself earns nothing.

### Hostility

Endermen still become hostile when a player looks at them, unless they are busy working. Breaking a block of a cairn, or hitting an enderman near one, turns every enderman within 24 blocks against the player. A player holding a [[Totem of the End]] is treated as one of their own: eye contact does not anger them, and breaking or fighting near a cairn does not turn them against that player.

Endermen still take damage from water.

## Wards

*Main article: [[Totem of Warding]]*

With [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]] installed, a ward keeps endermen out: no endermen spawn from a cairn inside it, and they do no work there.

## Commands

*Main article: [[Reworked Mobs Commands]]*

The game rule `bubbas_reworked_mobs:cairn_endermen` turns cairn spawning off and brings normal enderman spawning back. Operators can find, finish or place cairns with `/bubbas_reworked_mobs cairn`.

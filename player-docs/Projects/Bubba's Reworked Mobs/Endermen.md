An **enderman** in [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] is a caretaker of the overworld. It slowly puts the land back the way it first generated, and it builds strange purple structures called **enderman cairns**.

## Spawning

Most overworld endermen come from cairn sites, at night.

- **Ordinary endermen:** a few still appear anywhere, as rarely as other monsters. They only pick up and drop blocks at random, like normal endermen. Endermen from spawners are ordinary too.
- **Growing cairns:** a fresh cairn draws an enderman only now and then and grows by a block or two a night. Once it is clearly rising, more and more come, and the last part goes up quickly.
- **Finished cairns** draw the most.
- **The more endermen are already around a site, the faster more arrive.** Killing them slows this down but never stops it. Tearing a cairn down drops it back to the lower rate until it is rebuilt.
- Each cairn draws its own endermen, so cairns close together don't hold each other back.
- **Holes:** if the player digs up the ground near a cairn, endermen arrive at night carrying exactly what was taken (the right grass, dirt or stone) and put it back. The more holes, the more of them come.
- **By day,** cairn endermen out under the sky leave, vanishing in a puff of purple along with any block they carry. A name-tagged one stays.

Endermen in the Nether and the End are unchanged.

## Behavior

### Mending the land

An enderman takes blocks that don't belong in the natural landscape (a pile of dirt, a pillar, the wrong block on the ground's surface) and carries them to where they are missing. If nowhere needs a block it picked up (a tree cleared out of a cairn's way, say), it teleports off with it and vanishes a few seconds later.

- Holes dug near the surface slowly fill back in with the right kind of block.
- Farmland where dirt used to be gets jumped on until it turns back to dirt.
- Mines, caves and deep pits are left alone, and water and lava are never put back.
- Builds are left alone: planks, glass, bricks and other crafted blocks are not taken.
- Trees, flowers and other natural features stay where they are, unless a nearby cairn needs that exact block as an offering.

An enderman tends the land around wherever it happens to be: one that ends up on a farm or a path starts putting it back. It only notices what is close by, and not always. Now and then an idle enderman goes off on an errand to fix something farther away, then comes back to its cairn.

### Building cairns

An **enderman cairn** is a structure of End blocks (purpur, end stone bricks, end rods, stained glass) with offerings at its foot that depend on the biome: flowers, pumpkins, mushrooms, cactus and, rarely, TNT. Every cairn is different, and each takes one of seven forms:

- **Chorus:** a twisting purpur spire of one to three trunks.
- **Grove:** a thicket of three to five short twisting trunks on a wide footing.
- **Spire:** one tall straight column with tiers of short arms tipped with end rods.
- **Arch:** two columns joined across the top like a gateway, sometimes with glass hanging under the span.
- **Canopy:** a thick trunk opening into a crown of branches, like a purpur tree.
- **Henge:** a ring of end stone posts capped with purpur round a small spire.
- **Leaning:** a tall stalk leaning far to one side, with a shorter one leaning the other way.

Footings are round, square, cross- or diamond-shaped. Glass is magenta, purple or pink. Offerings lie in a ring, a cluster to one side, or a line leading away.

The tallest end rod of every cairn lies on its side and points toward the nearest stronghold.

How a cairn goes up:

- A new cairn site looks like nothing much: a few offerings, such as flowers on moss, mushrooms or a pumpkin, set out a little too neatly.
- Some endermen arrive carrying an End block the cairn is missing, each bringing a different one. Others arrive empty-handed and clear grass, dirt or trees out of its way, or bring a block it wants if they happen to spot one nearby.
- Building starts with an end stone footing sunk into the ground. A cairn reaches its full shape over two to six weeks of nights; bigger forms take longer.
- An enderman whose block can't go in yet sets it down near the cairn (never on the cairn or by the trade pad) and goes on with other work. Others bring it in once the blocks below it are in place.
- Endermen standing where a block has to go step aside so it can go in.
- A killed enderman drops the block it carries.

### Finished cairns

- A finished cairn shimmers with purple portal particles, and sparkles drift up from its top, visible from far away at night.
- Endermen at a finished cairn with nothing to do stand in a ring around it, staring at it. The ring grows as more arrive.
- With [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]] installed, the endermen of some finished cairns (about three in ten) leave a [[Totem of the Beginning]] lying at the cairn's foot as an offering. This happens about a minute after the cairn is finished, while one of its endermen is there, and only once per cairn, ever. A player takes it with an empty hand.
- A player who finishes a cairn themselves earns nothing for it.

### Hostility

- Endermen still become hostile when a player looks at them, unless they are busy working.
- Breaking a block of a cairn, or hitting an enderman near one, turns every enderman within 24 blocks against the player.
- A player holding a [[Totem of the End]] is treated as one of their own: eye contact does not anger them, and breaking or fighting near a cairn does not turn them against that player.

Endermen still take damage from water.

## Trading

*Main article: [[Trading at Cairns]]*

Every cairn has a trade pad. A player lays any item on it and walks away; the endermen take it and come back with something random, sometimes good and sometimes very weird.

## Wards

*Main article: [[Totem of Warding]]*

With [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]] installed, a ward keeps endermen out: no endermen spawn from a cairn inside it, and they do no work there.

## Commands

*Main article: [[Reworked Mobs Commands]]*

The game rule `bubbas_reworked_mobs:cairn_endermen` turns cairn spawning off; the few ordinary endermen still appear as before. Operators can find, finish or place cairns with `/bubbas_reworked_mobs cairn`.

# Soulfire

*Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]].*

**Soulfire** is a stronger kind of burning that comes from soul fire. A soul-burning mob or player burns with blue flames instead of orange ones, and the faint sound of souls comes from them while it lasts. Soulfire is fire with a soul part: the fire part works like ordinary burning, and the soul part hurts the living a little more.

## Causes

- Standing in **soul fire**.
- Touching a **lit soul campfire**. With this mod, soul campfires set mobs and players on fire, as fire does.
- **Phasing** from an [[Eye of the Soul]]: the player soul-burns for as long as it lasts.
- Being hit by a **Soulfire charge**: shot by a [[Soulfire Blaze]], or thrown or fired from a dispenser as a [[Soul Blaze Items#Soul Fire Charge|Soul Fire Charge]]. A hit sets the target soul-burning for 5 seconds.

Any ordinary way of catching fire turns Soulfire back into normal burning: touching ordinary fire, lava or a lit ordinary campfire, or being set alight by a fire charge, Fire Aspect, a flaming arrow or lightning. Soulfire ends when the fire goes out, as normal burning does.

## Damage

| Source | Living mobs and players | Undead |
|---|---|---|
| Soulfire burning (each second) | 1.5 | 1 (as ordinary burning) |
| Standing in soul fire, touching a lit soul campfire | 2 | 1 (as ordinary fire) |

Undead ignore the soul part and take exactly what ordinary fire would do to them. Mobs that are immune to fire, such as blazes, striders and wither skeletons, stay immune.

### Fire Resistance

Fire Resistance (the potion, or the perfect set of Fire Resistance gems in [[Bubba's Infused Gems]]) blocks only the fire part. A living player or mob with Fire Resistance still takes the soul part: about half a heart every 2 seconds while soul-burning, and soul fire and soul campfires still do half their usual damage. Undead with Fire Resistance take nothing. Fire Protection reduces Soulfire like any fire damage.

## Totems

Dying to Soulfire (the burning, soul fire or a soul campfire) **cannot be stopped by a Totem of Undying**, and a [[Totem of Keeping]] from [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]] does not keep the player's things. Both work as normal for a player who has [[Soulguard]].

## Protection

*Main article: [[Soulguard]]*

[[Soulguard]] (a potion effect, a treasure enchantment or, with [[Bubba's Infused Gems]], a gem) gives full protection from Soulfire. The [[Wither Captain#Captain's Helm|Captain's Helm]] carries it.

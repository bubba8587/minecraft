# Noble Finery

*Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]].*

**Noble finery** is the gemmed burial gear worn by some of the armoured dead in **noble crypts** ([[Crypt Yards]]) when [[Bubba's Infused Gems]] is installed.

## Behavior

- The noble in the sarcophagus always wears finery; about a third of the others do.
- Most armor pieces and weapons carry one gem, a little richer than ordinary gemmed mob gear. Some pieces carry none.
- Woken, they fight with those gem effects.
- Robbing or killing them yields the gemmed pieces.
- Without Infused Gems, noble crypts are unchanged.

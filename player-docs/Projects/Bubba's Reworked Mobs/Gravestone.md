A **gravestone** is a stone headstone on a small plinth. It works exactly like a standing sign: you can write on its front and back, and the edit screen opens right after you place it. Dyes colour the text, glow ink makes it glow, and honeycomb waxes it so it can't be edited. It can face any of 16 directions. You can't walk through it.

## Obtaining

Cut one from a single stone in a stonecutter. Gravestones also stand over the graves in [[Crypt Yards]]. Those never carry names: they're blank, or carved with a few letters of the enchanting-table alphabet. They aren't waxed, so you can carve your own words on a found stone.

## Breaking

Mine it with a pickaxe to get it back. Without a pickaxe it drops nothing.

A **woken undead** is a skeleton, zombie, husk or stray that has woken from sleep. It is much tougher and slower than a vanilla one, keeps the gear it slept with, and never despawns. Woken undead are easy to run from and hard to kill.

## Spawning

*Main article: [[Noise and Waking]]*

Woken undead appear when a [[Sleeping Undead|sleeping undead]] wakes, in the same place and with the same gear, facing the noise that woke it.

## Behavior

- **Tough and slow.** Woken undead have far more health than vanilla skeletons and zombies (vanilla: 20), weak fists, and slow movement.
- **Persistent.** They never despawn, and they always drop their gear when they die.
- **Movement.** Woken zombies shamble with their arms hanging: they drag one leg up, then fall forward onto the other. Their real movement follows the shamble, creeping and then taking a quicker step, but on average they are no faster than before. Woken skeletons stagger toward the player with both arms reaching out.
- **Groups.** Idle woken zombies slowly drift together into groups.
- **Husks and strays.** Woken husks don't burn by day and their bare-handed hits cause Hunger; woken strays shoot Slowness arrows, as in vanilla. See [[Sleeping Undead#Husks and strays]].

### Picking up loot

A woken undead that wakes with empty hands grabs something within a few blocks: a weapon if there is one (sword, axe, spear, bow, crossbow, trident or mace), otherwise whatever else is lying there, on display or dropped on the ground.

Later, an empty-handed undead that is not chasing anyone walks over to nearby [[Loot on Display|loot on display]] or items on the ground and picks them up. While it is chasing a player, it ignores loot.

## Combat

*Main article: [[Fighting the Undead]]*

Repeated fist hits from a woken undead shred armor. Smite can destroy one in a single hit, arrows do a quarter of their damage to woken skeletons, and headshots do double damage to woken zombies.

# Woken Undead

Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]].

- **Tougher and slower.** Much more health, weak fists, slow on their feet: easy to run from, hard to kill.
- **They stay.** Woken undead never despawn and keep the gear they slept with (it always drops when they die).
- **They move like the dead.** Woken zombies shamble with their arms hanging: they drag one leg up, then fall forward onto the other, and they really do move that way, creeping, then a quicker step (on average no faster than before). Woken skeletons stagger toward you with both arms reaching out. Idle zombies slowly drift together into groups.
- **They arm themselves.** An undead that wakes with empty hands grabs something within a few blocks: a weapon if there is one (sword, axe, spear, bow, crossbow, trident or mace), otherwise whatever else is lying there, on display or dropped on the ground. Later, an empty-handed undead that isn't chasing anyone wanders over to [[Loot on Display|loot on display]] (or items on the ground) nearby and picks it up. While it's after you, it ignores loot and keeps coming.

How to fight them: [[Fighting the Undead]].

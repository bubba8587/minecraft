A **woken undead** is a skeleton, zombie, husk or stray that has woken from sleep: much tougher and slower than a vanilla one, easy to run from and hard to kill.

## Spawning

*Main article: [[Noise and Waking]]*

- Appears when a [[Sleeping Undead|sleeping undead]] wakes, in the same place and with the same gear, facing the noise that woke it.
- A sleeping drowned wakes as an ordinary drowned, keeping its gear (see [[Shipwreck Crew]]).

## Behavior

- **Tough and slow:** far more health than vanilla skeletons and zombies (vanilla: 20), weak fists and slow movement.
- **Persistent:** never despawns, and always drops its gear when it dies.
- **Movement:**
  - Woken zombies shamble with their arms hanging: they drag one leg up, then fall forward onto the other.
  - Their real movement follows the shamble, creeping and then taking a quicker step, but on average they cover ground as fast as a steady walk at the same speed would.
  - Woken skeletons stagger toward the player with both arms reaching out.
- **Groups:** idle woken zombies slowly drift together into groups.
- **Husks and strays:** woken husks don't burn by day and their bare-handed hits cause Hunger; woken strays shoot Slowness arrows, as in vanilla. See [[Sleeping Undead#Husks and strays]].
- **Back to sleep:** survivors of the night lie down again at dawn; see [[Sleeping Undead]].

### Picking up loot

- **On waking:** a woken undead with empty hands grabs something within 3 blocks: a weapon if there is one (sword, axe, spear, bow, crossbow, trident or mace), otherwise whatever else is lying there, on display or dropped on the ground.
- **Later:** an empty-handed undead that is not chasing anyone walks over to nearby [[Loot on Display|loot on display]] or items on the ground and picks them up.
- While chasing a player, it ignores loot.

## Combat

*Main article: [[Fighting the Undead]]*

- Repeated fist hits from a woken undead shred armor.
- Smite can destroy one in a single hit.
- Arrows do a quarter of their damage to woken skeletons.
- Headshots do double damage to woken zombies.

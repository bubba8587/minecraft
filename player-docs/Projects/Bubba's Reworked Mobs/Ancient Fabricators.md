An **Ancient Fabricator** is a structure beside every ancient city. Its living warped roof drips monsters onto an ancient teleporter, which sends them up to the surface. Each Fabricator makes one kind of monster, shown in the great face on its back wall.

## Generation

Every ancient city has a Fabricator next to it, reached through a tunnel cut through the city's outer wall. Fabricators only appear in newly generated parts of a world.

## Behavior

While a player is nearby, the roof drops a monster onto the teleporter every few seconds. It lands limp and still. Eight lamps around the teleporter light one by one; when all are lit, whatever stands on the teleporter is lifted a little and sent up to the surface, where monsters wake on dark ground at night. A monster appearing in a burst of portal light is a sign a Fabricator lies beneath. In daylight the teleporter waits until night.

- Monsters can be killed on the teleporter before they are sent.
- Powering the lodestone at the centre of the teleporter with redstone stops it, and monsters pile up on the deck.
- A player who steps on the teleporter is sent up to the surface too.
- A spawner hidden in the roof is what keeps the Fabricator running. Breaking it stops the Fabricator for good.

## The Warden

A Warden watches from its office window. It stays still until it hears a player going for the roof or the beacons, or is hit, and then it hunts like any Warden.

## The beacons

The Fabricator's beacons weaken everyone nearby with Mining Fatigue, Weakness and Slowness. Taking the item out of the floor beacon, or breaking it, ends the effects. With [[Bubba's Infused Gems]] installed, the beacon runs on a harmful gem.

## Loot

The Warden's office holds chests with paper, books, echo shards, disc fragments and sometimes a music disc or a Swift Sneak book; with [[Bubba's Infused Gems]], sometimes a spare gem.

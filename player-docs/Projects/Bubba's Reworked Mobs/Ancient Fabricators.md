An **Ancient Fabricator** is a structure beside every ancient city. Its living warped roof drips monsters onto an ancient teleporter, which sends them up to the surface. Each Fabricator makes one kind of monster, shown in the great face on its back wall.

## Generation

Every ancient city has a Fabricator next to it, reached through a tunnel cut through the city's outer wall. Fabricators only appear in newly generated parts of a world.

## Behavior

While a player is nearby, the Fabricator rests in the dark for a while, often a minute or more, then the roof drops a monster onto the teleporter. It lands limp and still. Eight lamps around the teleporter light one by one; when all are lit, whatever stands on the teleporter is lifted a little and sent up to the surface in a burst of light, where monsters wake on dark ground at night. A monster appearing in a burst of portal light is a sign a Fabricator lies beneath. In daylight the teleporter waits until night.

- Monsters can be killed on the teleporter before they are sent.
- Powering the lodestone at the centre of the teleporter with redstone stops it, and monsters pile up on the deck.
- A player who steps on the teleporter is sent up to the surface too.
- A [[Totem of Warding]] warding the Fabricator (for example in its own beacon) stops the roof dropping monsters. The teleporter keeps working, and limp monsters already on the deck are left where they are.
- A spawner hidden in the roof is what keeps the Fabricator running. Breaking it stops the Fabricator for good.

## Secrets

A Fabricator hides more than its machine:

- One tile in the deck looks a little wrong. Under it, a ladder leads down into a sealed pit of failed products, with a chest of rejects.
- Up inside the roof, over the great dripper, is the chamber that feeds the machine, full of soul soil, bone and sculk. Twisting vines grow up into it from one of the chains. Breaking blocks up there wakes the Warden, even while sneaking.
- Below the deck lie terraces, ruined arches, a small hut, stairs going down every way, and chests buried in the rubble.
- The Warden's office has a sunken archive and a loft.

## The Warden

A Warden watches from its office window. It stays still until a player goes for the hidden spawner, the roof or the beacons without sneaking, breaks a block there, or hits it; then it hunts like any Warden, except that it never digs away. There is only one, and it does not come back.

## The beacons

The Fabricator's beacons weaken every player within about 90 blocks with Mining Fatigue, Weakness and Slowness. Breaking the floor beacon ends the effects; with [[Bubba's Reworked Beacons]] installed, so does taking the item out of it (a Fabricator Heart, which makes a debuff beacon of any beacon it is put in). With [[Bubba's Infused Gems]] installed, the beacon runs on a harmful gem.

## Loot

Getting past the Warden pays. Each of the three chests in its office holds paperwork, valuables (diamonds, emeralds, gold, iron, echo shards, bottles o' enchanting) and always one treasure: a Swift Sneak book, a high-level enchanted book or diamond tool or armor piece, an enchanted golden apple, a totem of undying, a netherite upgrade template, a recovery compass, a ward or silence armor trim, or a music disc. With [[Bubba's Infused Gems]], sometimes a spare gem as well.

The chests hidden elsewhere around a Fabricator (in the reject pit and buried in the city below) are smaller, but they hold diamonds, emeralds, golden apples and enchanted gear too.

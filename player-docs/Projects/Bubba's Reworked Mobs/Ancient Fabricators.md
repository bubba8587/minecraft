# Ancient Fabricators

*Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]].*

> [!infobox] Ancient Fabricator
> | | |

An **Ancient Fabricator** is a structure beside every ancient city whose living warped roof drips monsters onto an ancient teleporter that sends them up to the surface.

- Each Fabricator makes one kind of monster, shown as a great block-art face on its back wall.

## Generation

- Every ancient city has a Fabricator next to it, in a dark cavern reached through a tunnel cut through the city's outer wall.
- Fabricators only appear in newly generated parts of a world.
- Like an ancient city, a Fabricator is dark. The teleporter and the great dripper above it glow; most of the rest needs a torch.

## Behavior

While a player is nearby, the Fabricator works in cycles:

1. It rests in the dark for a while, often a minute or more.
2. The roof drops a monster onto the teleporter. The monster lands limp and still, and takes no fall damage.
3. Eight lamps around the teleporter light one by one, about two seconds apart.
4. When all eight are lit, whatever stands on the teleporter is lifted a little and sent up to the surface in a burst of light.
5. On the surface, the monster lies limp for a moment in a column of portal light, then wakes as one of this mod's reworked monsters.

Where monsters arrive:

- On dark ground within about 96 blocks of the Fabricator, never close to a player.
- In daylight, in dark spots instead, such as caves and overhangs above the Fabricator.
- A monster appearing in a burst of portal light is a sign that a Fabricator lies beneath.

Stopping and using it:

- Monsters can be killed on the teleporter before they are sent.
- Powering the lodestone at the centre of the teleporter with redstone stops it. Monsters then pile up limp on the deck, up to twelve.
- A player who steps on the teleporter is sent up to the surface too, at any time of day.
- A [[Totem of Warding]] warding the Fabricator (for example in its own beacon) stops the roof dropping monsters. The teleporter keeps working, and limp monsters already on the deck are left where they are.
- A spawner hidden in the roof keeps the Fabricator running. Breaking it stops the Fabricator for good, and every limp monster on the deck wakes up.
- Fabricators do not run on Peaceful, or when the game rule that lets spawners work is off.

## Secrets

- One tile in the deck looks a little wrong. Under it, a ladder leads down into a sealed pit of failed products, with a chest of rejects.
- Up inside the roof, over the great dripper, is the chamber that feeds the machine, full of soul soil, bone and sculk.
  - Twisting vines grow up into it from one of the chains.
  - Breaking blocks up there wakes the Warden, even while sneaking.
- Below the deck lie terraces, ruined arches, a small hut, stairs going down every way, and chests buried in the rubble.
- A causeway leads from the tunnel to the deck.
- The Warden's office has a sunken archive and a loft.

## The Warden

- A Warden watches the teleporter from its office window.
- It stays still and hears nothing until a player in Survival or Adventure mode:
  - goes for the hidden spawner, the roof or the beacons without sneaking;
  - breaks a block in those places, even while sneaking; or
  - hits it.
- Then it hunts like any Warden, except that it never digs away.
- There is only one, and it does not come back.

## The beacons

- The Fabricator has two beacons: one on the deck and one set upside down in the roof above it.
- They weaken every player in Survival or Adventure mode within about 90 blocks with Mining Fatigue III, Weakness and Slowness.
- Breaking the floor beacon ends the effects.
- With [[Bubba's Reworked Beacons]] installed, the two beacons are a working pair with a beam between them. The floor beacon then holds a **Fabricator Heart**; taking it out also ends the effects, and putting it in any other beacon makes a debuff beacon.
- With [[Bubba's Infused Gems]] installed as well, the floor beacon holds a harmful gem instead of the heart.

## Loot

Each of the three chests in the Warden's office holds:

- paperwork;
- valuables: diamonds, emeralds, gold, iron, echo shards and bottles o' enchanting;
- always one treasure: a Swift Sneak book, a high-level enchanted book, a diamond tool or armor piece, an enchanted golden apple, a totem of undying, a netherite upgrade template, a recovery compass, a ward or silence armor trim, or a music disc;
- with [[Bubba's Infused Gems]], sometimes a spare harmful gem as well.

Elsewhere:

- The chests hidden around a Fabricator (in the reject pit and buried in the ruins below) are smaller, but hold diamonds, emeralds, golden apples and enchanted gear too.
- The iron blocks under the beacons, and the beacons themselves, can be taken as well.

## Finding one

- Cartographers sell a Fabricator Explorer Map (see [[Explorer Maps]]).
- Operators can find and build Fabricators with commands (see [[Reworked Mobs Commands]]).

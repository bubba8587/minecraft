# Monster Camps

*Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]].*

> [!infobox] Monster camp
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Type** | Structure |
> | **Location** | Most overworld land biomes; the kind follows the biome |
> | **Contains** | A roster of monsters with a leader, banners, a loot chest, a dark room underground |

A **monster camp** is a dark, trodden clearing held by a band of undead. It has scrappy lookout towers on stilts, bone-and-skull rock piles, skulls on pikes, a cold campfire ringed by log seats, a lean-to and a chest of loot. Camps have **no light at all**, so they're hard to spot at night. Far fewer monsters spawn on their own (see [[Monster Spawning]]), so camps are where to go for skeletons and zombies.

## Kinds

| Kind | Where |
|---|---|
| Skeleton, zombie | Most biomes |
| Husk | Deserts and badlands |
| Stray | Snowy biomes |
| Bogged | Swamps and mangroves |

There are three sizes:

- **Small:** 3 monsters and a leader.
- **Medium:** 5 monsters and a leader.
- **Large:** 7 monsters and a leader, with two towers.

## The roster

The camp's monsters appear when you come within about 48 blocks. Archers man the towers and the rest stand round the fire. They stay at their camp and never despawn.

No two parts of a camp are built over each other: towers, rock piles, pikes, lean-tos and banners always keep clear of the stair tunnel, the fire and each other. A part with no room is left out, so some camps have fewer towers or rock piles than others.

By day they keep to the shade: under the lean-to, under the tower roofs, or down in the **underground room** beneath the camp, reached by a stair tunnel. Caught in the sun, they burn like any other undead (husks don't).

**The leader** is the same kind of monster, a bit bigger and much tougher. It wears an iron helmet and chestplate and carries an iron axe or a strong bow. It sometimes drops its gear.

## Banners are the key

Every camp flies banners in its kind's own pattern. The banners keep the camp alive:

- **Break every banner** of the camp's kind and the camp is gone for good.
- **Leave one standing** and a cleared camp comes back. About once a day there's a chance it refills, but only while nobody is near.
- **Put a banner back** anywhere in the camp and it's armed again. Camp banners drop with their pattern. A re-armed camp refills the next day you're away, so you can keep one as a farm.

## Lighting a fire

A lit campfire anywhere in a camp **quiets it** while it burns: no roster appears and it doesn't refill. Put the fire out and the camp wakes again. That's how to switch a camp off without breaking its banners.

## Pillager outposts

Pillager outposts follow the same rule. Break every banner in an outpost and pillagers stop spawning there; put a banner back and they return. Patrols are unchanged.

## Finding one

There are about six camps in every 1000 by 1000 blocks, never near villages. A skeleton or zombie camp is usually a few hundred blocks away. Husk, stray and bogged camps are only found in their own biomes. Operators can build one with `/bubbas_reworked_mobs camp build` (see [[Reworked Mobs Commands]]).

# Monster Camps

*Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]].*

> [!infobox] Monster camp

A **monster camp** is a dark, trodden clearing held by a band of monsters. Camps have **no light at all**, so they are hard to spot at night. Far fewer monsters spawn on their own (see [[Monster Spawning]]), so camps are where to go for skeletons and zombies. An armed camp also sends patrols out over the land around it (see [[Camp Patrols]]).

## Kinds

| Kind | Where |
|---|---|
| Skeleton, zombie | Most biomes |
| Husk | Deserts and badlands |
| Stray | Snowy biomes |
| Bogged | Swamps and mangroves |
| Piglin ([[#Nether outposts]]) | Crimson forests and the Nether wastes |

Each kind is built from its own materials: for example, husk camps use acacia and sandstone, stray camps spruce and packed ice, and bogged camps mangrove wood and mud bricks.

## Sizes

| Size | Monsters | Banners | Towers | Stable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small | 3 and a leader | 1 | up to 1 | none |
| Medium | 5 and a leader | 2 | up to 2 | about half |
| Large | 7 and a leader | 3 | up to 3 | always |

## Layout

Every camp has a cold, unlit campfire ringed by log seats, a chest of loot by the fire, at least one lean-to over bedrolls, and an underground room. Most also have some of:

- scrappy lookout towers on stilts, with a ladder and a slab roof;
- bone-and-skull rock piles;
- skulls on pikes.

The number of each part varies, so no two camps look alike. No part is ever built over another: towers, rock piles, pikes, lean-tos and banners keep clear of the stair tunnel, the fire and each other, and a part with no room is left out.

### Big camps

Large camps can have bigger landmarks, so a camp often reads as a plan from a distance:

- **Skull rocks:** a huge hollow skull of bone, calcite and quartz, with a heavy brow over dark eye sockets. The way in is through the mouth. Half of them are **half-buried**, with only the top of the skull and the eyes showing; the way in is through one eye. A chest stands at the back inside the skull. Pikes, rock piles and a banner stand round its sides and back, so the skull sits inside the camp.
- **Winding towers:** a tall log tower, 12 to 14 blocks high, with a wooden stair winding round the outside and an archer on the railed top. An archer's stash lies on the tower's sealed ground floor: it can be seen through the slit windows, and the player has to break in to reach it.
- **Ribcage arches:** a giant bone spine with ribs curling down to the ground, and a path running under it.

Any camp's towers can have a **ramp** of wooden stairs up to the platform. In medium and large camps, two towers can be joined by a sagging plank **bridge** with log rails and chains.

### Stables

Every large camp and about half of medium camps have a **stable**: a small roofed pen of the camp's wood, with log corner posts, a plank back wall, a fence rail with a gate facing the fire, a slab roof and hay bales at the back. Inside, one or two mounts are tied with leads to a post at the back:

- sometimes the camp's undead mount: a zombie horse (zombie camps), a skeleton horse (skeleton, stray and bogged camps) or a camel husk (husk camps);
- sometimes a captive living horse or donkey.

Clicking the lead knot on the post takes all the leads at once, so the player can lead the animals home, as with any leashed animal. The undead mounts stay in the shade of the roof; a zombie horse led out into the sun burns like any other zombie horse.

Stable animals are not part of the camp's monsters: killing or taking them does not count toward clearing the camp. When the camp's monsters come back after a refill, an empty stable is restocked as long as its post still stands. Breaking the post leaves it empty for good.

Camps with a stable often send riders on their patrols (see [[Camp Patrols]]).

### The underground room

A stair tunnel leads down to a dark room under the camp, with rock walls, a beamed ceiling, cobwebs, bones and a small chest. Its size varies from camp to camp. The tunnel has plenty of headroom and opens to the sky from its first step, and its top meets the ground flush, so the camp's monsters, leaders included, climb out of the room easily.

## Loot

A camp has up to four chests. From least to most valuable:

| Chest | Where | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| Fire chest | By the campfire, in every camp | Bones, arrows, rotten flesh, string, leather, bread, a little iron and gold, an emerald, gunpowder; sometimes an enchanted iron sword, axe or bow, chain armor, a saddle, a name tag, an enchanted book or a golden apple |
| Room chest | In the underground room, in every camp | Bones, arrows, coal, nuggets, string, candles; often an ingot, emeralds, golden carrots, and sometimes an enchanted book, an enchanted iron sword or bow, or a golden apple |
| Tower chest | On the sealed ground floor of a winding tower (some large camps) | Many arrows, tipped arrows (slowness, poison, weakness), string, feathers, flint, iron, gold, an emerald or two; usually a bow or crossbow (sometimes enchanted); sometimes a spyglass, golden carrots, an enchanted book or a name tag |
| Skull chest | At the back inside a skull rock (some large camps) | Bones, arrows, gold, iron, emeralds, golden carrots; always one enchanted iron weapon, armor piece or bow; often an enchanted book or a golden apple, rarely one or two diamonds, and sometimes (about 1 in 12) a music disc: 13, cat, blocks, chirp, far, mall, mellohi, stal, strad, ward, 11, wait or Lava Chicken |

## The roster

- The camp's monsters appear when a player comes within about 48 blocks, day or night. By day they appear in the shade.
- Archers man the towers and the rest stand round the fire.
- They stay at their camp and never despawn. Zombie camps have no babies.
- They wear armor about as often as ordinary monsters do (rarely), and some zombies and husks carry stone swords or axes.
- They guard their camp rather than hunt you down: chase one more than about 32 blocks from camp and it gives up and walks back.
- By day they keep to the shade: under the lean-to, under the tower roofs, or down in the underground room. Caught in the sun, they burn like any other undead (husks don't).

**The leader** is the same kind of monster, a bit bigger and much tougher, with more than twice the health. It wears an iron helmet and chestplate and carries an iron axe (zombies and husks) or a strong bow (skeletons, strays and bogged). It does not carry a banner (a patrol's leader does; see [[Camp Patrols]]). It drops its weapon half the time, and each armor piece one time in five.

## Banners are the key

Every camp flies banners in its kind's own pattern (for example, a white skull on black for skeletons and a black skull on green for zombies). The banners keep the camp alive:

- **Break every banner** of the camp's kind and the camp is gone for good.
- **Leave one standing** and a cleared camp comes back. About once a day there is a chance it refills, but only while nobody is within about 96 blocks.
- **Put a banner back** anywhere in the camp and it is armed again. Camp banners drop with their pattern; a banner of another kind does not count. A re-armed camp refills the next day the player is away, so a camp can be kept as a farm.

## Lighting a fire

A lit campfire or soul campfire anywhere in a camp **quiets it** while it burns: no roster appears and the camp does not refill. Put the fire out or break it and the camp wakes again. This is how to switch a camp off without breaking its banners. A quieted or cleared camp also stops sending patrols.

## Pillager outposts

Pillager outposts follow the same rule. Break every banner in an outpost and pillagers stop spawning there; put any banner back and they return. Patrols are unchanged.

## Nether outposts

A **Nether outpost** is the piglin kind of monster camp, found only in crimson forests and the Nether wastes. Outposts are much rarer than overworld camps and never stand near a bastion or fortress. Each sits on a levelled shelf of ground under the Nether's ceiling, with any lava close by sealed over with blackstone.

- **Look:** crimson-wood watchtowers with nether brick railings and polished blackstone roofs, a gilded blackstone gate in a stretch of rampart hung with soul lanterns, rock piles studded with gilded blackstone and gold, piglin heads and lanterns on pikes, and nether brick bridges between towers. Unlike overworld camps, outposts are lit.
- **Hoglin pen:** a fenced pen of crimson nylium with one to three hoglins kept as livestock, the first tied to a post in the middle. Piglins don't hunt them. They don't count toward clearing the outpost, and they come back with the roster if the pen is empty and its post still stands.
- **The roster:** piglins, about one in four of them a piglin brute. Lookouts on the towers carry crossbows; the rest carry crossbows or golden swords, and brutes golden axes. As in vanilla, piglins leave a player wearing gold alone.
- **The leader** is a big piglin brute in enchanted gold armor with an enchanted golden axe.
- **The portal room:** the stair tunnel leads deeper than in overworld camps, to a room with an obsidian Nether portal frame at the back, like a ruined portal. Some portals are lit and work as normal, some are whole but unlit with crying obsidian in the frame, and some are broken.
- **Loot:** a chest by the fire holds gold, crimson fungus, porkchops, quartz, a worn crossbow and sometimes the outpost's banner. The room's chest holds better gold gear, obsidian, crying obsidian, flint and steel, the piglin banner pattern, a snout armor trim and, rarely, netherite scrap. About 1 room chest in 7 also holds a netherite upgrade smithing template.

Banners, refilling and lit fires work the same as in every other camp. The outpost's banner is black with a yellow piglin snout.

## Finding one

- Camps never stand near villages, and need fairly flat, dry ground.
- There are roughly three camps in every 1000 by 1000 blocks. A skeleton or zombie camp is usually a few hundred blocks away.
- Husk, stray and bogged camps are only found in their own biomes, and piglin outposts only in the Nether.
- Cartographers sell a Monster Camp Explorer Map (see [[Explorer Maps]]).
- The leader of a camp's patrol always drops a map to its camp (see [[Camp Patrols]]).
- Operators can find and build camps with `/bubbas_reworked_mobs camp` (see [[Reworked Mobs Commands]]).

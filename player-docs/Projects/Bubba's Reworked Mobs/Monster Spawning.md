# Monster Spawning

*Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]].*

**Monster spawning** is cut by [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] to 15 % of normal for natural spawns, everywhere: on the surface, in caves, in the Nether and in the End. Most undead a player meets are [[Sleeping Undead|sleeping]] ones instead.

## Unchanged

Monsters from the following keep their normal spawning:

- Witch huts, ocean monuments, pillager outposts and Nether fortresses
- Spawners and trial spawners

## Game rules

*Main article: [[Reworked Mobs Commands]]*

The amount, and whether the cut applies at all, can be changed with game rules.

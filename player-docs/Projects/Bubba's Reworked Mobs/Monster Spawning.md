# Monster Spawning

Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]].

Natural monster spawning is cut to 15 % of normal, everywhere (surface, caves, the Nether and the End). Most undead you meet are [[Sleeping Undead|sleeping]] ones instead.

Unchanged: witch huts, ocean monuments, pillager outposts, Nether fortresses, spawners and trial spawners.

Adjust or turn it off with game rules: [[Reworked Mobs Commands]].

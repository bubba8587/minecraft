# Monster Spawning

*Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]].*

**Monster spawning** is cut by [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] to 15 % of normal for natural spawns, everywhere: on the surface, in caves, in the Nether and in the End.

## Where monsters come from

Most monsters a player meets come from places instead:

- [[Sleeping Undead]] in [[Crypt Yards]] and shipwrecks, which wake from noise;
- [[Monster Camps]], which hold skeletons, zombies and their biome kinds (and piglins in the Nether), and send [[Camp Patrols|patrols]] over the land around them at night;
- [[Ancient Fabricators]], which send monsters up to the surface above them;
- spider nests in caves (see [[Spiders]]).

## Exceptions

- **Creepers** do not spawn randomly at all. Each player has one watching creeper at night instead (see [[Creepers]]).
- **Endermen** in the overworld mostly spawn near enderman cairns, and only a few anywhere (see [[Endermen]]).
- **Drowned** can still spawn naturally once the reduced limit is full, as long as fewer than five drowned are within about 48 blocks of the spot, so up to five gather near a player by the water (see [[Drowned]]).

## Unchanged

Monsters from the following keep their normal spawning:

- witch huts, ocean monuments, pillager outposts and Nether fortresses (a pillager outpost stops spawning pillagers once its banners are broken; see [[Monster Camps#Pillager outposts]]);
- spawners, trial spawners and raids.

## Game rules

*Main article: [[Reworked Mobs Commands]]*

- The amount, and whether the cut applies at all, can be changed with game rules.

## Wards

*Main article: [[Totem of Warding]]*

- With [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]] and [[Bubba's Reworked Beacons]] installed, no monsters spawn naturally inside the area of a beacon holding a Totem of Warding, phantoms included.
- Spawners, trial spawners, raids and bosses spawn as normal, but any monster that ends up inside a ward is pushed out.
- No monster can enter a ward.

**Bubba's Reworked Mobs** is a Fabric mod that reworks Minecraft's mobs one family at a time. Part 1 covers the undead: skeletons and zombies lie dormant in the world as bodies that wake when players make noise near them, and far fewer monsters spawn on their own. Creepers are nervous watchers that explode only when startled, and endermen mend the land and build cairns.

In this early test version, sleeping bodies use the normal skeleton and zombie look, and item icons are placeholders until the final art is drawn.

## Sleeping undead

*Main article: [[Sleeping Undead]]*

Skeletons and zombies sit or lie motionless, often armed and armored. They can be robbed, dug up with a Silk Touch pickaxe, and placed again as traps or decoration.

## Waking

*Main article: [[Noise and Waking]]*

Noise near a sleeping undead adds up until it wakes. Sneaking and wool walls keep players quiet, and a waking undead can wake its neighbors.

## Woken undead

*Main article: [[Woken Undead]]*

Woken undead are tougher and slower than vanilla ones, never despawn, shamble or stagger, and arm themselves with loot lying nearby.

### Combat

*Main article: [[Fighting the Undead]]*

Repeated fist hits shred armor, Smite can destroy a woken undead in one hit, arrows do little to skeletons, and headshots do double damage to zombies.

## Loot on display

*Main article: [[Loot on Display]]*

Weapons and tools lie on the ground, hang on walls, stand stuck in the ground or are buried, and can be taken by players and by undead.

## Grave Talisman and Totem of Warding

The [[Grave Talisman]] and the [[Totem of Warding]] are now part of [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]]. With that mod installed, the talisman also senses sleeping undead and the totem calms them. Players upgrading from an earlier version of this mod should install it to keep their talismans and totems.

## Creepers

*Main article: [[Creepers]]*

Creepers watch players from behind cover and explode only when startled repeatedly; calm players are safe, and crouching during a hiss calms them. Each player has one watching creeper at night instead of random creeper spawns.

## Endermen

*Main article: [[Endermen]]*

Endermen slowly put the overworld back the way it first generated and build twisting purpur cairns, which point toward strongholds. They spawn from cairn sites instead of anywhere.

## Drowned

*Main article: [[Drowned]]*

Drowned are rare, tough lurkers that wait in shallow water, grab anyone who turns their back to the water, and drag them under to drown. Hard, fast hits break their grip.

## Crypt yards and gravestones

*Main articles: [[Crypt Yards]], [[Gravestone]]*

Fenced graveyards in every state of decay stand on dry, flat ground, their graves holding skeletons that never wake; many have a crypt below (a pauper's ossuary, a family vault or a noble crypt) whose sleepers do wake. Gravestones work like signs, and players can cut their own in a stonecutter.

## Spiders

*Main article: [[Spiders]]*

Spiders climb walls and ceilings. Cobweb nests cling to the ceilings of tall cave rooms. Walk underneath and a spider drops on a thread; if it lands, it reels you up and cocoons you. Giant spiders roam old-growth taiga, webbing the trees and snatching livestock.

## Monster camps

*Main article: [[Monster Camps]]*

Dark camps of skeletons, zombies, husks, strays or bogged, each with a leader. Break every banner to clear a camp for good, leave one to let it refill, or light a fire to quiet it. Pillager outposts stop spawning when their banners are gone.

## Bogged

*Main article: [[Bogged]]*

Bogged hide in swamp water and mud and ambush you when you turn your back.

## Ancient Fabricators

*Main article: [[Ancient Fabricators]]*

Beside every ancient city, an Ancient Fabricator drips monsters onto a teleporter that sends them to the surface. Players can farm them on the teleporter, stop it with redstone, or break its hidden spawner, past a Warden.

## Monster spawning

*Main article: [[Monster Spawning]]*

Natural monster spawning is cut to 15 % of normal everywhere. Structures and spawners are unchanged.

## Commands

*Main article: [[Reworked Mobs Commands]]*

Game rules control the spawn cut and watcher creepers, and an operator command puts items on display.

## Installation

The mod installs like any Fabric mod (see [[Installing Fabric Mods]]) and is needed on both the server and every player's game.

Sleeping undead generate in [[Crypt Yards]] and their crypts. They are in the creative inventory under the **Bubba's Reworked Mobs** tab (a lying zombie is its own entry), and every feature can be tried in the [[Bubba Test World]].

## Textures

The mod comes with vanilla-style 16x textures. A higher-resolution set, **Bubba's Reworked Mobs 32x (Faithful style)**, can be turned on in Options, Resource Packs; it goes above Faithful 32x if that pack is also used.

## Compatibility

The mod requires Fabric API and [Stormie's Spiders](https://modrinth.com/mod/stormies-spiders), which makes spiders climb walls. Only this mod's own undead are changed: normal zombies and skeletons from spawners and other mods behave as usual.

Optional: [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]], whose [[Grave Talisman]] and [[Totem of Warding]] sense and calm this mod's sleeping undead. Everything here works the same without it.

# Bubba's Reworked Mobs

Reworks Minecraft's mobs, a family at a time. Part 1 is the undead: skeletons and zombies now lie dormant in the world as bodies that wake up when you make noise near them, and far fewer monsters spawn at night.

Early test version: the sleeping bodies still use the normal skeleton and zombie look, and item icons are placeholders until the real art is drawn.

## Pages

- [[Sleeping Undead]]: what they are, robbing them, digging them up, placing them.
- [[Noise and Waking]]: what wakes them, how to sneak past, chain wake-ups.
- [[Woken Undead]]: what happens once they're up.
- [[Fighting the Undead]]: Smite, bows, and not getting cornered.
- [[Loot on Display]]: weapons and tools lying around, hung on walls or buried.
- [[Grave Talisman]]: a charm that glows when undead are near.
- [[Monster Spawning]]: far fewer monsters spawn on their own.
- [[Reworked Mobs Commands]]: game rules and commands.

## Installing

Install like any Fabric mod (see [[Installing Fabric Mods]]); it's needed on both the server and every player's game. In this test version sleeping undead don't generate in the world yet: find them in the creative inventory under **Bubba's Reworked Mobs** (a lying zombie is its own entry), or try them all in [[Bubba Test World]].

## Textures

Comes with vanilla-style 16x textures. For higher resolution, turn on **Bubba's Reworked Mobs 32x (Faithful style)** in Options, Resource Packs (place it above Faithful 32x if you use it).

## Works with

Requires Fabric API. Only the undead from this mod are changed: normal zombies and skeletons from spawners and other mods behave as usual.

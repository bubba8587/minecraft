**Bubba's Reworked Mobs** is a Fabric mod that reworks Minecraft's mobs one family at a time.

- Far fewer monsters spawn on their own; instead they come from places:
  - undead lie dormant in graveyards and crypts until noise wakes them;
  - spiders nest in caves;
  - monster camps hold skeletons and zombies (and piglins in the Nether);
  - Ancient Fabricators beside ancient cities send monsters to the surface.
- In the Nether, rare Wither Captains lead fortress garrisons and summon Soulfire Blazes, soul fire burns harder as Soulfire, and a fat Boss Piglin sits enthroned in piglin outposts.
- Creepers are nervous watchers that explode only when startled, endermen mend the land and build cairns, and drowned and bogged lurk at the water's edge.
- In this early test version, sleeping bodies use the normal mob look, and sleeper item icons are the matching mob heads.

## Sleeping undead

*Main article: [[Sleeping Undead]]*

- Skeletons, zombies, husks, strays and drowned sit or lie motionless, often armed and armored.
- They can be robbed, dug up with a Silk Touch pickaxe, and placed again as traps or decoration.

## Waking

*Main article: [[Noise and Waking]]*

- Noise near a sleeping undead adds up until it wakes.
- Sneaking and wool walls keep players quiet.
- A waking undead can wake its neighbors.

## Woken undead

*Main article: [[Woken Undead]]*

- Tougher and slower than vanilla undead.
- Never despawn, shamble or stagger, and arm themselves with loot lying nearby.

### Combat

*Main article: [[Fighting the Undead]]*

- Repeated fist hits shred armor.
- Smite can destroy a woken undead in one hit.
- Arrows do little to skeletons.
- Headshots do double damage to zombies.

## Loot on display

*Main article: [[Loot on Display]]*

- Weapons and tools lie on the ground, hang on walls, stand stuck in the ground or are buried.
- Players and undead can take them.

## Grave Talisman and Totem of Warding

- The [[Grave Talisman]] and the [[Totem of Warding]] are part of [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]].
- With that mod installed, the talisman also senses sleeping undead and the totem calms them.

## Undead at dawn, and fire

*Main article: [[Sleeping Undead]]*

- Undead that were up during the night go back to sleep at sunrise in the nearest dark, covered spot.
- Lit campfires and fire keep idle monsters that fear fire a few blocks away.

## Shipwreck crew

*Main article: [[Shipwreck Crew]]*

- Shipwrecks hold their drowned crew asleep between decks.

## Will-o'-wisps and maps

*Main articles: [[Will-o'-Wisps]], [[Explorer Maps]]*

- Faint soul flames lead the player to graveyards not yet found.
- Cartographers sell maps to graveyards, monster camps and Ancient Fabricators.

## Trading with endermen

*Main articles: [[Trading at Cairns]], [[Placing Items Anywhere]]*

- Sneak + middle click lays a held item on the ground anywhere.
- An item laid on a cairn's trade pad gets a random answer from the endermen.

## Creepers

*Main article: [[Creepers]]*

- Creepers watch players from behind cover and explode only when startled repeatedly.
- Calm players are safe, and crouching during a hiss calms them.
- Each player has one watching creeper at night instead of random creeper spawns.

## Endermen

*Main article: [[Endermen]]*

- Endermen slowly put the overworld back the way it first generated.
- They build twisting purpur cairns, which point toward strongholds.
- They spawn from cairn sites instead of anywhere.

## Drowned

*Main article: [[Drowned]]*

- Rare, tough lurkers that wait in shallow water.
- They grab anyone who turns their back to the water and drag them under to drown.
- Hard, fast hits break their grip.

## Crypt yards and gravestones

*Main articles: [[Crypt Yards]], [[Gravestone]]*

- Fenced graveyards in every state of decay stand on dry, flat ground.
- Their graves hold skeletons that never wake.
- Many have a crypt below (a pauper's ossuary, a family vault or a noble crypt) whose sleepers do wake.
- Gravestones work like signs, and players can cut their own in a stonecutter.
- With [[Bubba's Infused Gems]] installed, the dead in noble crypts wear gemmed armor (see [[Noble Finery]]).

## Spiders

*Main article: [[Spiders]]*

- Spiders climb walls and ceilings.
- Cobweb nests cling to the ceilings of tall cave rooms. When a player walks underneath, a spider drops on a thread; if it lands on the player, it reels them up and cocoons them.
- Giant spiders roam old-growth taiga, webbing the trees and snatching livestock into their own webs.

## Monster camps

*Main articles: [[Monster Camps]], [[Camp Patrols]]*

- Dark camps of skeletons, zombies, husks, strays or bogged, each with a leader.
- Breaking every banner clears a camp for good; leaving one lets it refill; a lit fire quiets it.
- Armed camps send patrols over the land around them at night (see [[Camp Patrols]]).
- Bigger camps keep a stable of mounts.
- Chests by the fire, in the underground room, in winding towers and in skull rocks hold better loot, in that order.
- Pillager outposts stop spawning when their banners are gone.
- In the Nether, piglin brute outposts with hoglin pens and a portal room stand in crimson forests and the Nether wastes; bigger ones have a [[Boss Piglin]] on a throne.

## Trader posts

*Main article: [[Trader Posts]]*

- Small market squares with a striped stall, llama pens and a campfire, on plains, meadows, savannas and desert edges.
- The wandering trader arrives behind the counter of a nearby post, with its llamas tied up in the pens.

## Bogged

*Main article: [[Bogged]]*

- Bogged hide in swamp water and mud and ambush players who turn their back.

## Ancient Fabricators

*Main article: [[Ancient Fabricators]]*

- Beside every ancient city, an Ancient Fabricator drips monsters onto a teleporter that sends them to the surface.
- Players can farm the monsters on the teleporter, stop it with redstone, or get past its Warden and break its hidden spawner.

## Wither Captain and Soulfire Blazes

*Main articles: [[Wither Captain]], [[Soulfire Blaze]]*

- Rarely, a wither skeleton in a Nether fortress is a Wither Captain: a tougher mini-boss with a boss bar.
- It stops to summon blue Soulfire Blazes, taking little damage while it does.
- It drops Nether Star Shards (four craft a nether star), a wither skeleton skull about one time in three, and sometimes its horned Captain's Helm, enchanted with Soulguard.
- Soulfire Blazes also turn up rarely in soul sand valleys, and drop Soul Blaze Rods.

## Boss Piglin

*Main article: [[Boss Piglin]]*

- Medium and large Nether outposts have a throne where a huge, fat Boss Piglin in trimmed gold armor and a crimson cape sits while his brutes fight.
- Once they are dead he waddles off for the outpost's portal to escape with his gold, though he is too fat for the tunnel down to it.

## Soulfire and Soulguard

*Main articles: [[Soulfire]], [[Soulguard]], [[Soul Blaze Items]], [[Eye of the Soul]]*

- Soul fire, lit soul campfires and Soulfire charges set mobs and players burning with blue Soulfire.
- Soulfire hurts the living more than ordinary fire (undead take the normal amount), gets partly through Fire Resistance, and can't be stopped by a Totem of Undying.
- Soulguard (a potion, a treasure enchantment found in Nether chests, and a gem with Bubba's Infused Gems) protects fully.
- Soulfire Blazes drop Soul Blaze Rods, which make Soul Blaze Powder, Soul Fire Charges and the Potion of Soulguard.
- An Eye of the Soul, crafted from Soul Blaze Powder and an ender pearl, lets the player pass through blocks for 5 seconds, blinded and soul-burning unless they have Soulguard.

## Monster spawning

*Main article: [[Monster Spawning]]*

- Natural monster spawning is cut to 15 % of normal everywhere.
- Spawners and most structures are unchanged.

## Commands

*Main article: [[Reworked Mobs Commands]]*

- Game rules control the spawn cut, watcher creepers, cairn endermen and visible meters.
- Operator commands put items on display and find or build cairns, graveyards, camps, trader posts, Fabricators, spider nests and giant spiders.

## Installation

- Installs like any Fabric mod (see [[Installing Fabric Mods]]); needed on both the server and every player's game.
- Sleeping undead generate in [[Crypt Yards]] and their crypts.
- They are in the creative inventory under the **Bubba's Reworked Mobs** tab (a lying zombie is its own entry).
- Every feature can be tried in the [[Bubba Test World]].

## Textures

- The mod comes with vanilla-style 16x textures.
- Its 32x textures, in the style of Classic Faithful 32x Jappa, come in a separate resource pack, **Bubba's Mods 32x**, which covers all of Bubba's mods (see [[Installing Resource Packs]]).
- Put it above Classic Faithful 32x Jappa when using that pack.

## Compatibility

- Requires Fabric API and [Stormie's Spiders](https://modrinth.com/mod/stormies-spiders), which makes spiders climb walls.
- Only this mod's own undead are changed: normal zombies and skeletons from spawners and other mods behave as usual.
- Optional: [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]], whose [[Grave Talisman]] and [[Totem of Warding]] sense and calm this mod's sleeping undead, and whose [[Totem of Keeping]] does not work against Soulfire deaths without Soulguard.
- Optional: with [[Bubba's Infused Gems]], Soulguard potions brew a Soulguard gem.
- Everything here works the same without them.

**Bubba's Reworked Mobs** is a Fabric mod that reworks Minecraft's mobs one family at a time. Far fewer monsters spawn on their own; instead they come from places: undead lie dormant in graveyards and crypts until noise wakes them, spiders nest in caves, monster camps hold skeletons and zombies (and piglins in the Nether), and Ancient Fabricators beside ancient cities send monsters to the surface. In the Nether, rare Wither Captains lead fortress garrisons and summon Soulfire Blazes. Creepers are nervous watchers that explode only when startled, endermen mend the land and build cairns, and drowned and bogged lurk at the water's edge.

In this early test version, sleeping bodies use the normal mob look, and sleeper item icons are the matching mob heads.

## Sleeping undead

*Main article: [[Sleeping Undead]]*

Skeletons, zombies, husks, strays and drowned sit or lie motionless, often armed and armored. They can be robbed, dug up with a Silk Touch pickaxe, and placed again as traps or decoration.

## Waking

*Main article: [[Noise and Waking]]*

Noise near a sleeping undead adds up until it wakes. Sneaking and wool walls keep players quiet, and a waking undead can wake its neighbors.

## Woken undead

*Main article: [[Woken Undead]]*

Woken undead are tougher and slower than vanilla ones, never despawn, shamble or stagger, and arm themselves with loot lying nearby.

### Combat

*Main article: [[Fighting the Undead]]*

Repeated fist hits shred armor, Smite can destroy a woken undead in one hit, arrows do little to skeletons, and headshots do double damage to zombies.

## Loot on display

*Main article: [[Loot on Display]]*

Weapons and tools lie on the ground, hang on walls, stand stuck in the ground or are buried, and can be taken by players and by undead.

## Grave Talisman and Totem of Warding

The [[Grave Talisman]] and the [[Totem of Warding]] are part of [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]]. With that mod installed, the talisman also senses sleeping undead and the totem calms them. Worlds from an earlier version of this mod, where both items first appeared, need that mod to keep their talismans and totems.

## Undead at dawn, and fire

*Main article: [[Sleeping Undead]]*

Undead that were up during the night go back to sleep at sunrise in the nearest dark, covered spot. Lit campfires and fire keep idle monsters that fear fire a few blocks away.

## Shipwreck crew

*Main article: [[Shipwreck Crew]]*

Shipwrecks hold their drowned crew asleep between decks.

## Will-o'-wisps and maps

*Main articles: [[Will-o'-Wisps]], [[Explorer Maps]]*

Faint soul flames lead the player to graveyards not yet found, and cartographers sell maps to graveyards, monster camps and Ancient Fabricators.

## Trading with endermen

*Main articles: [[Trading at Cairns]], [[Placing Items Anywhere]]*

Sneak + middle click lays a held item on the ground anywhere. Lay one on a cairn's trade pad and the endermen answer with something random.

## Creepers

*Main article: [[Creepers]]*

Creepers watch players from behind cover and explode only when startled repeatedly; calm players are safe, and crouching during a hiss calms them. Each player has one watching creeper at night instead of random creeper spawns.

## Endermen

*Main article: [[Endermen]]*

Endermen slowly put the overworld back the way it first generated and build twisting purpur cairns, which point toward strongholds. They spawn from cairn sites instead of anywhere.

## Drowned

*Main article: [[Drowned]]*

Drowned are rare, tough lurkers that wait in shallow water, grab anyone who turns their back to the water, and drag them under to drown. Hard, fast hits break their grip.

## Crypt yards and gravestones

*Main articles: [[Crypt Yards]], [[Gravestone]]*

Fenced graveyards in every state of decay stand on dry, flat ground. Their graves hold skeletons that never wake. Many have a crypt below (a pauper's ossuary, a family vault or a noble crypt) whose sleepers do wake. Gravestones work like signs, and players can cut their own in a stonecutter.

With [[Bubba's Infused Gems]] installed, the dead in noble crypts wear gemmed armor (see [[Noble Finery]]).

## Spiders

*Main article: [[Spiders]]*

Spiders climb walls and ceilings. Cobweb nests cling to the ceilings of tall cave rooms. When a player walks underneath, a spider drops on a thread; if it lands on the player, it reels them up and cocoons them. Giant spiders roam old-growth taiga, webbing the trees and snatching livestock into their own webs.

## Monster camps

*Main articles: [[Monster Camps]], [[Camp Patrols]]*

Dark camps of skeletons, zombies, husks, strays or bogged, each with a leader. Breaking every banner clears a camp for good, leaving one lets it refill, and a lit fire quiets it. Armed camps send patrols over the land around them at night (see [[Camp Patrols]]); bigger camps keep a stable of mounts, and chests by the fire, in the underground room, in winding towers and in skull rocks hold better loot in that order. Pillager outposts stop spawning when their banners are gone. In the Nether, piglin outposts with hoglin pens and a portal room stand in crimson forests and the Nether wastes.

## Trader posts

*Main article: [[Trader Posts]]*

Small market squares with a striped stall, llama pens and a campfire on plains, meadows, savannas and desert edges. The wandering trader arrives behind the counter of a nearby post, with its llamas tied up in the pens.

## Bogged

*Main article: [[Bogged]]*

Bogged hide in swamp water and mud and ambush players who turn their back.

## Ancient Fabricators

*Main article: [[Ancient Fabricators]]*

Beside every ancient city, an Ancient Fabricator drips monsters onto a teleporter that sends them to the surface. Players can farm the monsters on the teleporter, stop it with redstone, or get past its Warden and break its hidden spawner.

## Wither Captain and Soulfire Blazes

*Main articles: [[Wither Captain]], [[Soulfire Blaze]]*

Rarely, a wither skeleton in a Nether fortress is a Wither Captain: a tougher mini-boss with a boss bar that stops to summon blue Soulfire Blazes, taking little damage while it does. It drops Nether Star Shards, four of which craft a nether star, and sometimes its horned Captain's Helm, which makes the wearer immune to soul fire. Soulfire Blazes also turn up rarely in soul sand valleys.

## Monster spawning

*Main article: [[Monster Spawning]]*

Natural monster spawning is cut to 15 % of normal everywhere. Spawners and most structures are unchanged.

## Commands

*Main article: [[Reworked Mobs Commands]]*

Game rules control the spawn cut, watcher creepers, cairn endermen and visible meters. Operator commands put items on display and find or build cairns, graveyards, camps, trader posts, Fabricators, spider nests and giant spiders.

## Installation

The mod installs like any Fabric mod (see [[Installing Fabric Mods]]) and is needed on both the server and every player's game.

Sleeping undead generate in [[Crypt Yards]] and their crypts. They are in the creative inventory under the **Bubba's Reworked Mobs** tab (a lying zombie is its own entry), and every feature can be tried in the [[Bubba Test World]].

## Textures

The mod comes with vanilla-style 16x textures. Its 32x textures, in the style of Classic Faithful 32x Jappa, come in a separate resource pack, **Bubba's Mods 32x**, which covers all of Bubba's mods (see [[Installing Resource Packs]]). Put it above Classic Faithful 32x Jappa if you use that pack.

## Compatibility

The mod requires Fabric API and [Stormie's Spiders](https://modrinth.com/mod/stormies-spiders), which makes spiders climb walls. Only this mod's own undead are changed: normal zombies and skeletons from spawners and other mods behave as usual.

Optional: [[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]], whose [[Grave Talisman]] and [[Totem of Warding]] sense and calm this mod's sleeping undead. Everything here works the same without it.

The **Eye of the Soul** is an item that lets the player walk through blocks for 5 seconds. Using it gives the player **Phasing**, a status effect during which almost every block can be passed through like scaffolding. The player is blinded and burns with [[Soulfire]] the whole time.

## Obtaining

| Name | Ingredients | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Eye of the Soul | [[Soul Blaze Items#Soul Blaze Powder|Soul Blaze Powder]] + Ender Pearl (shapeless) | 1 Eye of the Soul |

## Usage

Using the Eye of the Soul (right-click) uses one up and gives the player **Phasing** for 5 seconds, with soul particles and a soul sound. There is a 1 second cooldown between uses. Using another while already Phasing sets the time back to 5 seconds.

## Phasing

While Phasing, every block that can be broken behaves like scaffolding for the player:

- **Walking** carries the player straight through walls.
- **Floors** hold the player up: the player stands on top of blocks, at their real height, so slabs, stairs and fences are stood on as usual.
- **Sneaking** sinks the player down through the floor.
- **Jumping** (held) climbs the player up through blocks, at ladder speed.
- Inside blocks, movement is slow, as on a ladder or scaffolding. With no input, the player slides down until their feet reach the top of a block, and stands there, as in a scaffolding tower.

Blocks that cannot be broken stay solid: bedrock, barriers, end portal frames, command blocks and the like. Reinforced deepslate, which can be broken, does not.

Phasing only lets the player through blocks. A mob given Phasing by command still collides normally, and a vehicle the player rides still collides with blocks.

### Blindness

While Phasing, the player is blinded as by the Blindness effect, with a dark soul-teal fog and no sky. Nothing lifts this blindness: not Night Vision, not milk. Inside a block the view is covered completely, so Phasing cannot be used to see through the ground.

### Cost

The player burns with [[Soulfire]] for the whole time Phasing lasts. Water does not put it out while Phasing, and it stops when Phasing ends. A player with [[Soulguard]] does not burn at all, which makes Phasing free.

### Ending Phasing

Phasing cannot be ended early: milk, a Totem of Undying, honey and effect clears from other mods leave it in place. Only the `/effect clear` command removes it.

A player who is still inside blocks when Phasing ends is not pushed out and starts to suffocate, as in vanilla. While Phasing, the player takes no suffocation damage.

## Textures

The Eye of the Soul has a vanilla-style 16x texture, the Eye of Ender recoloured to soul teal with a white soul flame in the middle, and the Phasing effect icon is a dashed player-shaped outline. Both have 32x versions in **Bubba's Mods 32x**.

A **Soulfire Blaze** is a blue blaze that burns with soul fire, summoned by the [[Wither Captain]] and found, rarely, in soul sand valleys.

## Spawning

- A [[Wither Captain]] summons one to three at a time while it is summoning. These burn out in a puff of soul fire, with no drops, when their captain dies.
- Rarely, Soulfire Blazes spawn on their own in soul sand valleys, one at a time.

## Behavior

- Moves and fights like a blaze.
- Its fireballs, **soulfire charges**, look like [[Soul Blaze Items#Soul Fire Charge|Soul Fire Charges]] and hit half again as hard as a blaze's (7.5 instead of 5).
- A charge sets its target [[Soulfire|soul-burning]] for 5 seconds.
- Where a charge lands, it leaves soul fire on soul sand and soul soil, and ordinary fire everywhere else.
- Water and other fire rules work as for a blaze.
- A player with [[Soulguard]] (for example from the [[Wither Captain#Captain's Helm|Captain's Helm]]) is not set alight by soulfire charges, though the hit still hurts.

## Drops

- [[Soul Blaze Items#Soul Blaze Rod|Soul Blaze Rods]] instead of blaze rods, at a blaze's odds: 0 or 1 when killed by a player, with Looting adding up to 1 more.
- Blazes summoned by a captain that burn out when it dies drop nothing.

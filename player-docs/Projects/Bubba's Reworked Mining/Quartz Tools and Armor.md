**Quartz tools and armor** are a white quartz set made for the Nether.

- **Quartz Sword, Shovel, Pickaxe, Axe, Hoe, Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings and Boots**.

## Crafting

- Crafted like iron gear, with **nether quartz** where the iron ingots go (sticks as usual).
- A pickaxe takes 3 blocks, so 12 nether quartz.
- Repaired at an anvil with nether quartz.

## Strength

- About iron's level.
- Tools harvest what iron harvests (including diamond ore) and do iron's damage, with a little less durability (220 uses).
- They mine deepslate like iron does: slowly and with heavy wear.
- **Ancient debris needs diamond.**
- The armor protects like iron, lasts a little less, and sounds like iron when put on.

## In the Nether

- Quartz tools mine the Nether's natural blocks **1.6 times faster**:
  - netherrack;
  - nether bricks of every kind, and nether brick slabs, stairs, walls and fences;
  - basalt, blackstone and gilded blackstone;
  - soul sand and soul soil, nylium, magma blocks, wart blocks;
  - glowstone and shroomlight;
  - nether quartz ore and nether gold ore;
  - crimson and warped stems and hyphae (stripped too);
  - bone blocks.
- Obsidian and ancient debris get no bonus.

## Textures

- Bright quartz white with faint rose-grey veins.
- A 32x version comes in the **Bubba's Mods 32x** resource pack.

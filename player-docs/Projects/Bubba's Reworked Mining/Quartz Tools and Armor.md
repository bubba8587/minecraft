A white quartz set made for the Nether: **Quartz Sword, Shovel, Pickaxe, Axe, Hoe, Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings and Boots**.

## Crafting

Craft them like iron gear, with **blocks of quartz** where the iron ingots go (sticks as usual). A pickaxe takes 3 blocks, so 12 nether quartz. Repair them at an anvil with blocks of quartz.

## Strength

Quartz is about iron's level. Its tools harvest what iron harvests (including diamond ore) and do iron's damage, with a little less durability (220 uses). They mine deepslate like iron does, slowly and with heavy wear. **Ancient debris still needs diamond.** The armor protects like iron, lasts a little less, and sounds like iron when you put it on.

## In the Nether

Quartz tools mine the Nether's natural blocks **1.6 times faster**: netherrack, nether bricks and their slabs, stairs, walls and fences, basalt, blackstone, soul sand and soil, nylium, magma, wart blocks, glowstone, shroomlight, nether quartz and gold ore, crimson and warped stems, and bone blocks.

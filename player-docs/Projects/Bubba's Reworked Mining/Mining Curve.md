# Mining Curve

*Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mining]].*

The **mining curve** is how fast each tool tier mines and how quickly it wears out in [[Bubba's Reworked Mining]]. Every tier mines at its own pace, with stone and deepslate as the steep part of the curve.

## Pickaxes on stone and deepslate

| Tool | Stone | Deepslate | Wear per deepslate block |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wood | about 3x slower than vanilla, 2 durability per block | breaks slowly and drops nothing | 3 |
| Stone | about 1.5x slower | about 8x slower | 3 |
| Copper | as vanilla | about 2.5x slower | 4 |
| Iron | as vanilla | about 2.5x slower | 3 |
| Gold | about 1.5x slower | breaks slowly and drops nothing | 3 |
| Diamond | as vanilla | as vanilla | 1 |
| Netherite | 1.2x faster | 1.2x faster | 1 |

An iron pickaxe takes about 1.9 seconds per deepslate block (vanilla 0.75 s) and lasts about 83 deepslate blocks without Unbreaking, or about 580 with Unbreaking III.

"Stone" here means stone, cobblestone, mossy cobblestone, granite, diorite, andesite, tuff and their ores. "Deepslate" means deepslate, cobbled deepslate and the deepslate ores. Bricks, polished and other crafted versions follow the gentler curve below.

## Other tools and blocks

Axes, shovels, hoes, and pickaxes on other blocks follow a gentler curve: wood and stone tools are a little slower than vanilla, iron and up are as vanilla, and netherite is a little faster. Swords wear as in vanilla.

## Enchantments and Haste

- **Efficiency** multiplies the tool's own speed: x1.5, 2.1, 2.8, 3.5 and 4.25 at levels I to V. An Efficiency V iron pickaxe beats a bare diamond one on deepslate; Efficiency V diamond is as fast as in vanilla.
- **Unbreaking** keeps a durability point with odds 1 in 3, 1 in 5 and 1 in 7 at levels I to III. Armor gets the same extra protection.
- **[[True Unbreaking]]**, found only in rare chests, stops all durability loss.
- **Haste**, from potions, beacons or [[Bubba's Infused Gems]] Haste gems, multiplies on top.

Tools from other mods stay vanilla.

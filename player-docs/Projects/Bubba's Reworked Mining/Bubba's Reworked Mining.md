# Bubba's Reworked Mining

> [!infobox] Bubba's Reworked Mining
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Type** | Fabric mod |
> | **Minecraft** | 26.3 |
> | **Needed on** | Server and every player's game |

**Bubba's Reworked Mining** makes tools a real progression, from "tough" to "a highly powered machine". Bare wood and stone tools are slow and wear out fast, deepslate is hard going until diamond, and Efficiency and Unbreaking become worth chasing. It also adds rare finds in plain stone, the **True Unbreaking** enchantment, and a quartz tool and armor set for the Nether.

## The mining curve

*Main article: [[Mining Curve]]*

Wood barely gets anywhere and can't mine deepslate at all. Stone is a bit slower on stone and incredibly slow on deepslate. Copper and iron are about normal on stone but slow on deepslate, and it wears them down fast. Diamond mines deepslate as in vanilla, and netherite is faster than vanilla. Axes, shovels and hoes follow a gentler version of the same idea.

## Enchantments

Efficiency multiplies a tool's own speed, so an Efficiency V iron pickaxe mines deepslate a little faster than a bare diamond one. Unbreaking is stronger: at level III a tool loses about 1 durability in 7.

**True Unbreaking** is a new enchantment: the item never loses durability. It is found only as an enchanted book in ancient city, End city, bastion treasure and ominous trial vault chests. It can't be combined with Mending.

## Rare finds

*Main article: [[Rare Finds]]*

Mining stone, granite, diorite, andesite, tuff or deepslate sometimes drops nuggets, and very rarely lapis, an emerald, or (from deepslate) a diamond.

## Quartz gear

*Main article: [[Quartz Tools and Armor]]*

A white quartz set, about iron's strength, that mines the Nether's blocks much faster.

## Installation

The mod installs like any Fabric mod (see [[Installing Fabric Mods]]) and is needed on both the server and every player's game. It includes a built-in 32x resource pack.

## Compatibility

[[Bubba's Infused Gems]]: Haste gems speed mining up on top of everything here, like the Haste effect.

# Rare Finds

*Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mining]].*

**Rare finds** are extra items that mining plain stone sometimes turns up on top of the block.

| Mined block | Chance per block | What drops |
|---|---|---|
| Stone, granite, diorite, andesite, tuff | 0.5 % | iron nuggets (1-3) most often, gold nuggets, rarely lapis, very rarely an emerald |
| Deepslate | 0.8 % | the same, slightly better, and a tiny chance of a diamond |

Each level of **Fortune** doubles the chance: with Fortune III it is 4 % for stone and 6.4 % for deepslate. **Silk Touch** never gives finds. Explosions can give finds at the no-Fortune chance. Cobblestone, calcite and ores give none.

A bare pickaxe finds a diamond about once per 12,500 deepslate blocks.

# Rare Finds

*Part of [[Bubba's Reworked Mining]].*

**Rare finds** are extra items that mining plain stone sometimes turns up on top of the block.

| Mined block | Chance per block | What drops |
|---|---|---|
| Stone, granite, diorite, andesite, tuff | 0.5 % | 1-3 iron nuggets most often, then 1-3 gold nuggets; rarely lapis; very rarely an emerald |
| Deepslate | 0.8 % | the same with slightly better odds, plus a tiny chance of a diamond |

- Each level of **Fortune** doubles the chance: with Fortune III it is 4 % for stone and 6.4 % for deepslate.
- **Silk Touch** never gives finds.
- Explosions can give finds, at the chance without Fortune.
- Placed blocks give finds just like natural ones.
- Cobblestone, calcite and ores give none.
- A bare pickaxe finds a diamond about once per 12,500 deepslate blocks.

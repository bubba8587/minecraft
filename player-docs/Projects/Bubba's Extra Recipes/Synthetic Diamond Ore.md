# Synthetic Diamond Ore

*Part of [[Bubba's Extra Recipes]].*

> [!infobox] Synthetic Diamond Ore

**Synthetic Diamond Ore** is deepslate diamond ore made in a blast furnace from a block of coal.

## Obtaining

*Main article: [[Recipe List]]*

| Ingredient | Result | Time | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| Block of coal | Synthetic Diamond Ore | 200 s | 5 |

## Usage

- **Datapack alone:** places and mines like normal deepslate diamond ore.
- **With the [[Extra Recipes Companion Mod]]**, placed Synthetic Diamond Ore behaves as synthetic:
  - When mined, it drops a diamond about 1 time in 4 and otherwise nothing.
  - Fortune has no effect, and it gives no experience.
  - Mining it with Silk Touch drops the Synthetic Diamond Ore item, so it can be moved.
  - Diamond ore generated in the world keeps its usual diamonds, Fortune and experience.
  - If the mod is removed from a world, placed Synthetic Diamond Ore turns into ordinary diamond ore and drops real diamonds.

**Synthetic Diamond Ore** is deepslate diamond ore made in a blast furnace from a block of coal. With the datapack alone it behaves like normal diamond ore; with the [[Extra Recipes Companion Mod]] it is much less generous.

## Obtaining

*Main article: [[Extra Recipes Recipes]]*

| Ingredient | Result | Time | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| Block of coal | Synthetic Diamond Ore | 200 s | 5 |

## Usage

**With the datapack alone,** Synthetic Diamond Ore places and mines like normal deepslate diamond ore.

**With the [[Extra Recipes Companion Mod]],** placed Synthetic Diamond Ore behaves as synthetic:

- When mined, it drops a diamond about 1 time in 4 and otherwise drops nothing. Fortune has no effect, and it gives no experience.
- Mining it with Silk Touch drops the Synthetic Diamond Ore item, so it can be moved.
- Diamond ore generated in the world is unchanged: it keeps its usual diamonds, Fortune and experience.
- If the mod is removed from a world, placed Synthetic Diamond Ore turns back into ordinary diamond ore and drops real diamonds.

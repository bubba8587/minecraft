# Extra Recipes Recipes

*Part of [[Bubba's Extra Recipes]].*

**Extra Recipes recipes** are the crafting, blasting and smoking recipes added by [[Bubba's Extra Recipes]]. None of them replaces a vanilla recipe. Each appears in the recipe book once the player has picked up one of its ingredients.

## Crafting

| Name | Ingredients | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Bamboo Paper | 3 bamboo in a horizontal row | 2 Bamboo Paper |
| Black Dye | 1 coal or charcoal (shapeless) | 1 black dye |
| Charcoal Block | 9 charcoal (shapeless) | 1 block of coal, named "Charcoal Block" |
| Trident | 3 prismarine shards, 4 prismarine crystals, 2 sticks (pattern below) | 1 trident |

### Trident pattern

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| Prismarine Shard | Prismarine Shard | Prismarine Shard |
| Prismarine Crystals | Stick | Prismarine Crystals |
| Prismarine Crystals | Stick | Prismarine Crystals |

### Bamboo Paper

Bamboo Paper works anywhere paper does. With the pack's resource pack it is light green; without it, it looks like plain paper.

## Blasting

All of these are made in a blast furnace.

| Name | Ingredient | Result | Time | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sand | Gravel or glass | Sand | 10 s | 0 |
| Gravel | Cobblestone | Gravel | 10 s | 0 |
| Quartz | Andesite, diorite or granite | Nether quartz | 10 s | 0.1 |
| Iron Nugget | Stone | Iron nugget | 25 s | 0.1 |
| [[Synthetic Diamond Ore]] | Block of coal | Deepslate diamond ore, named "Synthetic Diamond Ore" | 200 s | 5 |

With the datapack alone, Synthetic Diamond Ore mines like normal diamond ore. With the [[Extra Recipes Companion Mod]] it rarely drops a diamond; see [[Synthetic Diamond Ore]].

## Smoking

All of these are made in a smoker.

| Name | Ingredient | Result | Time | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Organic Glowstone Dust | Glow berries, glow lichen or a glow ink sac | Glowstone dust, named "Organic Glowstone Dust" | 10 s | 0.1 |
| Cured leather | Rotten flesh | Leather, with the lore "Cured zombie flesh. Gross..." | 15 s | 0.1 |

The **Glider** is a craftable, weaker relative of the elytra. Worn in the chest slot, it lets the player glide like an elytra, but it turns more slowly and gets less speed from firework rockets. It wears out while gliding, like an elytra, and is repaired with phantom membrane.

## Obtaining

### Crafting

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| Diamond | Bamboo | Diamond |
| Phantom Membrane | | Phantom Membrane |
| Feather | | Feather |

The recipe makes one Glider.

## Usage

The Glider is worn in the chest slot. Jumping while falling starts gliding, exactly as with an elytra.

### Flight

| | Glider | Elytra |
|---|---|---|
| Gliding down and lift | Same as the elytra | Normal |
| Turning toward where the player looks | Under a third as fast | Normal |
| Top speed with firework rockets | A little over half the elytra's | Normal |
| A rocket fired while diving faster than that | Keeps your speed (never slows you) | Normal |
| Durability | 324; wears out while gliding, repaired with phantom membrane | 432; repaired with phantom membrane |

### Durability

The Glider wears out by the elytra's own rules:

- It loses 1 durability per second of gliding: about 5 minutes 24 seconds of gliding from new (the elytra gets 7 minutes 12 seconds).
- At 1 durability left it stops gliding but never breaks: the player drops out of the glide and falls, exactly as with a worn-out elytra. Repair it to fly again.
- Taking hits while wearing it doesn't wear it.

### Repairing

- At an anvil with phantom membrane; each membrane restores a quarter of its durability.
- By combining two Gliders in a crafting grid or grindstone.

### Enchantments

The Glider takes Mending, Unbreaking, Curse of Binding and Curse of Vanishing from enchanted books at an anvil. Like the elytra, it can't be enchanted at an enchanting table. Unbreaking works as on the elytra: with Unbreaking III it wears about a quarter as fast.

## Appearance

The Glider looks like a home-made hang glider: phantom membrane stretched over a bamboo frame, with diamond caps on the spar ends and feathers on the trim. Worn, it shows as a pair of membrane sails on the player's back. The textures are temporary and will be redrawn; a 32x version comes in the **Bubba's Mods 32x** resource pack.

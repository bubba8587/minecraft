# Gem Effects

*Part of [[Bubba's Infused Gems]].*

**Gem effects** are the permanent, partial potion effects that socketed Gems give: to the wearer from armor, and to the target from weapons.

## Behavior

Any Gem fits armor or a weapon; **where it is set decides who gets its effects**. In armor, the wearer gets them all the time while wearing it. In a weapon, whatever the weapon hits gets them. Bonus effects and drawbacks follow the same rule, so a Poison gem in armor poisons its wearer, and a Regeneration gem on a sword heals the target. Mobs wearing gemmed armor or holding gemmed weapons are affected the same way.

### Strength

Gem effects are always weaker than a real potion. The best possible build reaches 95% of the strongest potion level, and only one build does: twelve perfect gems of one effect in a full set of netherite armor (or two in a netherite weapon).

- **Stacking:** gems of the same effect add up, each extra one counting a little less than the one before.
- **Material:** the same gem is stronger in better armor or weapons, from leather and wood up to netherite.
- **Matching set:** armor pieces of the same material next to each other (helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots) add strength. A matching helmet and chestplate beat a matching helmet and boots; a full matching set is best.
- **Drawbacks** are small and fixed, about a tenth of the effect's strongest potion level. Several of the same drawback add up a little, but stay weaker than the weakest real potion.

The `/gems` command shows the player's combined totals. *Main article: [[Infused Gems Commands]]*

## In armor

| Gem | Effect on the wearer |
|---|---|
| Speed | Faster movement (up to +38%) |
| Strength | More attack damage (up to +5.7) |
| Jump Boost | Higher jumps and a longer safe fall |
| Haste | Faster mining and attacks (found gems only) |
| Regeneration | Slow healing when hurt |
| Resistance (Turtle Master) | Less damage taken (up to 76% less) |
| Fire Resistance | Less fire and lava damage, and fire goes out sooner |
| Water Breathing | Air lasts longer underwater |
| Night Vision | The world is partly lit in the dark (fully at the very best) |
| Slow Falling | Slower falling and less fall damage |
| Healing | Each survived hit or fall returns part of the lost health (up to 25%) |
| Absorption | A shield of extra health that refills 30 seconds after the last hit |
| Luck | A little more luck (found gems only) |

Harmful gems in armor hurt the wearer: Poison (never below 1 health), Wither, Slowness, Weakness and Harming (more damage from each hit taken), plus the drawbacks Hunger, Mining Fatigue and Bad Luck.

## In weapons

Each hit gives the target a short, partial effect, refreshed by every hit; a stronger gem gives a stronger and longer effect. It appears in the target's effects as a percentage, for example *Poison 46%*.

| Gem | Effect on the target |
|---|---|
| Poison, Wither, Slowness, Weakness | The harmful effect, partial, for a few seconds |
| Speed, Strength, Jump Boost, Haste, Regeneration, Resistance, Luck | The helpful effect on the target (allowed, but pointless) |
| Harming | Extra magic damage on the first hit, then recharges for 10 seconds |
| Healing | Heals the target on the first hit, then recharges for 10 seconds |

As with potions, Healing and Harming are reversed on undead mobs.

### Bows and crossbows

Bows and crossbows with gems turn every arrow they fire into a tipped arrow with the gems' effects. The arrow flies and trails like a tipped arrow, but picked up again it is a plain arrow. When a real tipped arrow of the same effect is fired, the stronger effect applies; they do not add up.

# Gem Effects

*Part of [[Bubba's Infused Gems]].*

**Gem effects** are the permanent potion effects that socketed Gems give: to the wearer from armor, and to the target from weapons. Every gem effect works in both.

## Behavior

Any Gem fits armor or a weapon; **where it is set decides who gets its effects**. In armor, the wearer gets them all the time while wearing it. In a weapon, whatever the weapon hits gets them. Bonus effects and drawbacks follow the same rule, so a Poison gem in armor poisons its wearer, and a Regeneration gem on a sword heals the target. Mobs wearing gemmed armor or holding gemmed weapons are affected the same way.

### Strength

Gems below the best are weaker than a real potion. The best possible build is twelve perfect gems of one effect in a full set of netherite armor (or two in a netherite weapon), and only that build reaches the top, so a perfect set is worth chasing. In armor it gives the full strongest potion permanently for fighting effects (Strength, Resistance, Turtle Master, Regeneration, Health Boost, Absorption); it makes on/off effects complete (Fire Resistance and Water Breathing become the real effect, and Night Vision, Conduit Power and Invisibility work fully); and it goes past any potion for movement effects (Haste, Speed, Jump Boost, Dolphin's Grace, Slow Falling) and Luck. Harmful gems in armor stay at 90%. A perfect weapon hits with the strongest potion's level.

- **Stacking:** gems of the same effect add up, each extra one counting a little less than the one before.
- **Base gem:** the gem's Gem Power level (its base gem) matters most: a full iron set of amethyst gems reaches about a third of the best, diamond-base gems in diamond armor about 70%, and prismarine-base gems in netherite 88%.
- **Material:** the same gem is stronger in better armor or weapons, from leather and wood up to netherite. Gold counts like diamond, for its strong enchanting. Bows, crossbows, tridents and maces count like diamond.
- **Matching set:** armor pieces of the same material next to each other (helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots) add strength. A matching helmet and chestplate beat a matching helmet and boots; a full matching set is best.
- **Drawbacks** are small and fixed, about a tenth of the effect's strongest potion level. Several of the same drawback add up a little, but stay weaker than the weakest real potion.

### Gems and potions together

A gem and a potion (or beacon, or other source) of the same effect do not add up: the stronger one applies. A Speed gem set worn while a Speed II potion is active does nothing extra; under a Speed I potion it only adds the part above Speed I. The same goes for an effect a weapon gem puts on a target that already has the real effect.

### Undead

As with potions, Healing and Harming are reversed on undead, in armor and on weapons: a Healing gem worn by a zombie makes hits on it hurt more, and a Harming weapon heals undead targets. Undead ignore Poison and Regeneration gems, spiders ignore Poison, and wither skeletons ignore Wither.

The `/gems` command shows the player's combined totals. *Main article: [[Infused Gems Commands]]*

## Gems beyond potions

Some gems do what their source does, rather than apply its potion.

| Gem | Source | In armor | In a weapon |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wind Burst | Wind Charged potion | A fall's landing bounces the wearer with a small burst of wind (always at least a visible hop), and the fall hurts less (not at all with the best build) | Throws the target up and away, like a wind charge; on a mace with the Wind Burst enchantment, much further than any other weapon can |
| Weaving | Weaving potion | Walks through cobwebs faster; at the best build cobwebs don't hold the wearer at all: walking, sprinting, jumping and falling are as in open air | A chance to web the target in place with a real cobweb (only placed into empty space, never replacing a block) |
| Oozing | Oozing potion | Bounces off landings like a slime block, and falls hurt less (not at all with the best build) | A kill splits off small slimes that belong to the wielder: they never attack anyone and drop nothing; at most 8 near the player at a time; they stay while the player is around, and slimes left behind dissolve when their area unloads |
| Silence | Echo shard | The wearer's footsteps, swimming, landing and other movement make no vibrations (sculk sensors, wardens), some of the time with weak gems and always at the best build | Silences the target (pointless) |
| Ender | Chorus fruit | A chance that a projectile about to hurt the wearer teleports them away instead (not harmless ones like snowballs, and never explosions), like chorus fruit; stronger gems teleport further, and the perfect set always tries, but a wearer boxed in with nowhere safe to go is hit. Other damage still hurts | Gives it to the target (pointless) |
| Breeze | Breeze rod | A chance to deflect incoming projectiles, like a breeze: about half the time at 90% of the best, every time with the perfect set. Wind charges get through, as they do with a real breeze | Gives it to the target (pointless) |

Gem effects look the same as the real effect: poisoned or withered hearts, the green food bar of Hunger, the healing heart wave of Regeneration, the effect's icon and particles.

## In weapons

Each hit gives the target an effect for a few seconds, refreshed (never added up) by every hit. Each effect has one thing that grows with the gem's strength. Damage and weakening effects (Poison, Wither, Slowness, Weakness, Mining Fatigue, Hunger) get **stronger**: the best weapon matches a Poison II or Slowness IV potion; they last 4 seconds with a weak gem up to 8 seconds with the best. Screen effects (Blindness, Darkness, Nausea, Glowing) last **longer**. Levitation lifts **faster and longer**. Weaving and Ender work by **chance**. The effect appears in the target's effects as a percentage, for example *Poison 46%*. Mobs and players are affected the same way.

| Gem | Effect on the target |
|---|---|
| Poison, Wither, Slowness, Weakness, Mining Fatigue | The harmful effect, partial |
| Hunger | Drains food twice as fast as Hunger III; every hit costs a player at least half a drumstick |
| Blindness, Darkness, Infested | The effect itself, for longer the stronger the gem (Blindness 2 to 8 seconds) |
| Nausea | The effect itself: 4 seconds with a weak gem, 6 with a good one, 10 with the best |
| Glowing | The effect itself: 10 seconds with a weak gem, 30 with a good one, 60 with the best |
| Levitation | The target floats up: slowly for 3 seconds with a weak gem (0.3 blocks a second), faster and longer with stronger ones, up to 1.8 blocks a second for 6 seconds with the best (twice a shulker bullet's speed). Coming down hurts |
| Wind Burst | Throws the target up and away like a wind charge; even a weak gem throws it about half a block |
| Weaving | A chance to web the target with a real cobweb: 10% with a weak gem, 25% with a good one, 50% with the best, which drops 2 to 3 webs around it |
| Ender | A chance to teleport the target away like chorus fruit (up to 8 blocks, onto a floor): 10% with a weak gem, 25% with a good one, 40% with the best. A mob that gets teleported is confused for a second and forgets who it was fighting. Works on players too (they are only teleported) |
| Breeze | For a few seconds everything the target shoots or throws flies off course (arrows, crossbow bolts and fireworks, tridents, snowballs, witches' potions, llama spit, blaze and ghast fireballs, wither skulls, wind charges), and shulker bullets wobble while they home in: a little with a weak gem, most of them miss with the best |
| Harming | A little extra magic damage on every hit (up to 2.25) |
| Healing | Heals the target a little on every hit (up to 1.8) |
| Turtle Master | Its Resistance and Slowness together |
| Any other helpful gem | The helpful effect on the target (allowed, but pointless) |

| Weapon | Poison | Slowness |
|---|---|---|
| Iron sword, one weak gem | 0.51 health per second for 4 s | 18% slower for 4 s |
| Diamond sword, two good splash gems | 1.10 health per second for 5.3 s | 40% slower for 5.3 s |
| Netherite sword, two perfect gems (the best) | 1.67 health per second for 8 s | 60% slower for 8 s |

Swords, axes, spears, maces and tridents apply their gems on every hit, including sweeps and thrown tridents.

### Bows and crossbows

Bows and crossbows with gems turn every arrow they fire into a tipped arrow with the gems' effects. The arrow flies and trails like a tipped arrow, but picked up again it is a plain arrow; an arrow in flight keeps its gems when the world is saved. When a real tipped arrow of the same effect is fired, the stronger effect applies; they do not add up.

## In beacons

*See also: [[Beacon]]*

With [[Bubba's Reworked Beacons]] installed, a finished Gem can be placed in a beacon's item slot. It stays in the beacon and can be taken out again.

- The beacon gives the gem's effects, as if worn, to **every living thing in its area**: players and mobs, including the player who built it. A harmful gem makes a trap for everyone; only the player's own gems protect against it.
- Everything on the gem counts: its main effect, its bonus and its drawback.
- The pyramid limits the gem's Gem Power: 1 layer Gem Power I, 2 layers up to III, 3 layers up to V, 4 layers VI. A stronger gem works at the limit, and the beacon screen says *Only outputting Gem Power N*. The screen shows the gem's own tooltip lines, for example *Speed (100%)*.
- A perfect gem in a full 4-layer beacon is worth half a perfect armor set of that gem.
- A beacon and the player's own armor (or another beacon, or a potion) of the same effect do not add up: the stronger one applies.
- The effect lasts a little longer than the beacon's pulse (9 seconds plus 2 per pyramid layer, as in vanilla), so it fades soon after leaving the area.
- With this mod installed, the beacon's plain payments (an amethyst shard for Speed and so on) no longer work; only finished Gems and other mods' beacon items, such as the [[Totem of Warding]], fit the slot.

| Gem (perfect, 4 layers) | Effect in range |
|---|---|
| Speed | +37.5% speed (Speed II is +40%) |
| Strength | +3 attack damage (Strength I) |
| Resistance | 20% less damage (Resistance I) |
| Regeneration | 0.4 health per second (Regeneration I) |
| Haste | +50% mining speed, +25% attack speed |
| Fire Resistance | Half fire damage; fire goes out twice as fast |
| Breeze, Ender | 50% chance to deflect or teleport away from a projectile |
| Saturation | 1 food point every 5.3 seconds, up to 13 food |
| Poison (harmful) | 0.75 health per second, never below 1 health, for everyone in range |
| Slowness (harmful) | 27% slower, for everyone in range |

## Found gems

Found-only gems (Haste, Resistance, Health Boost, Hero of the Village, Mining Fatigue) and most Luck gems come from loot. Mobs that spawn in gemmed armor get its effects. *Main article: [[Finding Gems]]*

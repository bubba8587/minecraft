# Making Infused Gems

*Part of [[Bubba's Infused Gems]].*

> [!info] Infused Gem
> **Obtained by:** brewing
> **Stackable:** yes (16)
> **Used for:** [[Enchanting Gems|enchanting]] into a Gem

An **Infused Gem** is a gem brewed with a potion in a brewing stand, carrying that potion's effect.

## Obtaining

### Brewing

The player places a potion in a bottle slot of a brewing stand and a **base gem** in the ingredient slot, with blaze powder as fuel. As with any brew, one base gem infuses all three bottle slots.

| Bottle slot | Ingredient | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Potion (normal, splash or lingering) | Base gem | Infused Gem of that effect and form |
| Golden apple | Base gem | Infused Gem: Absorption (normal form) |
| Enchanted golden apple | Base gem | Stronger Infused Gem: Absorption (normal form) |
| Wither rose | Base gem | Infused Gem: Wither (normal form) |
| Infused Gem (normal form) | Gunpowder | The same Infused Gem in splash form |
| Infused Gem (splash form) | Dragon's breath | The same Infused Gem in lingering form |

### Base gems

The base gem sets the gem's **Infusion level**, which affects strength only.

| Infusion | Base gem |
|---|---|
| I | Amethyst shard |
| II | Nether quartz |
| III | Ender pearl |
| IV | Diamond |
| V | Prismarine shard |
| VI | Nether star |

## Properties

- **Effect**: from the potion or item used.
- **Potion strength**: a strong (glowstone) potion makes a stronger gem, a long (redstone) potion a slightly stronger one.
- **Form**: normal, splash or lingering. The form sets the highest enchanting tier: normal reaches tier 1, splash tier 2, lingering tier 3. *Main article: [[Enchanting Gems]]*

The item name shows these, for example *Infused Gem: Infusion III, Speed (Splash)*.

## Supported potions

Swiftness, Strength, Leaping, Regeneration, Turtle Master (gives Resistance), Fire Resistance, Water Breathing, Night Vision, Slow Falling, Healing, Poison, Slowness, Weakness and Harming, plus the golden apples and the wither rose. Invisibility, Luck, Wind Charged, Weaving, Oozing and Infested, and the item sources for other effects, are planned for a later version.

## Notes

- A hopper on the side of a brewing stand also pushes golden apples, wither roses and Infused Gems into the bottle slots.

# Enchanting Gems

*Part of [[Bubba's Infused Gems]].*

> [!infobox] Gem
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Obtained by** | Enchanting an Infused Gem |
> | **Stackable** | Yes (16) |
> | **Used for** | [[Socketing Gems\|socketing]] into armor and weapons |

**Enchanting** an Infused Gem at an enchanting table turns it into a finished **Gem**, rolling its final effects by tier.

## Usage

The player places an Infused Gem and lapis lazuli in an enchanting table. The three rows are three **tiers**. Each costs 1, 2 or 3 levels and lapis lazuli as in normal enchanting, and bookshelves around the table raise the level requirement the same way.

Rows above the gem's form are disabled:

| Infused Gem form | Tiers available |
|---|---|
| Normal | 1 |
| Splash | 1, 2 |
| Lingering | 1, 2, 3 |

Hovering over a row shows a hint of the result, like the enchantment clue for gear. The offered results stay the same until the player enchants something, so enchanting a cheap item first gives new rolls, as in vanilla.

## Results

| Tier | Result | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Weak version of the effect | 50% |
| 1 | Weak drawback only (a *Flawed Gem*) | 50% |
| 2 | Medium version of the effect | 50% |
| 2 | Medium, plus a weak bonus effect | 50% |
| 3 | Strongest version | 40% |
| 3 | Strongest, plus a weak bonus effect | 30% |
| 3 | Strongest, plus a medium bonus effect | 15% |
| 3 | Strongest, plus a weak drawback | 15% |

- A **bonus effect** is a different effect of the same kind: a helpful gem gets another helpful effect, a harmful gem another harmful one.
- A **drawback** is a mild effect of the opposite kind, never the gem's direct opposite: a Speed gem can get Hunger or Weakness, but never Slowness.

The Gem's tooltip shows each effect with its strength as a percentage of a perfect gem. A perfect gem (100%) requires the strongest potion, lingering form, a nether star and tier 3.

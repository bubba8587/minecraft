**Lava bubbles and geysers** are a feature of [[Bubba's World Tweaks]]: lava with magma blocks under it bubbles, erupts now and then as a smoking geyser, and lifts players who fly over it.

## Lava bubbles

- Wherever a magma block lies under lava (up to four blocks down), the surface above it bubbles.
- Bubbles are slow and sparse. Each one is a swelling of the lava itself, in lava's own colours, that rises for a few seconds and then bursts with a goopy plop.
- The Nether already has magma all along its lava seas, so their shores and shallows bubble on their own.

## Lava geysers

- Every magma block under lava is a vent with its own steady rhythm.
- A vent stays quiet for between about 80 seconds and three minutes, always the same for that vent, and a little longer under deeper lava.
- Then it erupts for about three seconds, throwing a tall column of black smoke and sparks with a low rumble.
- A player who watches a vent once can time the next eruption.
- Vents only erupt near players.

## Riding the updraft

- Standing, falling or gliding over an erupting lava geyser lifts the player, the way sulfur geysers do.
- Over an erupting lava geyser or sulfur geyser the player also picks up speed, so circling one climbs and speeds the player up.
- It works on foot, with an elytra, or with the [[The Glider|Glider]] from [[Bubba's Extra Recipes]].
- Spectators and flying creative players aren't moved.

## Lava bubbles

Wherever a magma block lies under lava (up to four blocks down), the surface above it bubbles. Bubbles are slow and sparse: now and then a swelling of the lava itself, in lava's own colours, rises for a few seconds, then bursts with a goopy plop. The Nether already has magma all along its lava seas, so their shores and shallows bubble on their own.

## Lava geysers

Now and then, a bubbling spot near a player erupts for about five seconds, throwing a tall column of black smoke and sparks with a low rumble.

## Riding the updraft

Standing, falling or gliding over an erupting lava geyser lifts you, the way sulfur geysers do. Over an erupting lava geyser or sulfur geyser you also pick up speed, so circling one climbs and speeds you up. It works on foot, with an elytra, or with the Glider from [[Bubba's Extra Recipes]]. Spectators and flying creative players aren't moved.

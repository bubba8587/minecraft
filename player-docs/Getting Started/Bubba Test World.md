# Bubba Test World

A ready-made creative world with a station for every feature:

- a graveyard of [[Sleeping Undead|sleeping undead]] (with a wool pen and a crypt);
- [[Loot on Display|loot on display]];
- a combat arena with gear chests;
- the [[Crafting Tweaks Recipes|Crafting Tweaks recipes]] with all their ingredients;
- a tall tower for trying [[The Glider]] against the elytra;
- a gem workshop for Bubba's Infused Gems: potions in all three forms, every base gem, a brewing stand, an enchanting table with a full set of bookshelves, a smithing table, a grindstone, armor and weapons, and a pen of mobs that stand still to hit.

It's always night, nothing spawns on its own, and signs at the spawn point say where everything is. Keep-inventory is on, so dying in the arena costs nothing.

It appears in the "mod testing" profile's world list after an install. Your copy is yours to wreck; installing again never overwrites it. To get a fresh one, install with the reset option (your old copy is kept under a new name).

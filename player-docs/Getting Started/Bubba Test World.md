# Bubba Test World

This tutorial describes the **Bubba Test World**, a ready-made creative world with a station for every feature in this guide, and how to get a fresh copy of it.

## Getting the world

The world appears in the world list of the "mod testing" profile after an install. The installed copy belongs to the player: installing again never overwrites it. To get a fresh copy, install with the reset option, which writes over the existing copy; nothing from the old copy is kept.

## World settings

- Always night; clear weather.
- Nothing spawns on its own; each station spawns what it needs.
- Keep-inventory is on, so dying in the combat arena costs nothing.
- Signs at the spawn point say where each station is.

## Stations

| Station | Contents |
|---|---|
| Graveyard | Rows of [[Sleeping Undead|sleeping undead]] in every pose and facing, with and without gear; a wool-walled pen for testing [[Noise and Waking|noise]]; a crypt packed with sleepers for chain wake-ups. |
| Loot on display | [[Loot on Display|Displayed items]] in every placement, and an unarmed sleeper next to an axe. |
| Combat arena | Chests of weapons (including Smite V swords) and armor, and sleepers to wake. See [[Fighting the Undead]]. |
| Crafting Tweaks | Every [[Crafting Tweaks Recipes|Crafting Tweaks recipe]]'s ingredients with a crafting table, blast furnace and smoker; [[Synthetic Diamond Ore]] next to natural ore. |
| Glider tower | A tall tower with Gliders, elytra and firework rockets at the top, for comparing [[The Glider]] with the elytra. |
| Gem workshop | For [[Bubba's Infused Gems]]: potions in all three forms, every base gem, a brewing stand, an enchanting table with a full set of bookshelves, a smithing table, a grindstone, armor and weapons, and a pen of mobs that stand still to be hit. |

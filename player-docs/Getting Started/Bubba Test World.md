# Bubba Test World

This tutorial describes the **Bubba Test World**, a ready-made creative world with stations for trying the projects' features, and how to get a fresh copy of it.

Some features have no station in this world: [[Drowned]], [[Ancient Fabricators]], [[Trader Posts]], Nether outposts and [[Bubba's Reworked Mining]]. All but Reworked Mining have natural examples in the **Bubba Natural World** (below).

## Getting the world

The world appears in the world list of the "mod testing" profile after an install. The installed copy belongs to the player: installing again never overwrites it. To get a fresh copy, install with the reset option, which writes over the existing copy; nothing from the old copy is kept.

## World settings

- Always night; clear weather.
- Nothing spawns on its own; each station spawns what it needs.
- Keep-inventory is on, so dying in the combat arena costs nothing.
- Signs at the spawn point say where each station is.

## Bubba Natural World

The **Bubba Natural World** is a second world from normal world generation, installed next to the test world. Signs on a platform at the spawn point teleport to a natural example of each of these:

- crypt yards;
- a camp of each kind, and a [[Monster Camps#Nether outposts|Nether outpost]] (that sign leads into the Nether);
- a pillager outpost and a [[Trader Posts|trader post]];
- a shipwreck with its sleeping crew;
- an [[Ancient Fabricators|Ancient Fabricator]];
- an enderman cairn;
- an old-growth taiga where giant spiders live, with one giant spider always placed there.

It is a creative world with cheats on. Unlike the test world, it plays like a normal world: it starts in the morning in clear weather, and time, weather and mob spawning all run as usual. Spider nests are not among the examples, since they cannot be located; the test world's Showcase has one.

## Gem testing grounds

The **gem testing grounds** are a set of stations for trying [[Gem Effects|gem effects]] in play, south of the spawn plaza. Most signs there are click signs: right-clicking one runs it.

### Wearing a gem set

1. Walk south from the spawn plaza into the wardrobe, the hall lined with signs.
2. Right-click the sign of a gem. The worn armor is taken off (and deleted), and a full netherite set with three perfect gems in every piece is put on; a sword with two of the same gems goes into the inventory. Gems that need a tool to test also give one: Haste a netherite pickaxe, shovel and axe, Mining Fatigue and Conduit Power a netherite pickaxe, Luck a fishing rod.
3. Every gem has two signs, one above the other: the oak sign gives the perfect set, the birch sign below it gives the same set at Base Power V (88% strength). Perfect sets have extras that weaker sets lack (for example, only a perfect Breeze set deflects every projectile), so trying both shows what the perfect set adds. Stations that test such an extra have both signs as well.
4. Signs on the right are helpful gems, signs on the left harmful ones. The lower row on the left gives Speed in every armor material, for comparing materials, and holds the Take off, Empty bag, No effects, Survival and Creative signs.

Each station below also has signs for the gems it tests. The armor stands past the wardrobe wear showcase sets; clicking a piece takes it.

## Boss arena

The **boss arena** is a walled arena south of the dark room and the food bench, entered through a gate in its north wall. Each boss is a tough mob in gem gear built around a combination of [[Gem Effects|gem effects]]. The signs on the outside of the north wall summon one boss at a time (summoning another removes the first); bosses only fight back against a player in survival.

| Boss | Mob | Gem combination |
|---|---|---|
| Webmaster | Zombie | Weaving armor and a Weaving sword: webs the player in place and walks through webs freely |
| Puppeteer | Vindicator | Axe with Levitation and Slow Falling: lifts the player and keeps them hanging |
| Blinker | Skeleton with a bow | Perfect Ender armor: teleports away from arrows unless boxed in |
| Juggernaut | Zombie | Turtle Master, Absorption and Regeneration armor |
| Gale | Piglin brute | Wind Burst mace: throws the player around |
| Plague | Wither skeleton | Sword with Poison and Hunger, on top of the wither skeleton's own Wither |
| Phantom | Zombie | Perfect Invisibility armor and a Darkness sword |
| Stalker | Husk | Silence and Invisibility armor, next to two sleeping skeletons it does not wake |

The Counter kits signs beside them give the player a set built against each boss:

| Kit | Against | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Web Walker | Webmaster | Weaving set and sword, shears |
| Crowd Ctrl | Puppeteer | Sword with Levitation and Slow Falling, bow with Levitation and Slowness, arrows |
| Deflector | Blinker | Perfect Breeze set, cobblestone to box the Blinker in |
| Siege | Juggernaut | Strength set, axe with Wither and Harming |
| Anchor | Gale | Wind Burst set (soft landings), Slow Falling sword |
| Attrition | Plague | Regeneration set, milk, golden apples |
| Lantern | Phantom | Night Vision set, Glowing sword |
| Hunter | Stalker | Silence set, sword with Glowing and Slowness |

A Clear arena sign removes the boss and everything it dropped.

## Beacons and warding

The **beacons and warding** stations are for trying [[Beacon|beacons]] from [[Bubba's Reworked Beacons]] and the [[Totem of Warding]]. They lie east and west of the boss arena, with one room high above it.

| Station | What to try |
|---|---|
| Beacon yard | Four beacons open to the sky on pyramids of 1, 2, 3 and 4 layers, each with a sign and a chest. A gem from the chest goes into the beacon: the pyramid caps its Base Power (I, III, V or VI) and sets the range (20 to 50 blocks). Each chest holds perfect and Base Power V gems, plus the old payment items, which are not accepted while [[Bubba's Infused Gems]] is installed. |
| Poison trap | A beacon west of the boss arena holding a Poison gem poisons everything around it, the player included. A pig and a cow in a pen show it; the zombie beside them is undead and immune. Signs mark the edge. |
| Paired beacons | A sign south of the mining wall teleports the player up to a closed room with a beacon in the floor and an upside-down beacon above it. A Totem of Warding from the chest goes into the floor beacon: the area 150 blocks around is warded at that height, and with the 4-layer pyramids the sleepers in the room stay asleep even when the bell rings. A sign on the wall leads back down. |
| Spawn test | Two dark boxes near the paired beacons room, one inside the ward and one above it. The Spawns on sign turns natural spawning on for the whole world; after a while on the ground, the Count spawns sign tells how many mobs are in each box. The Spawns off sign turns spawning off again, and Clear boxes empties them. |
| Bell | A bell beside three sleeping zombies 3, 9 and 15 blocks away. Ringing it wakes all three. A sign puts them back. |
| Light rings | A ring of sleeping skeletons lit by torches beside a ring in the dark. Sleepers in strong light are harder to wake than the same sleepers in the dark. A sign puts the dark ring back. |
| Totem of Warding | A sign in the wardrobe gives one. Held, it scares monsters away every 10 seconds. |

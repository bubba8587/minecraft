# Home

Welcome to the player guide for **Bubba's** Minecraft mods, datapacks and resource packs. It describes what each project adds, how to install it and what it works with. It is written for players, so it says nothing about how the projects are built.

All projects target the latest full release of Minecraft. [[Version Status]] names the current version and any project paused while it catches up.

## Projects

| Project | Type | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| [[Bubba's Extra Recipes]] | Datapack (optional Fabric mod) | Extra recipes for awkward or grindy items, plus the Glider and Synthetic Diamond Ore in the mod. |
| [[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]] | Fabric mod | Dormant undead, graveyards, monster camps, and reworked creepers, drowned, spiders and endermen. |
| [[Bubba's Infused Gems]] | Fabric mod | Potions brewed into gems, enchanted and socketed into armor and weapons for permanent effects. |
| [[Bubba's Reworked Beacons]] | Fabric mod | Beacons with one item slot instead of fixed powers, and paired beacons that work underground. |

Each project has a folder in `Projects/`: an overview page named after the project, plus a page per topic.

## What each mod adds

**[[Bubba's Reworked Mobs]]**
- Undead lie dormant as [[Sleeping Undead]] until [[Noise and Waking|noise wakes them]]; [[Woken Undead]] fight differently ([[Fighting the Undead]]), and [[Monster Spawning|natural spawns]] drop to 15 %.
- [[Crypt Yards]] with [[Gravestone|gravestones]] and crypts, [[Monster Camps]] with banner keys, [[Trader Posts]] where the wandering trader sets up, and [[Loot on Display]] for the undead to guard.
- Reworked [[Creepers]] that watch and startle, shore-lurking [[Drowned]] that drag players under, and lurking [[Bogged]].
- Cave-nesting [[Spiders]] with giants, webs and cocoons; caretaker [[Endermen]] that build cairns and [[Trading at Cairns|trade]] at them; [[Placing Items Anywhere|lay any item on the ground]] with sneak + middle click.
- A drowned [[Shipwreck Crew]], [[Will-o'-Wisps]] that lead to unfound graveyards, [[Explorer Maps]] from cartographers, and [[Noble Finery]] with Infused Gems.
- [[Ancient Fabricators]], mob machines beside every ancient city. Commands: [[Reworked Mobs Commands]].

**[[Bubba's Totems & Artifacts]]**
- The [[Grave Talisman]] glows near undead; the [[Totem of Warding]] keeps monsters away.
- [[Totem of Keeping|Keeping]], [[Totem of Gathering|Gathering]], [[Totem of Fortification|Fortification]], [[Totem of Growth|Growth]], [[Totem of Seeking|Seeking]], [[Totem of Herding|Herding]], [[Totem of Clear Skies|Clear Skies]] and [[Totem of the North Wind|the North Wind]]; most also work in a beacon.
- The [[Totem of the Beginning]] restores the land; the [[Totem of the End]] makes endermen kin and teleports.
- Any totem can be set down as a [[Totem Statue]], which gives a small version of its effect.
- [[Relic Maps|Relic map shreds]] from chests combine into maps that lead to buried [[Relic Vaults]].

**[[Bubba's Infused Gems]]**
- [[Making Infused Gems|Brew potions into gems]], [[Enchanting Gems|enchant]] them and [[Socketing Gems|socket]] them into gear.
- [[Gem Effects]] for armor and weapons; where to [[Finding Gems|find gems]]; [[Infused Gems Commands]].

**[[Bubba's Reworked Beacons]]**
- A [[Beacon]] holds one item that decides its effect; upside-down beacons pair up to work underground.

**[[Bubba's Reworked Mining]]**
- A steep [[Mining Curve]] from slow bare tools to an enchanted machine, [[Rare Finds]] in plain stone, the [[True Unbreaking]] book, and [[Quartz Tools and Armor]] for the Nether.

**[[Bubba's World Tweaks]]**
- [[Lava Bubbles and Geysers]]: lava bubbles over magma and erupts in geysers of black smoke; lava and sulfur geysers lift and speed up players above them, so circling one climbs.

**[[Bubba's Extra Recipes]]**
- A [[Recipe List]] for awkward or grindy items, as a datapack.
- The [[Extra Recipes Companion Mod]] adds [[The Glider]] and [[Synthetic Diamond Ore]].

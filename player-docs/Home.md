# Home

Player guides for the mods, datapacks and resource packs made in this repo. Everything here is written for players: what a project does, how to install and use it, and what it works with.

All projects target the latest full release of Minecraft. See [[Version Status]] for what's on the current version and anything that's paused while it catches up.

## Test world

**Bubba Test World** is a ready-made creative world with a station for every feature: a graveyard of sleeping undead (with a wool pen and a crypt), loot on display, a combat arena with gear chests, the Crafting Tweaks recipes with all their ingredients, and a tall tower for trying the Glider against the elytra. It's always night, nothing spawns on its own, and signs at the spawn point say where everything is.

It appears in the "mod testing" profile's world list after an install. Your copy is yours to wreck; installing again never overwrites it. To get a fresh one, install with the reset option (your old copy is kept under a new name).

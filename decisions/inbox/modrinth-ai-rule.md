## Decision

Pending the owner. Until answered: nothing is published to Modrinth, no AI-generated images are ever made for icons, galleries or textures, and A1 modrinthShipped and A2 agentBuildable are read as in tension, with A1's publishing gated on this answer.

## Why

Source: https://modrinth.com/legal/rules section 6 "Usage of Generative AI", 6.1 disclosure and 6.2 prohibited content. Breaking it risks rejection or removal of every project on the account, and only the owner can decide how much of each project is theirs.

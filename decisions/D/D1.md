## Decision

Dormant undead are real blocks, not entities or display-entity pseudo-blocks. They can be mined with Silk Touch, cost nothing to tick in large numbers, and never count against mob caps. Waking one replaces the block with a real mob.

## Why

The owner's call, given in chat: "I would want them to be mineable with Silk Touch and I would want having a ton of them spawned in a chunk to not be laggy / count against mob count limits. that's why I think the static undead should be a real block (or similar)." This is a designed exception to the rule of adding as few new blocks as possible: the owner gave the reason.

Removed by: Minecraft offering a non-block way to get all three (Silk-Touch-collectable, no tick cost in bulk, outside mob caps), or data-driven custom blocks arriving.

## Decision

Bubba's Reworked Mobs nearly eliminates vanilla's random mob spawning. Monsters come from deliberate, readable placement: sleeping undead, structures and camps, and tightly limited mod-controlled spawns (e.g. one watching creeper per player).

## Why

Owner (2026-11-16): 'for all mobs, we want to try to almost eliminate random spawning'; 'that's an important aspect, yes, that's leaf worthy. it's the reasoning behind why I wanted to do Sleepers in the first place.'

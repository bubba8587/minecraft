## Decision

Every mod and pack targets the latest non-snapshot Minecraft release. When a new release comes out, the repo moves to it promptly, even if that means temporarily disabling features, mods or dependencies that aren't ready for it yet.

## Why

The owner's call, given in chat: "I don't like waiting and I'd always like as much of my tweaks as possible on the newest part of the game."

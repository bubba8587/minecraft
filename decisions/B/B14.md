## Decision

A project goes on Modrinth only when it meets Modrinth's generative-AI rules: the "Contains AI-generated content" disclosure is always set, no image on the project page, icon or gallery is AI-generated, and the project is not primarily AI output because a person (the owner or a collaborator) made a substantial part of it, such as its design, textures, models or code. A project that does not meet that bar is released as a GitHub Release instead, and moves to Modrinth once it does.

## Why

Asked about this and two other open questions, the owner answered "you can do whatever you want idk" (2026-11-14), handing the call to the agent. Modrinth's content rules (https://modrinth.com/legal/rules section 6, checked 2026-11-14) ban projects that are primarily AI output and AI images on project pages; breaking them risks every project on the account. Following the rule strictly costs some reach on agent-heavy projects; breaking it could cost the account.

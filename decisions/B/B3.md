## Decision

New mods are built for the newest stable Minecraft release (26.3 as of 2026-11) and move forward with each release. Older versions such as 1.21.x get a build only when the owner asks for one.

## Why

Players on Modrinth skew toward current versions, and 26.x is unobfuscated so the newest release is also the easiest to mod correctly. Guessing at the owner's preference.

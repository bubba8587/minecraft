## Decision

Our mods aim to work cleanly alongside a list of other mods the owner plays with or that most players run (performance, rendering, terrain, structure, map and UI mods). The list lives in specs/compat.md and changes freely (the owner: 'known fluid'); what it says is tested and fixed before a release.

## Why

The owner, 2026-11-23: 'i wanna be compatible with some other mods that i've found and normally have on' (their porp Modrinth profile: Sodium, Iris, Lithium, Voxy, Tectonic, Towns & Towers, Explorify, Cristel Lib, Mod Menu, Xaero's maps and others), then 'yes, compat list, known fluid tho', and 'easy wins on other highly downloaded mods'.

## Consequences

- specs/compat.md holds the list, what each mod means for us and its status.
- Easy compatibility wins come first; deeper fixes follow testing with the mods loaded.
- A release checks the listed mods that touch the released project.

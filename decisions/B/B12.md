## Decision

Mods and packs in this repo are released under the MIT license.

## Why

Asked about this and two other open questions, the owner answered "you can do whatever you want idk" (2026-11-14), handing the call to the agent. MIT fits B9 libsOverHandroll (we lean on other people's open code, so ours should be just as reusable), has no source-publication trap in Modrinth moderation the way copyleft licenses do, and is the most common license among Fabric mods. Assets made by people may carry their own license, noted per project.

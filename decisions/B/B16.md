## Decision

Every custom texture ships in two resolutions: 16x in vanilla style, and 32x matching the style of the Classic Faithful 32x Jappa resource pack (the pack the owner plays with).

## Why

The owner's call, given in chat: "we will support, also, 16x and 32x textures, matching vanilla style &/ Faithful 32x texturepack." Changed by the owner on 2026-11-23 to Classic Faithful 32x Jappa: it is the 32x pack that is actively updated and the one the owner uses.

## Consequences

- A project with custom textures is not finished until both sets exist and look at home next to vanilla and next to Classic Faithful 32x Jappa respectively. 32x art made in the earlier Faithful 32x style is restyled over time.
- Classic Faithful's (and Faithful's) work may be referenced or modified only under their licenses, with clear credit and a visible link in the project's credits and listing; the exact terms are rechecked before each release that uses derived art.
- Textures are images: none may be AI-generated where Modrinth's rules forbid it, and textures are the most natural human-made part that carries a project over the bar for a Modrinth release.

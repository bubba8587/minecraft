## Decision

Every custom texture ships in two resolutions: 16x in vanilla style, and 32x matching the style of the Faithful 32x resource pack.

## Why

The owner's call, given in chat: "we will support, also, 16x and 32x textures, matching vanilla style &/ Faithful 32x texturepack."

## Consequences

- A project with custom textures is not finished until both sets exist and look at home next to vanilla and next to Faithful 32x respectively.
- Faithful's work may be referenced or modified under the Faithful License (v4, 2026-07-31, https://faithfulpack.net/license) only with clear credit and a visible link to https://faithfulpack.net in the project's credits and listing; the exact terms are rechecked before each release that uses Faithful-derived art.
- Textures are images: none may be AI-generated where Modrinth's rules forbid it, and textures are the most natural human-made part that carries a project over the bar for a Modrinth release.

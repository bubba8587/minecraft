## Decision

A release is published by pushing a git tag of the form <project>/v<version>; CI builds, tests and uploads it to Modrinth. Nothing is uploaded by hand.

## Why

Releases from CI are repeatable and an agent can do them without the owner's machine. Guessing at the owner's preference.

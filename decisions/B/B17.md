## Decision

Mods build on vanilla's own mechanics, decision points and visuals (e.g. the item cooldown sweep, vanilla heart types, chorus fruit's teleport, beacon areas) and only hand-roll what vanilla can't do.

## Why

Owner's repeated instruction (2026-11-15/16): 'use vanilla mechanics where possible', 'reusing vanilla mechanics and ideas where possible'.

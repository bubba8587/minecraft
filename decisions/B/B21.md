## Decision

The whole map stays dangerous, about as dangerous as vanilla. A player makes an area permanently safer by clearing its monster camps (breaking their banners) or by placing Totems of Warding (held, statue or beacon). That is the goal the danger serves. Threats that reach out from camps (such as patrols) come from the armed camps near the player, so clearing camps quiets the land around them; warded areas get no such spawns.

## Why

Owner, 2026-11-25: the underlying thesis behind monster camps, camp patrols and Warding totems. Danger should be something the player pushes back, area by area, not something that is simply on or off.

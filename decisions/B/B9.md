## Decision

Mods here use existing outside libraries and mods aggressively. Code is hand-rolled only where it is needed for a well-designed, performant mod: no library does it, the available one doesn't support the target version, or it would cost real performance or design quality.

## Why

The owner's call, given in chat: "aggressive use of existing outside libs & mods, handrolling only what's necessary to achieve a well-designed, performant mod." Meant more as a standing reminder than a hard rule.

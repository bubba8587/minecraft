## Decision

A new mod, Bubba's Reworked Mining: bare tools are much slower and wear faster (wood barely works; deepslate very hard below iron), while Efficiency and Unbreaking get stronger, so enchantments and gems carry a real sense of progression. Plain stone and deepslate rarely drop nuggets and gems. True Unbreaking is a rare loot-only book.

## Why

Owner, 2026-11-22: 'really just establishing a curve here and making enchantments (& gems) very worthwhile, such that you can go from tough to highly powered machine and get a real sense of progression'; slow the early game a bit and the midgame a lot via deepslate.

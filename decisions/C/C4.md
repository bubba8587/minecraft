## Decision

A mod adds effect gems: a class of items that each embody a permanent potion effect, which a player adds to and removes from armor. They are very strong, so they are hard to get, never give full potion strength, and carry drawbacks.

## Why

The owner's idea, given in chat on 2026-11-14: 'effect gems. these gems are a class of items that embody permanent potion effects and can be added to/removed from armor. very strong, so need to be hard to get... need to figure out drawbacks, and they can't have full potion strength with infinite duration.'

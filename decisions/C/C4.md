## Decision

A mod adds effect gems: a class of items that each embody a permanent effect (potion effects and a few beyond potions), set into armor, weapons and (with Bubba's Reworked Beacons) beacons, and removed again. They are very strong, so they are hard to get; most builds give a partial effect, and only a perfect, heavily ground-out build reaches or (for some movement effects) passes the strongest potion. Rolled drawbacks exist.

Updated 2026-11-16 (owner: 'retitle/edit what you need'): the original 'never full potion strength, on armor' no longer holds after the owner's later decisions (weapons, beacons, per-effect perfect caps); details in specs/effect-gems.md.

## Why

The owner's idea, given in chat on 2026-11-14: 'effect gems. these gems are a class of items that embody permanent potion effects and can be added to/removed from armor. very strong, so need to be hard to get... need to figure out drawbacks, and they can't have full potion strength with infinite duration.'

## Decision

Bubba's Extra Recipes (named Bubba's Crafting Tweaks until 2026-11-22) is the core set of extra recipes, shipped as a datapack. Other mods here may add recipes of their own, but this pack is the shared baseline.

## Why

The owner's call, given in chat with the pack attached: "here's Bubba's Crafting Tweaks... should hold as the recipe set i want for datapack-level recipes (other mods may add their own, but we'll have this core set, basically)."

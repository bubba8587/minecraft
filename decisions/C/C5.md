## Decision

Rework vanilla beacons as their own mod: one item slot replaces powers and payment, pyramid size caps the item's power, sky beacons keep vanilla's column, upside-down paired beacons ward wide underground boxes; Infused Gems and Reworked Mobs plug in through an optional hook, all three stay independent.

## Why

Owner (2026-11-16): one coherent area system for gems and Warding, mob-free zones without torch spam, separated into its own mod.

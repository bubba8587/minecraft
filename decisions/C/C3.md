## Decision

Bubba's Extra Recipes (formerly Crafting Tweaks) gets a companion Fabric mod for what the recipe datapack can't do alone: the Glider (a craftable, weaker cousin of the elytra with its own look and worse handling) and the Synthetic Diamond Ore's own drops, with more such items over time. The mod bundles the datapack so mod users install one thing; the datapack keeps working without it.

## Why

The owner's call, given in chat: "Can call it Glider. And yes i'm fine with it moving into its own small mod, that small mod will likely pick up a variety of similar items." Then: "put it in the gear mod for now. let's have that just be a crafting-tweaks companion."

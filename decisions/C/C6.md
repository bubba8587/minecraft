## Decision

A separate mod holds artifact items: the Grave Talisman and all Totems (Warding, Keeping, Gathering, Fortification, Growth, Seeking). They move out of Reworked Mobs; integration with Reworked Mobs (sleepers) and Reworked Beacons is optional through small hooks, so every mod stays independent.

## Why

Owner (2026-11-16): 'this could be its own mod, Bubba's Totems & Artifacts. Grave Talisman and all totems go in here.'

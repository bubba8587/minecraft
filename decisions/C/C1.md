## Decision

Bubba's Reworked Mobs: one mod that reworks every mob to some degree, built in parts. Part 1 reworks the undead (creepers and monster camps come next):

- Natural random spawning keeps vanilla's light-based rules but becomes drastically rarer, kept for occasional ambience and jump scares.
- Skeletons and zombies are placed statically at world generation, dormant: skeletons sitting with a weapon in hand, nearby, or none; zombies lying in shallow graves or sitting.
- Noise, through the warden's vibration system, wakes them. A waking undead can wake nearby dormant ones. A quiet player can steal their weapons, nearby treasure chests, and armor off their bodies; taking armor off a body always has a chance to wake it.
- Woken zombies pick up their weapon and chase the player, and zombies slowly drift to group with each other.
- Zombies and skeletons are much slower, with staggering animations, and have much higher health. Smite has a chance to kill them instantly with particle effects; bows get an equivalent.
- Undead hit weakly with bare fists but deal high durability damage to armor.
- Rooms and dungeons hold many dormant undead.

## Why

The owner's design, given in chat on 2026-11-14 as the first mod idea for the repo, then named and widened: "this mod will touch all the mobs to some degree, this is just the first part... Bubba's Reworked Mobs."

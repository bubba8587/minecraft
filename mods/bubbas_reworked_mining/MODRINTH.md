A rework of mining and tool progression, plus rare finds in plain stone, a new enchantment and a quartz tool and armor set.

## Tool progression

- Overhauled tool progression: Fists, Wood and Stone Pickaxes are slower and weaker. Deepslate is significantly slower to mine and does higher durability damage to everything weaker than a diamond pickaxe. However, the Efficiency enchantment is stronger and much more valuable.
- Wood can't mine deepslate at all; copper and iron are about normal on stone but wear down fast on deepslate; diamond mines deepslate as in vanilla, and netherite is faster than vanilla.
- Axes, shovels and hoes follow a gentler version of the same curve.
- Efficiency multiplies a tool's own speed, so an Efficiency V iron pickaxe out-mines a bare diamond one on deepslate. Unbreaking is stronger too.

## Rare finds

- Mining stone and deepslate has a small chance of dropping iron & gold nuggets, and a tiny chance of dropping more valuable gems.
- This covers stone, granite, diorite, andesite, tuff and deepslate. Deepslate can very rarely drop a diamond.

## True Unbreaking

- The rare True Unbreaking enchanted book can completely prevent your item from ever taking durability damage again.
- It's only found in rare chests.

## Quartz gear

- Quartz tools and armor are similar to copper and iron, with extra efficiency in the Nether.

## Installing

- Needs [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api).
- Install it on the server and on every player's game.
- 32x textures (Classic Faithful 32x Jappa style) come in the separate **Bubba's Mods 32x** resource pack. Credits are in the mod's CREDITS.md.

## Works well with

- **Infused Gems**: Haste gems speed mining up on top of everything here.
- **Reworked Mobs**: noble crypts are the best place to find True Unbreaking.

Tools from other mods keep their vanilla behavior.

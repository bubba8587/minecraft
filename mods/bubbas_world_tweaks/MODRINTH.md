Small touches to the world itself. The first one makes the Nether's lava seas come alive.

## Lava bubbles and geysers

- Magma blocks below Lava create bubbles and powerful black smoke geysers. These work just like Sulfur Geysers.
- Wherever a magma block lies under lava (up to four blocks down), the surface above it slowly bubbles and plops.
- Every magma block under lava is a vent with its own steady rhythm: quiet for between about 80 seconds and three minutes, then a three-second eruption of black smoke and sparks with a low rumble. Watch a vent once and you can time the next one.
- Standing, falling or gliding over an erupting lava geyser lifts you, the same way sulfur geysers do, and both kinds give a little speed boost. Circle a geyser to climb.
- No new world generation: the Nether already has magma all along its lava seas, so existing worlds bubble right away.

## Installing

- Needs [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api).
- Install it on the server and on every player's game.
- 32x textures (Classic Faithful 32x Jappa style) come in the separate **Bubba's Mods 32x** resource pack.

## Works well with

- **Bubba's Extra Recipes**: its Glider can ride geyser updrafts for Nether travel. Not required.

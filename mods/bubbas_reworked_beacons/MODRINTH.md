A rework of the beacon: one item slot instead of fixed powers and payments, effects for everything in range, and beacons that pair up to work underground. No new blocks or items.

## Beacons

- Reworked Beacons: the default behavior switches from consumed ingots to an un-consumed set of gems (Amethyst, Quartz, Ender Pearl, Diamond, Prismarine Shard and Nether Star, one for each of vanilla's six powers).
- The item stays in the beacon and can be taken back out. The pyramid limits how strong an item the beacon can use.
- Beacon effects now apply equally to all entities in range: players, animals and monsters alike.

## Paired Beacons

- Paired Beacons: a beacon can be paired with another upside down beacon for a more horizontal area of effect which works without reaching the sky.
- A beacon placed under a block hangs upside down. Put one above a normal beacon to make a pair.

## Works well with

- Highly recommended to use this mod with Infused Gems and/or Totems & Artifacts. With Infused Gems installed, both vanilla ingots and this mod's default gems are replaced by Infused Gems which broadcast their specific effect. Totems, from Totems and Artifacts, also broadcast their effects (Totem of Undying not included).
- Neither of those mods is required.

## Installing

- Needs [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api).
- Install it on the server and on every player's game.
- In existing worlds, beacons keep working as sky beacons but lose their old powers until an item is put in.
- Other mods that change the beacon screen or beacon powers are likely to clash with this one.
